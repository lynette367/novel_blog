"Healer Jiang sent a message that the Great King is in a physical condition, and asked the two lords to go quickly."

Zheng Yuming stood up all of a sudden after hearing this, Wu is at war with Zhao at the mouth of the throat, and it would not be a good thing for Wu if the Western Marquis died at such a time.

"It won't be so fast!"
