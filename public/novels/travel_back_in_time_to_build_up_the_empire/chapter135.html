"Mr. Prime Minister, is it possible to make the country prosperous with just one word?" Yang He asked, standing up from his seat.

This is one of the academies of the North Academy, a place where famous scholars and scholars exchange ideas, and all scholars of the North Academy can participate in it as long as they are interested in it. Nowadays, North College has a weekly system, seven days for a round, one to six for study days, the seventh day for students to rest. And from Monday to Saturday every night, there are famous scholar to and students to exchange ideas, as to which one will come, only in the afternoon of the day will be posted on the announcement. Zheng Yuming and Chu Chaohui would come two to three times a month.

"According to reason, it is impossible to rely on a word to make the country prosper." Zheng Yuming kneeling in the center of the school, surrounded by all the students sitting on the ground, the four corners of the school, standing Zheng Yuming's personal guards, while the big cow is kneeling closely behind Zheng Yuming to protect.

The classrooms of the North Academy had already used modern desks and chairs, and the instructors used blackboards and chalk to teach classes. But in the famous scholar with the students face-to-face academic exchanges in the academy, or to take the Great Qin etiquette, did not take the latter-day kind of speech.

Zheng Yuming moved his legs which were already a bit numb, he was really not used to this kneeling position. "However, governing the country relies on the king alone to decide, if the king is not right in what he is thinking or does not listen to the correct opinions of his ministers, then it is very possible that one word will revitalize the country and one word will kill the country."

"Then is there a way to deal with it?" Wei Zhong stood up and bowed for advice.

"This requires a kind of constraint, not because of one person's preference to set the whole situation, for example, our great king, never unauthorized to determine the big event, Wu court meeting, never is the king's court court ministers most agree with the resolution will be passed, in this way, can maximize the avoidance of wrong decision making." Zheng Yuming said this, the face is not red, today's royal court, Wu Wangsheng is a pose, the royal court in the vast majority of ministers to Zheng Yuming and Chu Chaohui two people look forward to, Zheng Yuming out of the decree, of course, is in the absolute majority of consent to pass.

"But where is the authority of a king who does this?" Fu Chou suddenly stood up, his face red.

Fu Chou this sentence voice sharp, with a kind of questioning, the students in the hall are surprised to look at him, kneeling next to Fu Chou Yang He reached out and pulled Fu Chou's corner, feel Fu Chou asked too rude.

"The authority of the king is in the hearts of the people." Zheng Yuming still smiled, his face unchanged as he pointed to his heart. "The king does not rule the country by his own preferences, accommodates opposition, suppresses his supreme power, chooses policies of governance that are favorable to the country , so that the people can live in peace and contentment, and the country is rich and strong, how can the people not respect and love such a king?"

Speaking here, Zheng Yuming got up and his words became impassioned, "A monarch who only cares about his own majesty, so that no one dares to disobey his own words, over time, the country will be close to extinction, from ancient times to the present day, is not Xia Jie, Shang Zhou, and the Weekend King such a monarch? Before their demise, they precisely wanted to kill all the opposing voices in the world, but the result was counterproductive, burying their own country."

As soon as Zheng Yuming's words fell, the scholars let out a ''buzzing'' sound, nodding their heads in agreement abounded.

"At the beginning of every dynasty, the founding kings and lords were always wise and had the capacity to accommodate mountains and rivers, but after a few generations of kings, there were fewer such wise kings, and such a situation arose only because of the varying talents of the kings who succeeded to the throne, and as long as there were such dim-witted kings as Xia Jie, Shang Zhou, and the Weekend King, the people of the world were in dire straits, and the people of the world would rise up and revolt. The people of the world will be in dire straits, and those who are knowledgeable will rise up in revolt. If we don't want such a situation to arise, we have to avoid the emergence of fainting kings, or even if there is such a headstrong character of the king, the country can still be safe and sound, which requires a system that restrains the emergence of such a situation, that is, the implementation of the voting system of the court."

"Lord Prime Minister, who can guarantee that the minister's opinion is the correct one?" Fu Chou's face turned redder and redder, and his attitude became more and more seemingly unforgiving.

"Yes, even if the king and ministers overwhelmingly agree with an opinion, there is no guarantee that the resolution adopted is completely correct, which requires the supervision of the people, affecting the interests of the people, the people are bound to make a great deal of opposition voices, and at this point, the king and his ministers need to listen to the views of all sides. Being a king and a minister is in fact very difficult to govern a country, and it is precisely because it is difficult that it is all the more necessary to strive for excellence, to listen to a wide range of opinions, and to understand the situation of the country and the people's livelihood. And all of you, you will be the pillars of the country in the future, you must have a full stomach of experience, so that when you participate in the imperial government in the future, you will be able to make the least mistakes in the state policy."

Zheng Yuming's high hopes for the students, so that the students' hearts are boiling, when Zheng Yuming's speech is over, a burst of applause in the school hall, this kind of applause can only be seen in the north and south academies of the Kingdom of Wu, the applause represents respect, on behalf of the affirmation and encouragement.
