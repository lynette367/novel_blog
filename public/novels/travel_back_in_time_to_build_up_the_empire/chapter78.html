Wei Qing froze, his eyes looked at Chu Chao Hui to seek an answer, Zhou Cheng was full of puzzlement, not understanding how Chu Chao Hui, who had just incited the officers to completely eliminate the Tiger Tribe at the military meeting, now explicitly asked to let the Tiger Tribe have a way out.

Chu Chao Hui finished speaking and propped both hands on the conference table. "Think about why you should let the Tiger Tribe live."

Wei Qing raised his head fiercely, "My lord, Wei Qing understands, not wanting the Tiger Tribe to disappear is to allow the Eagle Tribe to have an enemy presence, so that the Eagle Tribe will have to rely on us all the time."
