The great river was similar to the Yellow River of the twenty-first century. The population density in this time and space was not too high, the vegetation everywhere was not too badly damaged, the trees on both sides of the great river were lush and the river water was very clear.

Staring at the clear river, Zheng Yuming secretly resolved that he must admonish his descendants to pay attention to maintaining the water and soil, and not to make the Yellow River appear again in this time and space.

Zheng Yuming is thinking about soil and water problems, but Zhao Xuan is ambitious about the rushing river. Along the way, the great fertile land because of the vassals do not care about the management and waste, Zhao Xuan to replace the ambition of more and more intense.

Early the next morning, all the members of the mission had supper, put on their wide dresses, raised the ceremonial banners, and marched in a neat procession to Haojing.

Haojing got the news as early as when the Zhao and Wu envoys crossed the river. The ceremonial officers in charge of welcoming the lords were waiting at the city gates early.

Despite the decline of the Qin Emperor, Haojing, as the capital of the Emperor for hundreds of years, had city building specifications that were unrivaled by any of the vassal kings. The city wall was quite tall and lofty, and the moat was very wide and deep, with clear water, and many women washed their clothes beside the moat.

The specification of the city is the Tien Zi system, the city tower is nine-story high, the city gate is made of huge wood that is hundreds of years old, and the exterior is wrapped with brass, which glows with golden light under the sunlight.

Led by the ceremonial officers, Zhao Xuan and Zheng Yuming entered the city gates with their envoys, and only two hundred of the escorts of the two countries followed them into the city, while the rest of the men and horses were stationed outside Haojing.

The envoys of Zhao and Wu arrived at Haojing on the 10th day of the 5th lunar month. Before them, 40 to 50 vassal states had already come, all of which were small and medium-sized states, and Zhao and Wu were the first two vassal powers to arrive.

Regardless of whether it is a powerful or a small country, the monarchs and feudal lords are generally dressed in black, and at a glance, they are in black. This was not the case two hundred years ago, when no vassal monarch dared to wear black openly. Nowadays, as long as one has the status of a royal family, one can use black, and the honorary color can be used at will, so it can be seen that the majesty of the Qin Emperor no longer exists.

"Yudie, today's hour is still early, how about you and I tour Haojing together?"

Zheng Yuming opened his mouth wide and compared the yard with his hand. "This is the debt?"

In the twenty-first century, there is the allusion to a debtor's platform, which says that King Blushing of Zhou owed a lot of debt and was unable to repay it, and was forced by his debtors to hide on a platform. Zheng Yuming did not expect the Great Qin Son of Heaven to similarly interpret this story in front of him.

How many coins are owed in total?"

"It's only natural for my subjects to offer Heavenly Prince, and with Heavenly Prince in such dire straits, how can my subjects be at ease!" Zheng Yuming's pilgrimage was originally to buy fame, and the fact that Emperor Qin owed money in front of him was just the right opportunity.

Zhao Xuan stared at Zheng Yuming for a long time, recalling Wu's unconditional assistance last year, and realized that Zheng Yuming was truly a loose cannon.

The debtors near the courtyard gate were overjoyed to hear that someone was helping Prince Qin to pay off his debts. Zheng Yuming did not have 300,000 gold cash money with him, so he could only ask Wei Cargo to look for merchants in Haojing who had business dealings with Wu, and borrowed all the coins from them to pay off Qin Tianzi's debts one by one.

The day after paying off the debt for the Qin Emperor, the Qin Emperor sent the Yellow Gate to summon Zheng Yuming.
