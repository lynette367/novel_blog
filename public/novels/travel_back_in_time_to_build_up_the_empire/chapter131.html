"Thanks a lot!" Yang He received the charcoal pencil and thanked him repeatedly. "My name is Yang He, may I ask what is your name, kind brother?"

"My surname is Fu Chou." The clean-cut candidate spoke his name in a low voice only after a long time.

When Yang He learned from the merchant that as long as he signed an agreement with the North College of Wu, he could further his studies without any worries, he was immediately moved, and borrowed a sum of money from his clansmen and rushed to the capital city of Wu. Yang He finished the enrollment formalities, received the dormitory number plate for his future stay, and picked up his big bag to look for the dormitory. The student dormitory in the North Academy was two to a room, and when Yang He pushed open the door of the dormitory, he found that his roommate was actually Fu Chou, who had lent him a charcoal pencil.
