The king of Chu liked hundred of plays, and the most favorite among the hundred of plays was jiaolai play, so the hundred officials of Chu threw themselves into the king's favor and tried their best to find wonderful jiaolai plays to please the king of Chu. On this day, a merchant of Wu Kingdom offered a novel Jiao Dai Opera for the King of Chu to enjoy through Zuo Da Fu. The king of Chu favored Zuo Daifu and accepted his recommendation, so he held a banquet in the palace and gathered all the ministers of the king's court and his concubines' young children to watch it.

Jiaojia opera is derived from primitive people fighting with wild beasts. Generally speaking, there are two artists wearing masks to decorate warriors and beasts, and they compete with each other, deciding the winner by the strength of their power.

The performance method of the Jiao Dai Opera presented by Wu merchants is very different from that of the past, in that the performers do not compete with each other, but rather focus on the content of the performance. The performance process is also different from the past as only a simple accompaniment of gongs and drums, the whole process of mixing a variety of music, rare is that the role in the performance process also said lines, so that the viewer understands what is happening in the theater.

The show opened with a warrior without make-up, only full body armor, wearing a sword, claiming to be Wu Sung, traveled around the vassal states for six years to return home, and in the evening traveled to the vicinity of a hillock, hungry, want to find a village to buy some food.

After selling food to Wu Sung, the villagers kindly advised Wu Sung not to go forward, informing him that there was a vicious tiger in front of him, which had already injured countless people, and the people could not defeat it by encircling and fighting it, so they had to walk around the Gang. The villagers advised Wu Song to stay at their house. Wu Sung suspected that the village people want to get more money from him, immediately ignored, ate and went on the road by himself.

Walked to the place called Jing Yang Gang, Wu Sung sat down for a nap, drink from the village people that exchange of green wine, do not want to this green wine flavor is very good, Wu Sung will be three gourds of green wine all drink all, drink all the wine after the onset of force, looking for a piece of smooth green stone, lie down on your back ready to sleep. At this time, the dubbing of the urgent pipes and strings, the sound of music and tension, the rain is coming, a big tiger with white eyes in the wind whistling pounced over.

Eyes white tiger appeared, the king of Chu monarchs and ministers were startled, around the service of the concubine and the maid is even more scared of the blossom of color, because the eyes white tiger makeup is too much like, as if the real tiger in front of the face.

People and tigers meet, the sound of music is thrilling, and everyone is scared and worried about the warrior Wu Sung. Drunken Wu Sung lost his weapon and fought with the white tiger with his empty hands and fists, and the process of the fight was thrilling and thrilling, hitting people's heartstrings, and Wu Sung finally won the battle with his bravery.

At the end of the performance, the king of Chu was the first to clap the table and applaud, followed by the ministers, and the performers were rewarded with very generous gifts.

"Love, I wonder where Wu Song is? I would like to summon the warrior." The King of Chu sidled up to his favorite minister, Zuo Daifu, and inquired.

Wusong in the offset theater is set as a Chu man, and the wine that Wusong drinks is the famous Chu wine, green wine, which enhances the authenticity of the performance, plus the lines are so well written that the King of Chu thinks that Wusong fights the tiger is really true.

The left doctor froze, he did not expect the king of Chu will take the hundred against the play seriously, a moment to think, rise to flatter: "The king of the arrows shot a tiger, valor is stronger than Wu Sung, the artist is just based on this performance. Because of the king's bravery, the country's Huben brave people abound, Wu Song is our country's Huben!"

King of Chu when he was young hunting encountered tiger, tiger by the King of Chu and the guards of the arrow shot to death, the tiger in the body of a hundred arrows, like a hedgehog, the King of Chu with the same arrows and the guards, he shot the arrow has not set up in no one knows. But at that time the people present all cheered is the king of Chu will tiger shot dead.

"Haha, Ai Qing is really not humble!" The King of Chu was very happy as he held his goatee beard.

"The humble servant is only telling the truth. It won't be like the self aggrandizement of Zhao's subjects, boasting about their Crown Prince Zhao Xuan's bravery like a heavenly being." Zuo Daifu's words changed and pulled to Zhao Xuan.

As soon as the King of Chu heard the word Zhao Xuan, his face showed his displeasure: "But it's just a brat who doesn't know the heights of heaven."

"The Great King should teach a lesson to the boy who doesn't know the sky and the earth." Zuo Daifu advanced.

The King of Chu meditated for a moment and shook his head, "It's not worth it to make a fuss over a cocky kid."

The king of Chu was eager to teach Zhao a lesson, but as the king of a country, he understood that the whole country must be poured into Zhao in order to win.

"To teach Zhao Xuan a lesson, why do you need the King to do it himself!" The right doctor sitting at the banquet table suddenly rose to admonish, the right doctor is different from the left doctor who was favored by the king of Chu and climbed up to the position of the doctor, the right doctor comes from a big family, is the representative of the big family of Chu in the royal court. The right doctor doesn't open his mouth easily, if he remonstrates, the king of Chu will generally pay attention to it.

"Oh, aiqing have good plan?" Although the king of Chu is becoming more and more mediocre, in his heart, he is still clear that the right doctor is more capable than the left doctor.

"The Duke of Wei was framed by Zhao Xuan for the destruction of his country and his family, and he wants to eat his flesh. If the king lends him his troops and horses, Duke Shih will go all out to seek revenge on Zhao, and Zhao Xuan will be taught a lesson."

The feud between Zhao and Chu was actually a dispute over the interests of the nobles in power between Zhao and Chu. As the representative of a major family in Chu, the Right Honorable Doctor, of course, did not have a favorable opinion of Zhao Xuan. When the Wu merchants persuaded the Right Honorable Doctor to go to the king of Chu to help Gongzi Xie, the Right Honorable Doctor immediately agreed.

"There have been many natural disasters in the past few years, and I don't want to impose more taxes."

The king of Chu frowned, lending troops to Gongzi Xie to conquer Zhao was tantamount to Chu providing troops and financial resources for Gongzi Xie, in which case the price paid by Chu was too great for the king of Chu to be happy.

The king of Chu's eyes lit up, and he deeply felt that this was a good idea, Chu didn't spend any money, and could still earn rent while making Zhao Xuan suffer, it was really a good plan.
