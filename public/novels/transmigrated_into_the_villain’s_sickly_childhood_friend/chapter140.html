Because of Chen Canaan's disappearance, Qiao Wen had to express his worries to the outside world, and Lin Zirui invited him to dinner several times as a result.

"Wen, there's always a silver lining, Nan is a big man, and his hands are so good, I believe he'll be back soon." On this day, the two of them had dinner and said goodbye, Lin Zi Rui saw his sad face, reached out and patted his shoulder, softly soothing, "To say the least, it's still my uncle's fault, going to night fishing in the middle of the night, hastily getting on an illegal boat not to mention, but also leaving Nan behind."

Qiao Wen said, "It's been a little more than half a month and he hasn't come back yet. I'm worried that he's been gotten by those people to Southeast Asia and has gotten into some kind of trouble."

Lin Zi Rui said, "I've already asked someone to help me ask around, there should be news soon. I see that the weather is good these days, and it just so happens that I've bought a new yacht, so why don't we go out to the sea tomorrow and have a BBQ in the sun, and you can relax a bit too."

Jovian hesitated for a moment and nodded, "That's fine, he's a big man, I can't worry about him all the time."

Lin Zi Rui smiled, "Yes, you were never in the same world, so maybe it's good for each other to go your separate ways."

Jovian just smiled and didn't say anything.

After waiting for so long, finally waited for this day, to say not excited is false. From coming to this world, to now has been six years, their destiny, although not to say is not manipulated by the five masters, but always away from the hand of the clouds and rain. In fact, by five master manipulation is not only him and Chen Canaan, from the stock market property market to smuggling drug trafficking and money laundering, bottomless pit general desire, so that many people in the city camp service life, are affected by them.

The next morning, Qiao Wen was about to leave the house when he waited for Zhou Renjun's call.

"Man, you guessed it right, I just received a notification from Boss Lee's side that the shipment that Kevin Chung was in charge of last time is indeed coming into the port today, and when the cargo ship enters the port, Mr. J will personally use the radio to notify him that he is ready to receive the shipment. So there is radio equipment on that yacht he went out to sea on, as long as he sends and receives messages by radio on the ship, we can use technology to locate the source of transmission, but we must seize his radio equipment as evidence to prove his identity, so I will look for an opportunity to get on board his ship when the time comes."

Jovian nodded knowingly, "Wait until I'm sure he's sent a message and signaled you before you come aboard, lest you arouse his suspicions and interrupt the plan."

"Okay, then I'll be around the cruise ship when I get there, waiting for your signal."

He hung up the phone thoughtfully, and Chen Canaan came over, "Are we really going to arrest Lin Zirui today?"

"Uh-huh."

Chen Canaan said worriedly, "He won't jump over the wall in a hurry and you'll be in danger then, right? I think you can simply find an excuse not to go, anyway, he's doing something today, so just let the police catch him."

Qiao Wen shook his head, "The fact that his identity has never been exposed for so many years means that he is very cautious, originally today's outing was in my name, if I suddenly don't go, he will most likely cancel his plans."

Chen Canaan said, "But with the goods coming in, he can't just ignore it, can he?"

Qiao Wen laughed: "Do you think he really but the money for this shipment? Let the police surrender it, he won't care, as long as his identity isn't exposed. All he enjoys is strategizing and watching from the sidelines."

"What a pervert." Chen Canaan gritted his teeth and added after some thought, "But I'm still not at ease."

Qiao Wen smiled and patted his shoulder, "Alright, now he's in the light and we're in the dark, with Zhou Sir's arrangements, it'll be fine."

Chen Canaan grunted reluctantly and hugged him fiercely and nibbled on him two more times before releasing him.

When they rendezvoused at the pier, the two Lin family brothers had already arrived first, and when they saw Qiaowen coming over, Lin Zihui excitedly greeted him, "Ah Wen, you're here?"

It was obvious that going on a sea trip with his older brother was a joy for him. Originally, Qiao Wen had tried very hard to appear relaxed, but the thought that perhaps before long, Lin Zi Hui would have to face the reality that his brother was the big boss behind the scenes was bound to be a big blow to him.

The good thing was that because of Chen Canaan, it made sense that he would be depressed even if he looked depressed.

Lin Zihui took him by the shoulders and soothed him, "Ah Wen, I know you're worried about Ah Nan, I believe he's fine, he's so capable, who can do anything to him? I'm just worried about him going astray and doing bad things now."

Qiao Wen smiled helplessly and said, "I'm fine, let's come out to play, don't talk about him, it's quite a spoiler."

Lin Zihui bristled, "When he comes back, I'll have to give him a good talking to."

Jovian nodded and looked up at Lin Zirui waiting beside the yacht, raising his hand and waving at him, "Jeffrey!"

Lin Zi Rui smiled and nodded, he wore sunglasses on his face, and even when he was out at sea, he wore black suit pants and a white shirt, only without a tie, and with his sleeves half rolled up at the elbow, he was in a half-casual, half-serious outfit.

It had to be said that he did have the air of a nobleman from head to toe.

She was a beautiful woman, but she was a thief.

A group of people boarded the yacht, Lin Zi Hui and Qiao Wen both came alone without assistants or bodyguards, Lin Zi Rui did bring three of his men, one of them was none other than Qin Yunfei who was wearing a flowery shirt and beach pants.

"Fei, go start the boat." Lin Zi Rui commanded.

Qin Yunfei nodded and went to the driver's side.

This was a brand new luxury yacht with a total of three floors, and Qiao Wen followed Lin Zi Rui to the uppermost platform, on which was a huge leather sofa, and the coffee table had already been set up with chilled drinks and fine wine.

Paired with today's windy, cloudless day and the many ships out at sea, Jovian would have enjoyed the sea trip if he didn't have something on his mind.

Lin Zi Rui greeted his brother and Qiao Wen and sat down, took out a bottle of drink and poured it into three glasses, smiling, "I know Wen doesn't drink, so we'll have the drink first, and Zi Hui and I will have another drink later."

Lin Zihui took the glass, looked around and said, "Big brother, this new toy of yours is really nice, I'll get one later."

Lin Zi Rui laughed, "You can use it anytime you like, there's no need to buy another one, it's also considered less polluting."

Lin Zihui nodded cheerfully, "That's right, then I'll just buy an unpowered sailboat later on, it doesn't use oil."

Jovian looked at the brotherly and friendly duo without moving, and said, "It's a nice ship indeed, Jeffrey will give us a good tour later."

Lin Zi Rui said, "Of course, the cabin room was designed by me personally, it's the only one in the world. When I finish my drink, I'll have to show it to you guys to satisfy my vanity."

Looking at his calm demeanor, Jovian thought again of the master-at-arms sitting alone on the hill on the sandbox in his apartment. He was like that skipper at the moment, watching tonight's deal. He was personally present, there would not be that part of inspecting the goods, he only needed to see the ship enter the waters and would immediately send a message to Boss Li.

Waiting for a drink to go down, the yacht slowly sailed away from the dock, and Lin Zi Rui took the two down to visit. The cabin is a suite, with a parlor kitchen and bedroom, the decor is simple and chic, revealing the owner's elegant taste everywhere.

But Qiaowen didn't care about that, he cared about Lin Zirui's radio equipment. Yet there was no suspected contraption to be seen in this glimpse of the cabin, or in the cockpit.

And Lin Zi Rui was always relaxed out of the trip, the cargo ships that entered the harbor, one by one passed by, he did not have any unusual behavior at all. After noon, the first BBQ ended, everyone ate and drank enough, and then lay down on the upper terrace to take a nap in the wind.

Qiao Wen was simply about to drift off to sleep when Lin Zi Rui suddenly got up, "I'm going down to use the restroom."

Qiaowen's face remained languid, but in his heart, alarm bells were ringing, and when the people left, he sat up unexpectedly.

Lin Zi Hui, who was already on the verge of falling asleep, said in a daze, "Ah Wen, you're going to the restroom too?"
