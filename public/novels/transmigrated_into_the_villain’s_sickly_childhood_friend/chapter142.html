Lin Zi Rui, who was already lax, unexpectedly failed to defend against his blow, and the gun in his hand flew off. Zhou Renjun quickly reacted, his body lunged forward, kicking the gun that had just landed out of the deck.

And at that moment, Lin Zi Rui also realized something, because the radio that was thrown off the boat by Qiao Wen did not make the sound of falling into the water. He pushed Lin Zi Hui out of the way, rushed to Qiao Wen's side, and looked under the boat, only to see a man in a black bathing suit, holding up the black box with one hand, and sliding the water with the other hand, like a frog, quietly rowing towards the stern of the boat against the hull.

Lin Zirui's face changed drastically.

This person was no other than Canaan Chen.

Chen Canaan realized the gaze from above, raised his head and looked upwards, saying, "Luckily, I caught it, otherwise it would have been a problem if it had gone into the water!"

Anyway, people have been found, he did not bother to continue to swim to the stern, until the center of the boat, climbed on the hull of the hanging life buoys, water monkeys as fast as scurrying up to the boat - many years of experience on the beam, at this time once again came in handy.

He stepped barefoot on the deck, his wet body dripping downward, reached up to touch his face, and said, "Sir Chow, Xiao Qiao and I are not a bad match, are we?"

Lin Zi Rui's face was icy cold as he faced last week's Ren Jun, the other man had already pointed a gun at him, and said word for word back to Chen Canaan's, "That's for sure, you and Wen have always been the perfect partners."

When Lin Zi Rui rushed over just now, Qiao Wen had already silently moved aside and was now standing side by side with Chen Canaan.

When Lin Zi Rui looked over again, Chen Canaan pulled Qiao Wen behind himself as if he was protecting his calf, and glared angrily at him himself.

Originally in the driver's cab of the three, has long been heard running out, in addition to Qin Yunfei, the remaining two Lin Zirui's henchmen, obviously no knowledge of this, but also know the identity of Zhou Renjun, do not dare to move in the slightest, only blankly looking at the confrontation of several people.

Jovian frowned.

Zhou Renjun said, "Lin Zihui, put your hands up!"

Lin Zi Rui slowly raised his hand and said in a warm voice word by word, "Zi Hui, do you believe them or big brother? If you believe big brother ......"

However, Qiao Wen interrupted him, "Zi Hui, don't listen to what he said, you forgot what your Abba told you before he died, he didn't let you associate with the Lin family because he knew what his eldest son was like. Lin Zi Rui gently breathed a sigh of relief and laughed, "I was right in thinking that the person who understands me the most in this world is really you, Ah Wen."

Because he had finally separated that face from Lin Nan completely, Qiao Wen faced him at this time without any waves in his heart, and only hoped that everything would settle down as soon as possible.

Because he's really kind of tired.

With Ah Wen and this many of you with me, I really don't lose."

Everyone realized something was wrong, and Zhou Renjun had even pulled the trigger first before reacting to it.

However it didn't work, Lin Zi Rui pulled out a miniature grenade from somewhere and pulled the fuse open.

It was just that when it was too late, it was too soon, as several people reacted to jumping into the sea, a figure darted over like an arrow out of a string, slamming into Lin Zi Rui, and together with the grenade, they flew over the fence.

Boom.

A shocking wave exploded, and the yacht was nearly overturned in this huge wave. The people on the boat fell to the ground in a heavenly spin, and Chen Canaan tightly protected Qiao Wen under his body.

I don't know how long it took, but Qiao Wen finally came to his senses from the drowsy buzzing, and barely opened his eyes to see Chen Canaan above him with his eyes tightly closed, and hurriedly reached out and pushed him, "Brother Nan!"

Qiao Wen shook his head and turned his head to look around, Zhou Renjun and Lin Zi Hui, as well as the two men who didn't know what was going on, were also struggling to get up, apparently none of them were in any serious trouble.

The ship had come to a standstill and the waters had regained their calm, and Jovian looked across the railing toward the surface, where there was a faint pool of dark red blood that was dispersing.

He hadn't expected the dust to settle on the Fifth Master in this way.

What's more, I didn't expect this to be the end of Qin Yunfei.

Zhou Renjun took out his cell phone to contact the nearby brothers, in fact, he did not need to contact, just now this movement, waiting for the police not far away, has also opened the boat sailed over.

Lin Zihui echoed in a daze, "I'm fine too."

Zhou Renjun walked over and patted him on the shoulder, "Zi Hui, don't think nonsense, you still have us friends."

Lin Zi Hui was silent.

Qiao Wen had originally wanted to comfort him for a few moments, but thinking that such a big deal was not something that could be consoled in a few words, it would be better to give him space to slowly digest it.

Anyway, it all goes back to the past.

If Qin Yunfei was still alive, he might be able to take the initiative to shake hands with him and call him Brother Fei.

But obviously that won't happen, so this person can also be completely removed from his life.

He felt that he was a bit of a villain, just like Lin Zi Rui said, he originally came from a rotten background and didn't have that much greatness in his chest, he just wanted to live a peaceful life with Qiao Wen.

Now that this ideal, at last, was coming, he was of course fiercely happy.

Jovian said, "Go back and hurry to the hospital to check, be careful of another concussion."

Jovian laughed, "Well, if it's going to be really stupid, I'll be in charge of you for the rest of your life."
