"Tian Fusheng," Gu Yuanbai rubbed his brow, his energy low, "Make me a cup of strong tea."

Often times, as the dynasty lengthens, the more constraints the emperor is subjected to.

Military and imperial power was alive and well at the time of the founding emperors, the first emperors had the ability to lift the table, they had soldiers in their hands, they had the power to strike down the world, and their reforms could come from the top down. However, as the dynasty aged, the power in the emperor's hands became less and less.

On the land of Daheng, there were powerful clans of the local strongholds, which were so powerful that they annexed land and violated the law, and some of them even killed people, which was the black power of the ancient times. How could the central government tolerate the local authorities? They occupied tens of thousands of acres of good land and farms, some of them colluded with the government, and some of them even took over the government. ①

The lineage is involved with the lineage, and a piece of sludge can be pulled from one vine.

The emperor not only had to balance the bureaucratic clique and eunuch clique with the military power of the honored ones, but also had to deal with these magnates.

Such a situation could only be broken by forceful means, and then the order of Gu Yuanbai could be reconstructed.

Gu Yuanbai knew that the Daheng Dynasty was surrounded by enemy countries that coveted it, and he also knew of certain unstable factors within its borders.

And the factors within the territory are some of his indulgences.

He had deliberately spared some of the remnants of the powerhouse Lu Feng, turning a blind eye to their escape, just because Gu Yuanbai still had a use for keeping them.

Perhaps in some people's eyes, he was sitting precariously as an emperor, and this world was about to be in turmoil.

But what they didn't know was that Gu Yuanbai was waiting for this upheaval, and was even stealthily pushing for changes within the territory behind the scenes. He had driven the remnants of Lu Feng to where he wanted them to go, intending to use this upheaval to pull out the most deeply rooted part of the tumor within the Daheng Kingdom.

He intends to use the power of his enemies to stamp out the land and wealth of the powerful families.

After the enemies had stamped out the powerful clans, Gu Yuanbai would use his most benevolent reputation to take over the land, peasants, gold and silver that had fallen into the hands of the enemies.

He will use the name of the king's division, which stands at the highest moral ground, to go and wipe out these brutal and greedy rebels.
