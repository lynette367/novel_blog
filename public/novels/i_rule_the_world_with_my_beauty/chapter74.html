The sparrow in the birdcage suddenly bumped up against the cage, Xue Yuan looked down and laughed, "What are you bumping into, do you want to die so badly?"

He glanced at the plaque of the Chu Mansion not far away and came to a realization, "Or do you have eyes for someone else, Lord Chu?"

Kong Yilin bowed deeply, "I will fulfill my mission."

Gu Yuanbai asked him to do two things. One was to go and explore the people's customs along the way, looking for valuable local resources that could be developed. The second was to find out how the people in various places were hiding their accounts and how well they were being indoctrinated.

To organize the household registers, officials had to go door-to-door, counting the number of people and checking their appearance and age to see if they could match the information recorded in the official records.

This is a huge project, and progress is advancing slowly, but once the approximate number of people missing from these two areas is tallied, the country is roughly known.
