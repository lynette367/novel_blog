The other thing is also related to Gu Yuanbai, this Chang Yuyan once made thirteen poems in one breath to satirize the dignitaries who don't know the suffering of the people, not only to satirize the powerful minister Lu Feng, but also to satirize him as an emperor.

I think you are all hot chickens if you let all the poor people under the sky to feed you big lords who only know how to eat delicacies and wear fancy clothes."

Thinking of this, Gu Yuanbai deepened his smile, "Do you have a poem to present as well?"

Chang Yuyan's writing, whether it was articles or poems, were all beautifully written, and even better, he was also famous, and was an excellent talent who could stir up public opinion. Chang Yuyan subconsciously followed and did as he was told, and when he raised his eyes, he saw the face of the saint looking at him with a smile on his face.

The saint looked at him appreciatively and said towards the ministers on his side, "My Daheng's young talents are all of a high caliber and are good men who can stand up to the heavens and the earth."

Chang Yuyan's ears instantly reddened, and she only felt a wave of shame roil up in her heart.
