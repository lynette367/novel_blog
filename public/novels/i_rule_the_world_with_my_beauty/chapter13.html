Gu Yuanbai's brows furrowed, instantly alert, and he slowly backed up, retreating to another angle before he could see what was in the grass.

With black fur, vertical green pupils, and hideous fangs exposed, they were actually two adult wolves.

Gu Yuanbai's heart sank.

These two wolves were head to head burying their heads in the grass and licking something, and it was only then that Gu Yuanbai remembered that this seemed to be the place where the bowl of deer blood had been shattered during the day. The blood in the corridor was swept clean, but the blood that flowed into the grass left a fishy odor because it was covered up.
