The willow branches in February have just sent out new buds, the spring in the cold wind has not yet blossomed, the dust rises up to the sky, ten thousand horses, gradually turning into a small black spot.

Gu Yuanbai exhaled a mouthful of cloudy air and slowly smiled again.

Separation is not a big deal, Xue Yuan will eventually come back one day, while each other young, it is good to go now.
