Clansmen who have good heirs in their families, or who have a reputation for being wise in their own right, are more likely to commit foolishness.

The wind is calm in the capital, but the palace is out of the footsteps of dozens of eunuchs holding the imperial decree in a hurry.

These eunuchs were sent to travel to the various princely houses of the patriarchs to read the will of the Emperor.

Gu Yuanbai gave them a chance to confess and be lenient.

The language on the imperial decree is concise, but the eunuch reads it out fiercely and harshly, asking them to hand over the remnants of Lu Feng hidden in the mansion, handing over the tumor that encourages them to be disrespectful to His Majesty, and as long as they are handed over, His Majesty can treat them leniently.

Everyone in the Prince Zong's House was trembling and terrified, but no matter how they pressed, the eunuch only said that there was still half an hour.

After half an hour, if the person who dares to do it doesn't dare to come out and confess on his own initiative, then he will have to accept the Emperor's thunderbolt.

And in this half hour, the eunuch stood archly before the gate of the clan house, looking with a cold face at the faces of the royal relatives, either innocent or apprehensive.

More than one person's heart thumped faster and faster, hiding within the crowd with a sweaty face, cold sweat sliding down to the ground drop by drop from his chin.

Scared, terrified, but still in disbelief of what His Holiness could do.

Time passed by, and it seemed as if the capital city knew what was going to happen, as the ministers banned the gate houses, and there was not a single person on the streets in front of the gate of the clan's royal residence.

The dusky sun in the early afternoon gradually moves westward.

The legs of the clan relatives who had done certain things kneeling on the ground were soft, not knowing whether it was because they had been kneeling for half an hour or because of some kind of inexplicable fear. In the midst of the time passing, not a single person in the Royal House of Patriarchs came forward to take the initiative to recognize the punishment. Finally, half an hour passed.

Their footsteps were so heavy that they could make the ground shake, and their shields and long swords flashed with bloodthirsty light. The leading general roared, "By the decree of His Majesty, we remove the Qing rebel army, idle people make way!"

On the street, the door of the house is closed, from the window gap to see this long line of black armor forbidden army ran through the door of their own homes, the sharp tip of those swords reflecting the light of the setting sun, on the ground, in front of the door to cut out a path of horrifyingly bright light.

These forbidden soldiers that Gu Yuanbai had spent a lot of money to raise, the daily training and drills gave them a strong body, and the daily good meat and rice gave them the strength to be able to hold up their armor and pick up their swords and shields.

Countless clansmen with large open doors and mansions looked at this group of forbidden army when they came over then they were in two wars, until these forbidden army ran past their own doors, these clansmen felt that they were alive again and could breathe, they fell softly to the ground, the slaves also fell softly to the ground, until now, they didn't know that the emperor's words were true.

When the people inside the mansion gate saw this dark and pressurized piece of forbidden troops, there were already people who fainted with both eyes rolled back. Panic spread, and finally someone couldn't help but let out a low sob.

General Cheng coldly raised his voice, "We are under orders to clear out the rebels. If there is any resistance, shoot on sight!"

King Qi couldn't take a single breath and almost pouted as he stared hard at the group of armored soldiers in front of him with wide eyes, his legs shaking more and more under his robe.

He also had a young child by his side, that was the Prince's youngest son, who was born sharp and intelligent, and had been known as a prodigy of goodness since he was a child. At this time, this child is grasping the hand of the servant beside him, crying out to find his mother.

A kid that's not even weaned yet, and he's "as smart and kind as the current emperor"?

Bah! General Cheng's eyes were on fire, and the soldiers behind him were dumbfounded.

King Qi was the brother of the late emperor, he was a dozen years younger than the late emperor, and when the late emperor claimed the throne, King Qi was not much of a threat. King Qi was also able to be a peaceful prince in his own right, so he had gained a good reputation.

But when Gu Yuanbai came to the throne, Gu Yuanbai, who was physically weak and likely unable to give birth to an heir, gave the King of Qi the opportunity to expand his ambitions. When Lu Feng, a powerful minister, was around, the King of Qi took gold and silver and had a name with Lu Feng, and while the other royal relatives were groveling, he had already dreamed of bigger things.

Lu Feng didn't dare to risk the greatness of heaven and earth by claiming the title of king and emperor, he only dared to wait for Gu Yuanbai to die and support the last puppet emperor, Qi Wang was too old for Lu Feng to feel at ease, but Qi Wang had a son.

He had many, many sons.

But how did Gu Yuanbai find out?

How dare Gu Yuanbai?

He's his royal uncle!

The forbidden soldiers blocking the door stared at him in silence.

They're all royal relatives no matter what!

The King of Qi said in a loud voice, "You and the others dare to intimidate the royal relatives and dare to make a move against this king?!"

All the masters in the Qi King's Mansion broke into a shout, "We want to see His Majesty! You say you want to clear the rebels, why are you surrounding our royal residence!"

The number of people who broke into curses was as if they were subjected to dependence, and more and more people were impassioned, pointing backhandedly at the forbidden army and cursing them to death.

It wasn't until the unknown son of the Qi Wang Fu family was hit head-on with a blow to the head and fell to the ground with blood pouring out of his head that all this noise came to a violent halt.

Large swaths of the forbidden army rushed into the Qi Wang House, the sound of crying and howling shook the sky, the slaves lay on the ground, the Qi Wang House was like hell.

The masters were clubbed and the slaves were clubbed. But while the slaves can be killed, the masters must still be left breathing.

King Qi fell softly into a pool of blood as he watched those black-armored forbidden soldiers touch the study, and even quickly captured a few of Lu Feng's men.

They were fast like they already knew who these people were.

King Qi was dizzy, and the anger in his heart had long since turned into shivering fear.

Gu Yuanbai, Gu Yuanbai ...... He is even more ruthless than the late emperor, too ruthless and terrible.

This emperor is terrible.
