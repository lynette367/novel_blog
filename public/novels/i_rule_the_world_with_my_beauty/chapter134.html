A young general of great fame and prestige, a young general who had been heard and feared in the northern frontier, was hard pushed to such a bad state of abandoning his armor and losing his helmet.

His sweat had soaked through his clothes, causing them to turn two shades of color. Gu Yuanbai leaned on top of the wall of the car, and with every shake, the lucite beads in front of him would make a crisp ringing sound.
