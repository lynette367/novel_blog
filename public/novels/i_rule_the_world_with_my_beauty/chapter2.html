There are two official schools in the capital, one is the state school, the second is the Imperial College. That night, the news in the state school somehow spread to the Imperial College, the Imperial College's headmaster had the cheek to issue a unilateral invitation to cooperate, but also set up four teams of cuju team, intends to tomorrow in front of the emperor with the state school to have a good competition.

What's so fun about playing by yourselves in the academy? Bring us along! Our students are tall and strong, and they're good at soccer!

In the piece of finding pain for the state school, the Imperial College took it to death.

On the second day, His Majesty really drove, His Majesty dressed in regular clothes, sitting in a pavilion covered with a cloth, this time is still spring cold, His Majesty's side of the waiters and civil and military ministers, none of them dared to let His Majesty to blow some cold winds again.

Pavilion only to the side of the field to the empty, fire pots piled up on one side, at this time the game has not yet begun, but the side of the field has been crowded with the people who have heard of the game.

These people, they want to see His Majesty.

There was a cacophony of voices outside the arena, all topping the sky after the hustle and bustle, and there were people who climbed up into the trees, hugging the trunks and craning their necks to look into the arena.

The son of the Minister of the Household, Tang Mian, clenched his fists to the point of some numbness, he only felt his chest tighten with tension, and after glancing at the pavilion in the distance where His Holiness was staying, the tension turned into a bearish fighting spirit again.

His best friend, the Prince of Pingchang Marquis, was at that moment nervously speaking with Tang Mian, "I feel as if my calves are cramping up."

Tang Mian was shocked, "Rub it quickly, the game will start in a while, we have to play beautifully for the Emperor!"

"It's because I know that His Holiness is here that I'm nervous," Marquis Pingchang's son said with a bitter face, "My father heard that I'm going to kick a soccer ball for His Holiness today, and called me up early in the morning before dawn, juggling fists and running, and I'm exhausted to death."

Tang Mian was dumbfounded as he turned left and right worriedly, "You can't be missing when you're playing the Dragon's Tail so well."

Marquis Pingchang's son couldn't help but feel smug as he struggled to swing his leg and let out a "hiss", "I'll rub it first."

Most of the field to play cuju are not yet the crown rites of the boy, heard that the saint is coming, and now there are so many people around to see, although there is some stage fright, but excitement and excitement accounted for the majority.

Tian Fusheng heartbroken saint, carefully warmed a pot of tea for him, "running up sweating, just after sweating easy to get cold, in the end is young, can stand."

Tang Mian's ears reddened, and he was busy drinking it all in one sip, "He doesn't have a door on his mouth, and he's just talking nonsense."

The boy was in a hurry and said, "Feel free to put on some clothes first, the master is in a hurry."

Marquis Pingchang's son hurriedly followed him over, and together with Marquis Pingchang, they went to the pavilion to face the saint.

Gu Yuanbai happened to have invited the head teachers of the two academies over to speak, and after receiving the briefing, he said, "Come in."

I want to paint ......" he pointed to the sky, and although he was afraid, he still boldly felt an overwhelming excitement, "paint that one."

"I'm not crazy," Li Yan squeezed his eyes towards him, "we are not according to the painting, eyebrows and eyes in me, nose and lips in you, want to see the painting we will meet the painting together, usually nothing to hide the painting in the bedroom, who can still find out?"
