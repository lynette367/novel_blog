Gu Yuanbai walked out of the house filled with the smell of medicine and looked at a lone dead tree in the compound, his eyes a little dry.

Beside him, Tian Fusheng and his palace attendant had already covered their faces and cried, and the imperial physician followed the saint's left side, whispering the diagnosis.

"Go."

The carriage bumped and undulated on the uneven road, and as the Buyeo Village gradually moved away, Tian Fusheng had already wiped away his face full of tears and was worried and careful as he waited on Gu Yuanbai in the carriage.

Gu Yuanbai leaned on top of the soft collapse, looking at the scenery outside the carriage and fuming until the carriage drove into the capital, then he called a halt, got off the carriage, and personally headed towards the palace on foot.

The capital is at the foot of the Son of Heaven, prosperous and populous, there are a few children holding up sugar figures laughing and running past, Gu Yuanbai stopped and looked at these children.

Men in rough linen clothes were doing street-side work, women were hard at work on household chores, men and women, young and old, were all busy trying to make a good living.

Gu Yuanbai didn't know what he was thinking, and the guards protecting him behind him as well as the palace people didn't know what he was thinking either.

They simply followed the young tenno in silence, alert to everything around them.

The capital was full of dignitaries, and Gu Yuanbai and his party did not attract much attention. Gu Yuanbai withdrew his mind and his feet continued to walk forward, however, two steps had just been taken when a flake of snow suddenly drifted down from in front of his eyes.

"Ah! It's snowing daddy!"

"It's snowing!"

A cry of delight from children rang out around them, Gu Yuanbai lost his smile and shook his head, Tian Fusheng hurriedly draped the fox fur over him, "Master, get on the carriage, right?"

"Walk a little longer," Gu Yuanbai said, "I haven't seen snow in the capital for a long time either."

The snow in February in the capital is like goose feathers, the head of the guards held up the umbrella for the saint, the snow white snowflakes slid down from the edge of the umbrella, and some of them were blown by the wind onto the saint's green silk that was hanging down to his waist.

They walked past the tavern and teahouse, and upstairs on the Scholar's House, Xue Yuan was next to the window shaking a bottle of wine, and as soon as he looked down, he saw this line of people.

His Holiness's face was hidden under an umbrella, but the faces of Tian Fusheng and the head of the guards were familiar, and Xue Yuan shook his drink, stuck his hand out of the window, and loosened his five fingers one by one as he waited for the party to pass his window.

"Ka-ching-"

The wine bottle shattered and fell not far behind Gu Yuanbai, and the guards instantly tensed their bodies and looked menacingly towards the upper floors.

Gu Yuanbai pushed away the umbrella, his sight was not blocked, when he looked upwards, there was a hand resting casually at the second floor window, without much thought, Gu Yuanbai knew that it was the owner of this hand who threw down the bottle of wine that almost hit him.

The corners of Gu Yuanbai's lips hooked up, but his voice was as cold as a snowflake, "Bring him down."

A few moments later, Xue Yuan, who was full of the smell of wine, was brought down from the Scholarship House by the guards, the snowflakes fluttered and flew even more, and the umbrella didn't have much more use, Gu Yuanbai had already asked the head of the guards to put away the useless umbrella, and after standing alone in the cold wind for a while, a lot of white snow had already accumulated on his body.

Xue Yuan was brought to Gu Yuanbai's side, and when Gu Yuanbai saw it was him, he continued to smile, "So it's the son of General Xue's family."

Tian Fusheng said, "Master, should we send Mr. Xue back to General Xue's residence?"

While they were talking, Xue Yuan burped and stretched his face to look at Gu Yuanbai for a moment before saying, "Your Holiness?!"

Gu Yuanbai quietly looked at him, flakes of snow were hanging down from his hair, fox fur, and even his eyelashes, and these flakes fell on his body surprisingly didn't melt away immediately. Compared to him, Xue Yuan's body was clean and dry, and those snowflakes had already been vaporized into water by the heat from his body before they even fell.

Seeing this, Gu Yuanbai was in an even worse mood.

No emperor would be in a good mood in the presence of someone who would take over his power in the future and who was a hundred times healthier than he was.

Tian Fusheng covered his nose and pinched his throat, "Master, Mr. Xue should be drunk."

Gu Yuanbai suddenly smiled, he walked to stand next to the porcelain tiles, the person who was escorting Xue Yuan also brought Xue Yuan over, Xue Yuan's expression was relaxed, his legs walked slowly, looking at it this way, those guards didn't look like they were crushing people, but like they were serving them.

The snowflakes drifted down to his nose, just as an itch rose, and Gu Yuanbai coughed lowly a few times and said in a mute voice, "Kneel down."

The guards who pressed down on Xue Yuan's arms exerted force, solidly pressing Xue Yuan's knees onto the sharp porcelain tiles that shattered to the ground.

Large pieces of broken porcelain pierced into the flesh, and blood instantly spread over his pants and onto the ground, snowflakes drifted onto this blood and were quickly melted into water, allowing the blood to spread even faster on the ground.

The perfunctory look on Xue Yuan's face abruptly withdrew, and he looked up at Gu Yuanbai in a gloomy and uncertain manner.

Gu Yuanbai smiled softly at him and suddenly reached out and tugged on his hair, he lowered his head and said word for word in Xue Yuan's ear, "I'm in a very bad mood today, Little Sir Xue, don't give me the chance to be able to break your mother's heart, do you hear me?"

Xue Yuan was forced to lift his chin, his jaw tensed into an arc ready to rise, his scalp was tugged to the point of numbness, and when the word "mother" reached his ears, he smiled sadly and sneered, "Yuan knows."

Gu Yuanbai: "Very well."

He let go of his hand, Xue Yuan slightly sideways, watching the little emperor's lips pale with a smile as he backed away from his ear, the pain in his knee gradually receded, but Xue Yuan's whole body was already on fire.

He looked down at the injury on his knee and grinned a grim smile away. Only when the Saint and his party were gone did Xue Yuan brace himself on the ground and stand up, limping towards the Xue Mansion.
