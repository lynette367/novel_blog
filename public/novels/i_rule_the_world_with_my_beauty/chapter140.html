There are several city walls in the northwest, and when Gu Yuanbai was on top of the walls overlooking the miles, he suddenly remembered that in the original story, didn't the Western Xia capture five or six cities in Daheng from the northwest?

And now, everything has changed.

The purpose of this battle was not to defeat Xixia, but to invade Xixia in one fell swoop. The harsh winter conditions multiplied the pressure on the logistical lines that followed to transport munitions and food, and no part of the battle could go wrong, and those in the rear had to ensure the safety of the soldiers on the front line.

The people who stayed in the rear were all people that Gu Yuanbai trusted extremely.
