At the end of September, the days had come to the eve of All Saints' Day.

The congratulatory gifts sent by various countries have already been put into the national treasury one by one. Regarding the congratulatory gifts of those powerful people, Gu Yuanbai had them returned, and then hinted at the matter of the northern locust plague.

The magnates were really all powerful people with flexible brains, and immediately reacted to the hints of the saint; they inquired about the locust plague in the north, gathered together, and finally planned to transport 100,000 ducks to the north to exterminate the locusts.

Locusts produce toxins when they gather in large numbers, the yellow locusts are poisonous and only the green locusts that fall alone are non-toxic. It is the toxicity emitted by the locusts in large numbers that keeps the birds of prey away from the locusts.

Autumn locusts can only live for three months, when they are about to die, they will look for a place to lay eggs, this period is called the waking period. Waking period, the locusts in the body of the toxin will be dissipated, this time is the ducks to go up to eat them, 100,000 ducks, a duck a day can eat two hundred locusts, can quickly control the locust plague.

The magnates counted the time, now to the northern border to send ducks, delivered just when the locusts have arrived in the period of waking, ducks to that can play a role, and so will the locusts ate up, these ducks can eat the belly full of meat in the bowl of the soldiers, which is not a bad idea?

They were busy expressing their loyalty and attentiveness to His Majesty, while His Majesty, in the middle of the capital, was burning with a little, a tiny bit of hope that he could survive.

"Grandfather had said to my daughter that the formula for the treatment of the congenital malady one, he also only got fifty percent of it, and his younger brother was more talented than him, if this uncle survived from fleeing the famine with a large number of medical books, there must be a formula in it that can cure His Holiness's disease."
