Xue Yuan had something that Gu Yuanbai envied, and that was the freedom that no one else had in this era; he did as he pleased and had a body that could match his talents.

His feelings and temperament were like fire, and if Gu Yuanbai had been an onlooker, he would have appreciated a personality like Xue Yuan's. If he were in modern times, he might have become friends with Xue Yuan who raised their glasses and drank.

But in ancient times, in the feudal dynasty, he was like a madman with such hot emotions.

Gu Yuanbai exerted himself and pulled his hand out.

"To be disrespectful to your superiors, your words have a deficit," Gu Yuanbai said, "Xue Yuan, I have spared you many times. Forgive you for having collected the Jinghu South and Jiangnan areas for me, forgive you for having risked everything to save me, and forgive you for your father's dedication to me. Some of the oversteps you've done in your day-to-day life, I can turn a blind eye to."

"Others know how to take this opportunity to be more polite, in and out of the degree, so as to please me," Gu Yuanbai voice the more cold, "only you, not only do not know to restrain, but also time and time again to challenge the bottom line of my heart."

"I want this life of yours, so why do I need to show my face to you? There is no shortage of people who want to fight for me."

There was a vague and inexplicable anger rising in the bottom of Gu Yuanbai's heart, which rushed to his heart, and his sleeve robe swung violently as he reached out and pinched Xue Yuan's chin, suppressing his voice, "Any one of them would have to be more obedient than you."

Xue Yuan's breathing heaved, his body tightened, and blood once again oozed from the wound that had just been bandaged.

He tried his best to suppress the haze in his heart and feigned a disinterested smile, "Your Holiness, none of them are as useful as me."

"This big talk makes me want to laugh," Gu Yuanbai pulled up his lips and smiled coldly, "Geniuses and talents are all in the royal door. Xue Yuan, how great is your talent, so great that all the world's talents can't be compared to you?"

"And how confident are you that none of them will be more loyal to me than you?"

Xue Yuan was silent.

After a long time, he sighed quietly.

Gu Yuanbai thought that he had admitted his mistake and let go of his hand, "Today's fifty large planks are the punishment for Xue Qing's wanton behavior."

"I only wish you to be clear," Gu Yuanbai's low, muffled voice was so good that one's ears would go soft, but the coldness in his words froze the hearts of the people, "Daheng's law is not something that can be transgressed just because you have the talent to do so."

Gu Yuanbai was not a pedantic person, his thoughts were even more advanced than any other person in this world.

However, the ancient law, the power of an emperor, these absolutely do not allow anyone to step over.

Imperial power is honored, Gu Yuanbai is an emperor, the emperor has to consolidate the imperial power, once a person makes a mistake and cannot be punished, what deterrent can the emperor have?

Today, no matter what kind of reason it was, it was possible to cut off the tail finger of Marquis Anle's son. Then tomorrow, is it possible to go ahead and kill off someone else's life for another reason?

His Holiness concluded, "If fifty lashes are not enough, then beat them until they are."

After saying that, Gu Yuanbai turned around and headed out.

His face was expressionless, and his intimidating pressure made the people inside and outside the room not dare to raise their heads in the slightest. As he crossed the threshold with one foot, Xue Yuan spoke behind him.

"Your Holiness, even if I am not talented enough, I have something that they cannot afford or dare to give," Xue Yuan's voice was calm, "I--"

"Shut up." Gu Yuanbai said.

Xue Yuan smiled seemingly.

The sweat was salty and wet, staining the mattress. The smell of blood grew stronger, but Xue Yuan looked calmer than before.

He braced himself and looked at Gu Yuanbai from within the stifling and steaming air of the house, his voice not too loud, not too small, four flat, "Your Holiness asked me earlier why I refused the transfer, and I can say it now, because I want to stay by your side."

"My heart is pleased with you," his voice was steeply lowered, as if it came from far, far away, somewhat distorted, "the bell is in love with Your Majesty, a heart that no one else dares to give."

Because the next person would be afraid to die.

With a fluttering sound, all the people who heard these words had their knees softened, and all of them fell to the ground.

Chills ran up their spines, cold sweat slipping from their heads, and as they listened to Xue Yuan's treacherous words, they only wished they hadn't appeared here.

Gu Yuanbai didn't say anything.

In the middle of the narrow courtyard, so many people did not make a single sound. The raucous cicadas continued to chirp, one after another, hypnotizing people.

The yard was full of people who were afraid of losing their lives because they were listening to these words.

Even Tian Fusheng was on tenterhooks and nervous.

Only after a long time did Gu Yuanbai slowly say, "Tian Fusheng, take these people down."

There were already people in the courtyard who couldn't restrain themselves from shivering, their expressions so terrified that it was as if they would lose their lives the next moment.

His Holiness continued, "Let them know what to remember and what to forget."

Tian Fusheng rose trembling, "Yes."

Gu Yuanbai did not look askance, as if nothing had just happened, nothing had been heard, and without changing his face, he continued to stride out of this small courtyard.

In modern times, Gu Yuanbai had no shortage of people courting him.

But Xue Yuan is particularly special in this case. Gu Yuanbai doesn't know if Xue Yuan was bent by his own kiss.

If so, he felt guilty in his heart, but after the guilt, what else could Gu Yuanbai do?

Whoever Xue Yuan likes, it's better than liking him.

Whoever it was had more time to spend with him than Gu Yuanbai.

It was only once His Holiness left that the people in the courtyard heaved a steep sigh of relief, and they sat paralyzed, thankful that they still had a life left.

With your skills, you would even be framed by your brother. As far as your brother's stupidity is concerned, tell me honestly, did you do it on purpose?"

"On purpose?" Xue Yuan moved his mouth, "The scout's brain is truly different from the norm."

"What does this, what does this mean?"

"Write when I tell you to write," Xue Yuan hooked his lips and smiled grimly, "write well, and master will reward you with something."
