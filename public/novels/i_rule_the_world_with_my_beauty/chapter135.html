The next morning after the morning court, Chu Wei will ask for an order to enter the Xuanzheng Hall.

He was dressed in official robes, and in his hands, he was holding a scroll of paintings that had been placed into a cloth belt. He was accompanied by an official of the Imperial Historical Office, who had always been obsessed with Li Qingyun's paintings and was quite knowledgeable about them. He was invited by Tian Fusheng to take a look at the two paintings to see if they were the same as the real one, and whether they could be merged into one.

Today is the rainy weather, the painting will flood some moisture, make the paper slightly wrinkled. The painting in the emperor's coffers has been placed on the paperwork, and the eyes of the officials of the Imperial Palace of Justice brightened, and they looked towards the painting one by one.

Gu Yuanbai laughed and joked, "This look in Wanqing's eyes, it's like he's going to set Li Qingyun's painting on fire in general."

Lord Wan gave a restrained smile and saluted with Chu Wei. After rising, Chu Wei handed the eunuch the cloth belt in his arms.

The upper and lower volumes of the Thousand Miles of Rivers and Mountains have finally been put together.

Gu Yuanbai took one look, then could not help but lose his smile, "Chu Qing, your painting must be fake."

Although he didn't understand paintings, he could at least tell how old or new the paintings were, if he looked at them individually it wasn't much, when the two paintings were put together, the difference between the old and the new abruptly grew.

Chu Wei's lips opened and closed, eventually pursing his lips straight and looking down at the painting on the table.

Looked a few disappointed looks.

Lord Wan suddenly "eek" a sound, close to look at Chu Wei's painting, "Your Holiness, this can be really strange, although the old and the new are different, but the painting of the brush or landscape direction, are Li Qingyun painting habits. Do not look at the old and new, just look at the painting, as if it really is Li Qingyun painting in general."

Gu Yuanbai froze, the tip of his nose wrinkling slightly, "Really?"

Lord Wan didn't dare to talk about the painting to the fullest, "I'll look at it again."

Rainy weather, there is no daylight, Mr. Wan looked more and more like, the heart also feel more strange. He will be up and down the two pictures together, look, no gap where the break, every place with the previous volume together, this is not a painting, imitation of the painting of the people and how to do it?

"It's too similar," exclaimed Lord Wan, "even if I knew it was an imitation, I wouldn't dare to say that there was any difference in the painting."

The corner of Gu Yuanbai's eyes hooked, "Interesting."

He stepped forward and Lord Wan backed away. His Holiness bent over and looked at the painting that Chu Wei had presented to him.

Chu Wei, on the other hand, was looking at His Holiness.

Gu Yuanbai's black hair wanted to fall on his spine, and with every slight movement he made, the few strands of hair on the very edge became dangerously heavy.

If it hangs down, will it sweep over Chu Wei's painting?

If he swept it, he was afraid that he would be stained with a corner of ink that had already been dampened by the humidity of the rain.

Chu Wei's thoughts have just passed, the hair of the Holy Spirit will slide down from both sides, Chu Wei subconsciously stepped forward quickly, and caught the hair in time before it touched the painting.

The eyes of the saint cast on his body, Chu Wei gentleman like jade, he calmly said: "This painting I do not know how many people's hands, or do not touch the saint as well."

Gu Yuanbai smiled, straightened up, and patted Chu Wei's arm, "Chu Qing is meticulous."

The black hair followed suit and cut away from Chu Wei's hand.

Chu Wei withdrew his hand, a subtle smile rising in his eyes, "I wouldn't dare."

In all the world, the only person who can be better off after being a soldier than before is Daheng.

Tian Fusheng choked and obediently said, "The imperial physician said that bedtime would be possible after half a month."

"Half a month," Gu Yuanbai grunted, "I remember."

Tian Fusheng's face was odd, "Lord Xue said the same thing."

Your Majesty, how is it that your heart is in tune with Lord Xue's?

Gu Yuanbai snorted out a laugh.

He went to sleep with such a pleasant mood, and when he woke up, Tian Fusheng told him that the people buried in Xue's house had come to report that Xue Yuan had been beaten by Xue's old general yesterday night and had been locked up in the ancestral hall with his wounds for the whole night.

As soon as Tian Fusheng's words fell, Gu Yuanbai coldly lowered his face. His face was unsightly, his eyes dark, and Tian Fusheng trembled, "Your Holiness?"
