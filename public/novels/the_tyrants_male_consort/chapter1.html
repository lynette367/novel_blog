Xue Yao maintained the position of holding the toy wooden carriage high above his head, motionless, with a serious expression as if he was about to stand up and blow up a bunker.

There was the sound of a group of boys laughing and joking around them, and the sound of a young child crying.

As his mind gradually cleared, the last memory in Xue Yao's mind was the roar of the airplane before it crashed.

He needed to find a quiet place to think about the current situation, so he put down the toy wooden horse he had raised above his head, his arms a little sore.

A boy's displeased voice came, "What's the dilly-dallying! Ah Yao? Drop it!"

Ah Yao? Is it calling out to him?
