Once again, herbs and crop seeds, 250 points for Tier 2 and 1,500 points for Ultimate.

Finally, there's Fabric Lumber and Steel, the same whitewash points required to open the Food Mall.

The nine side quests for the second level of the mall are all different.

But on second thought, most of those mission requirements, he couldn't complete.
