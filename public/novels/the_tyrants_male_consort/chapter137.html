In the midst of the division's fratricide, no one saw another warm baby all afternoon.

In the evening, after placing Gu Qingyuan temporarily in Lu Qian's side hall, Xue Yao left the palace to go to the Rui Wang Mansion to look for Lu Jinan to discuss the matter of guarding Aunt Po's temple.

He had planned to talk to Lu Qian about this matter, but he was worried that the rebel pups would go to the trouble of that household official on impulse.

In order to suppress the matter as soon as possible, the lesser the commotion, the better, Xue Yao felt that it would be more prudent to find King Rui to do the job.

After Lu Jinan was informed of the situation, he didn't hesitate to promise to help Xue Yao settle the matter and stayed at the mansion for the evening meal.

During the meal, when she saw Lu Jinan turn his head to dismiss the attendants, Xue Yao became alert.

"Xue Yao, many years ago, I thought you were a man who anticipated things like a god." Lu Jinan scrutinized Xue Yao with a calm gaze, "I hadn't realized it until you said it, not only the grain collection, but later on, when Ping Rong County had an epidemic, and when my father went on the expedition, you reminded me in advance to beware of some dangers, were these things told to you by Auntie Parker, the Goddess of God, or did you extrapolate them on your own?"

Xue Yao's heart thumped and his face blanched slightly.

"Don't be nervous." Lu Jinan personally lifted the wine pot and added a cup of wine for him, "I am just a little curious, I am in no way suspicious of you, if you have such unforeseen talent, how many disasters and regrets can you spare for Da Qi in the future? If you still trust me, you might as well tell me the truth, lest your talents not be fully utilized."

"Mister Xue naturally trusts Your Highness." Xue Yao immediately expressed his loyalty, "Otherwise, I wouldn't have informed you unreservedly of the things I know, things that even King Ning has no knowledge of."

Lu Jinan nodded, "Then where exactly did you learn about these things? Is it true that you have the ability to foretell things?"

Xue Yao was torn, there was no doubt that he trusted Lu Jinan, but admitting that he had such heavenly abilities was too great a threat.

And with all the major havoc of the original behind him, the future is unknowable to him now that everyone's fates have pretty much been turned in his direction.

If he admits at this point that he has so much unforeseen ability to foretell future disasters and fails to warn of them in advance, will others speculate that he is doing it on purpose, with bad intentions? Waiting for an opportunity to retaliate?

"How could I possibly have the ability to foretell things." Xue Yao decided to deny it, "All those things that your highness said are traceable, I did have some speculations, and then it just happened to really happen only, in reality, there were more things that I didn't guess accurately, your highness, do you still remember that I had reminded you in Ping Rong County to implement the change of law after the Royal Pole, and I never expected that the King of Ning would be made the Crown Prince."

After saying that, Xue Yao greeted Lu Jinan's probing gaze with a frank expression.

After a moment of silence, Lu Jinan nodded and raised his hand to greet him with a drink.

The three rows of halls in the three southern institutes were connected side by side, separated by a wall, and Lu Zhan lived in the middle institute, with the fifth brother to the east and the seventh brother to the west.

Now the fifth brother has gone to live in the palace outside the royal residence, and recently busy with the wedding, a few days did not come into the palace to accompany them to practice martial arts reading.

Lu Zhan took the corner door to find his brother, no notification was needed, and naturally there was no eunuch to tell him that there was an unfamiliar guest in the courtyard.

Gu Qingyuan's gaze gradually cleared, finally remembering where he had seen this little liar before.

This little liar has led a siege with his senior brother before, and seems to be his senior brother s ...... brother?

These days, are princes reduced to mixing food and cheating money?

By the light of the moon to see the small liar's face can not hide the color of guilt, Gu warrior forced not to laugh, silent for a moment, suddenly the long sword to the ground, leaning against the wall slowly sat on the ground, tilted his head back to look at the cold moon, sadly lamented: ''What else can be done? Do you think Gu looks like someone who can afford three taels of silver? I can only sell myself into slavery, waste all my martial arts skills, and never ask about my future."
