After following a group of doctors onto the official ship, Zhang Si realized the true purpose of Xue Yao's trip.

"Don't worry, I'm just afraid of encountering robbers on the road, and when we arrive at Ping Rong County, we won't let you come into contact with the patients." Xue Yao explained the situation to Zhang Si.

"What's there to worry about?" Zhang Si didn't think so: "My life is very hard, more than 30 years ago, my father and brother died of plague, but I survived it. You should be careful with your own body, I don't think you can handle this."

Xue Yao looked at Zhang Si in surprise: "Your family was victimized by the plague?"

"My mother and I escaped." Zhang Si smiled, "My mother, that sickly child, somehow didn't die in the plague. Her husband and son all died cleanly, but she tried to keep my seed with all her might, so she really escaped alive, and didn't catch the plague, but got consumptive disease."

Xue Yao was shocked to hear this.

In ancient times, when the common people encountered this kind of disaster, it was simply cruel and horrible, but Zhang Si was able to face these sufferings with such an indifferent attitude, I wonder what he had experienced.

Consumption is tuberculosis, ancient times is the existence of almost terminal disease, the rich infected, well will maintain, and may live a little longer, the poor infected, is tortured to death.

Xue Yao heard Zhang Si say that his mother was the only surviving relative, so he tried to help, "You are in the palace, if it is not convenient to take care of your mother ......"

Zhang Si snorted, "What else is there to take care of? My mother died long ago."

Xue Yao was dumbfounded.

Zhang Si looked at the scenery on both sides of the canal, narrowed his eyes helplessly recalled: "might as well catch that plague, two feet a stirrup to die clean, she got the damn consumption disease, the family grain and rice for the bit of copper, all for her to drink medicine to drink up, the more the body drank the worse, the person is not useful, a waste of money for nothing."

Xue Yao heard this somewhat harshly, but in his heart, he felt that he was not qualified to stand in the shoes of a clothed man and judge Zhang Si's character.

"Her breath hangs constantly, I can't leave her alone, can I?" Zhang Si stared blankly into the distance, recalling the beginning of his degeneration, "There was a charlatan who lied to me, saying that he had a miraculous medicine, and that three grains would be able to cure my mother's illness, and that the price was expensive, two taels of silver for one grain, so I went door-to-door in the middle of the night to take people's money, with the intention of selling my body to become a servant for the member's family in the future, and then repaying it after I had earned the money, and I really didn't feel like a hairy thief at that time. "

"Later ......" Zhang Si licked his lips, his face became gloomy: "Three pills did not eat well, but my mother got addicted to the medicine, no medicine life is worse than death, I went to find that bone-setter, he raised the price, one asked me to ask for five taels of silver. "

Xue Yao was suddenly stunned, and it was only at this moment that he saw real emotions in Zhang Si's vicissitudes of the eyes.

It turns out that real suffering can rob some people of the ability to show their grief.

All the burdens had to be carried by him alone, and there wasn't even time for pretense.

This seemingly bland attitude does not mean that he is apathetic; perhaps it is only by maintaining such a calm demeanor that he is able to keep from being knocked down by suffering.

Xue Yao suddenly had a hard time thinking to himself - in his last life, the little chubby cub was caught off guard carrying all the burdens when his father's big brother went.

Also from that time, the Seventh Prince never showed weakness or sadness again.

Toughness and coldness may not always be innate, it may simply be that there is no more retreat and dependence, only to become the dependence of others themselves.

Zhang Si is still recalling his past: "I was born to steal, steal for a year, did not let the government to catch, and even alarmed the world outside the high man passing through the township. That high man arrested me for the people's sake, and was going to hand me over to the government and ask why I stole, but the high man was moved with compassion and even took me as a disciple, it's just a pity that I still let him down, and my mother is still dead, but a scourge like me is still alive."

"You're no scourge." Xue Yao said unhappily, "Zhang Si, you've promised to make me a friend, I'm a person who only befriends heroes and good men, and I don't scrutinize the past, so you have to cherish me as a friend, and be a good hero and good man for the rest of your life."

Zhang Si grinned, "Success, I will do my best to keep your friend."

When they arrived at Ping Rong County, the boundless rice paddies rushed Xue Yao to peace of mind.

The fact that the crops have not been neglected indicates that the epidemic is not yet serious, and that the seventy or so people infected with the plague should not have been misrepresented.

Along with a few imperial physicians and famous doctors from the capital, they rushed to the landing place.

It was a mansion specializing in receiving central government officials, with a four-entry courtyard with a garden, and was treated quite well.

Xue Yao asked the concierge, "Are there any patients attending the residence?"

The concierge replied back, "The doctors all visit the house to see the patients, there were two original doctors in the mansion who contracted the disease, one passed away half a month ago and was sent back to his hometown, and the other has already left Ping Rong."

Xue Yao nodded and took Zhang Si to live in the third courtyard room, and had his entourage bring disinfectant water to pour over the rooms where the patients had lived, and open the doors and windows to ventilate the room.

Such a move made the accompanying doctor very puzzled, there was no concept of viruses in ancient times, Xue Yao's inexplicable actions, in the eyes of the onlookers, were instead superstitious and odd actions.

The next day, it was even more incomprehensible when he wore a protective mask and went out with Drs.

This scientific safety measure is seen by other docs as disrespectful to the patient.

Xue Yao had anticipated this situation before he arrived, but there was no way to explain to the ancients that a virus that required a microscope to see could be transmitted through the air, so he could only act as an alien.

After two days of clinical observation, Xue Yao felt that the patient's symptoms were somewhat similar to those of a plague described by Liu Shangyou during the Chongzhen period in historical records.

It is not uncommon for patients to have symptoms of coughing up blood, but symptoms of lymphoid masses in the groin and armpits make adenomatous plague likely.

It was literally the beginning of a holocaust.

Xue Yao left the patient's residence in a state of shock and immediately took out his homemade mixed fatty acid soap and demanded that the doctor who had come into contact with the patient wash his hands before washing his face.

The eunuchs and famous doctors were very disdainful of Xue Yao's odd demeanor and demands, but since he was a member of the Crown Prince's faction, it was not easy for everyone to say anything, so they all silently and obediently washed their hands with soap.

After the wash, the emergency session began.

"This symptom has never been recorded in history books." The head eunuch spoke first: "Judging from the pulse, it is a yin condition, prescribing a dose of pungent, warm, and great heat to regulate it should be able to save her life for the time being."

Several of the doctors stroked their beards and voiced additional opinions.

Xue Yao patiently listened to the crowd before respectfully reminding, "Your Excellency, this disease is different from ordinary yin disease, the patient's family takes turns to be infected, this is a sign of a pandemic, I thought it would be best to send all the infected people en masse to a remote suburb and isolate them for treatment, so as to avoid spreading the disease to more innocent people."

The surrounding healers looked at each other, obviously somewhat resistant to this oddly behaving layman.

The head eunuch said, "Even if it's an epidemic, as long as there's no direct contact, it won't be infected, if Mr. Xue isn't assured, he doesn't have to accompany us to the clinic in the future. Practicing medicine and treating the sick is our duty as healers, there is no reason to arrest the patients and throw them into the wild."

Xue Yao's heart thumped, feeling like it was going to end.

In the case of plague, there is currently no technical way to extract a vaccine.

Luckily, this county is not densely populated, otherwise the plague would have been out of control.

For this plague, the only recipe that could be made in historical works was Wu Xuan Chong's Law of Curing Bubonic Plague, but the utility of his recipe was very limited, and the methods of prevention and avoidance recorded in the book were quite feasible.

As far as current technology is concerned, isolating the patient is the most effective method.
