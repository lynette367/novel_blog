Xue Yao didn't mind His Highness learning one more "craft", but only: "Can His Highness learn from two masters?"

The Sword Saint raised an eyebrow and said, "It doesn't matter, I'm only afraid that Lu Qian won't be able to eat the two schools of martial arts, the foundation of the sword and stick is very different, with little overlap, and the bitterness of the two portions isn't something a normal person can withstand."

Sect Master Xu said, "It doesn't matter to me, it's just cheap for you kid."

Xue Yao worried, "Will the sword and stick be mutually exclusive? Will practicing the stick affect Your Highness's sword skills?"

The Sword Saint explained, "No, I'm just afraid that I don't have enough energy to learn the art."

Xue Yao worriedly said, "Then how did your hair turn gray at such a young age?"

"You kid is simply looking for a fight!" Sect Master Xu stormed out, waving his cane angrily, "The old man is ninety-seven years old at the age of his teeth! It's hard not to still have a head of black hair!"

"You are ninety-seven years old!" Xue Yao looked at the old man's thirty or forty year old face in shock!

"Hmph." Sect Master Xu raised his condescending head, "How about it? Can't see it, can you?"

The Sword Saint's decade-old demolition of his old nemesis: "It looks like ninety-six at the most."

"Your Highness!" Xue Yao turned his head to look at Lu Qian, "Quickly worship this great master!"

Lu Qian froze, hesitantly and a bit disgustedly looking at Sect Master Xu.

"Please, Benefactor, accept a bow from my apprentice!" Xue Yao raised his hand and pressed the back of the little chubby cub's head, forcing the little chubby cub to give that great master a bow.

This was great, the fat cub had one more master teacher than in his last life!

Practicing swordsmanship just happens to draw out the excess yang yuan in His Highness's body, and in this life, if he practices another yang yuan-absorbing Green Dragon Stick internal martial art, will the excess yang yuan in His Highness's body have nowhere to be vented?

The ship docked, Xue Yao no longer think nonsense, focusing on the five pups full whiskers and tails to change back.

Originally, they were worried that the Khitans would resort to fraud, but now that there were two martial arts masters accompanying them in the hostage exchange, there was no need to worry too much.

The two tall men had planned to steal back or snatch back the fifth prince by force, but now that there was an exchange of hostages, it was more economical to take a few children straight into the enemy camp, that's how tough it was.

The remnants of the Khitan army had long since safely withdrawn from the battlefield, and the only ones left on the riverbank waiting for the exchange of hostages were the remnants of two battalions of Khitan soldiers and a general.

The first messenger sent by the Khitan to the Qi army camp has not yet been heard from, and the second messenger went across the river just two days ago and has not yet returned either.

Originally thought that the Qi army has abandoned their fifth prince, broken the promise, killed their only remaining prince, I did not expect, this day actually waited for a group of "old and weak soldiers" to send back their Khitan prince.

Because of the complete defeat, the Khitan generals no longer had the heart to provoke, and seeing the prince return unharmed, without saying a word, they took the group of Han people to see their fifth prince.

Walking outside the tent where the Fifth Prince was being held, Xue Yao heard the Fifth Prince's poetry recitation coming from the tent through the heavy felt cloth-

"A glass of turbid wine is a million miles away from home, and there is no plan for Yan Ran to return ......"

"Lift up your eyes and look up to the sky with a long and magnificent cry. Thirty miles of fame and dust and earth, eight thousand miles of clouds and moon ......"

"You guys hurry up and take him away." The Khitan general urged Xue Yao and the others with his eyes.

"How could the Fifth Prince's voice be so hoarse!" Xue Yao protested angrily, "You mistreated the hostages!"

The Khitan general was speechless, "Try chattering for eight hours a day, anyone's voice would be muffled."

Xue Yao was speechless, although his family's five cubs talked more, this was an enemy camp, it was impossible for the fifth prince to talk so much with the enemy, how could he chatter on and on for eight hours a day?

As a matter of fact, the Fifth Prince really couldn't find anyone to chat with in this military camp.

Guessing that his father was planning to sacrifice himself and sever the bloodline of the Khitan royal family, the Fifth Prince lived each day as if it were the moment before the sacrifice, roaring some impassioned last words whenever he could.

One is to give himself courage, and the other is to leave the enemy with the image of his Daqi royal family's valor.

Unexpectedly, half a month had passed, the Khitans still did not make a move, the Fifth Prince roared until his voice was muffled, fortunately Xue Yao arrived in time to avoid embarrassment.

When he lifted the tent curtain, Xue Yao almost didn't recognize the brown man sitting cross-legged on the carpet with closed eyes and a bearded face.

"Live as a man, die also ......" The fifth prince heard someone lifting the curtain to come in, still closed his eyes, recited the last words of the generous sacrifice.

"Fifth brother?" Lu Qian was the first to recognize his own brother.

The Fifth Prince froze, closed his eyes and was quiet for a while, then let out a bitter laugh, "Is it another hallucination? Seventh brother, if you can hear, take care of Princess Hui for Fifth Brother, in the future, Fifth Brother can no longer ...... well!"

It really is Chitti's voice!

Chitti was thinner than he remembered, and his profile looked much more heroic than before.

"Chitti! It's really you!" The Fifth Prince jumped up violently and grabbed onto Older Seventh's arm, "Are you here to take Fifth Brother away?"

"No. Master came to visit his family, take a look and leave." The Seventh Prince had a cold face.

The Fifth Prince looked at his brother blankly.

"But he can't even memorize Fifth Brother's favorite, Full River Red." Lu Qian looked at Fifth Brother, "As for Princess Hui, Brother should keep it for his own care."

Xue Yao stepped forward and straightened the Fifth Prince's messy hair, tearfully whispering, "We should go back, Fifth Highness, everyone is waiting for you."

Facing death at any moment for more than half a month, the Fifth Prince was brave for more than half a month, but at the end of this valor, wow, he cried out.

With both arms in one hand, he wrapped his arms around Xue Yao and Seventh Brother, whimpering and complaining, "What took you guys so long to get here!"

"You guys just brought two old men here to replace me?" The Fifth Prince was incredulous.

Master Xu: "I can't thank you enough!"

"Sword Saint?!" The Fifth Prince, selectively invisible to Sect Leader Xu like many mortals, turned to the Sword Saint and marveled, "You really are Senior Sword Saint!"

The Sword Saint humbly shook his head and sighed, "It's just a false name, you are Lu Qian's elder brother? Just now, I heard you recite with resounding words, truly worthy of my Da Qi's good men with iron bones."

The Fifth Prince knew that his chance to perform had come, and in order to leave a good impression on the Sword Saint, he immediately cleared his throat, put on a deep air, raised his hand and tapped the shoulder of the seventh brother beside him, and spoke in a subdued manner, "My brother once asked me: fifth brother, do you think that your life is more important or the honor of your family and country? I told him at the time--"

The Fifth Prince frowned and said, "He may have contracted a wind-cold, and will have to step up his efforts to return to the palace to recuperate."

Who did the little fat boy learn to provoke with such ferocity? Is it those crooks?
