Xue Yao straightened up, leading the Seventh Prince in one hand and the Sixth Prince in the other to keep up with the Fifth Prince's footsteps.

He's not talkative and doesn't initiate much interaction with people.

If someone asks him to do something he doesn't want to do, he will always say "I'm so tired".

Such a sense of indifference and detachment made Xue Yao not dare to send out the big move of "Wan Chai Milk".

On a hot day, Xue Yao felt that the north wind was passing through and it was penetratingly cold.

Is this little guy a loner by nature, or is he holding a grudge?

Helpless, Xue Yao bowed and prepared to leave.

"Second Prince Xue stayed back." A middle-aged eunuch stepped forward and smiled, "Concubine Shio invites you to enter the palace to catch up."

Xue Yao went to the Green Vault Hall and had his meal with Consort Shi.

Xue Yao suspiciously looked at Consort Shi and tried, "Your Highness doesn't seem to be too fond of making small talk with people."

Concubine Shio frowned at his words, sighed with a lost expression, slowly nodded her head, and whispered, "You've noticed it too? This child's temperament is a bit odd, other children are mostly not like this when they are three years old."

Xue Yao wondered, "His Highness is just not very talkative, there's nothing odd about it, there's no need for Your Highness to worry."

Princess Shio shook her head, "It seems you haven't realized it yet."

Xue Yao shuffled suspiciously.

Extraordinary memory.

Math talent and creativity.
