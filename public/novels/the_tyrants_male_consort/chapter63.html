In her haste, the Crown Princess's handkerchief shook off, and was about to grab it, when a gust of wind blew and told her to raise her hand and grab it in the air.

The paddle was tucked back into her hand, and the Crown Prince whispered to her, "Don't worry."

The Dauphin froze, violently, and she didn't know what else she wasn't comfortable with.

Her beloved man had returned safely to the capital, and she had long been relieved.

The crown prince was amused by her clueless appearance and moved to her ear, whispering, "Lone will not touch other women."

The man was already making her deer in headlights just by looking at him from afar.

Before the age of eight, Lu Jinan had also seen his father and mother who were like glue, and had also felt almost all of his father's love.

Until Hua Guifei appeared and the Sixth Prince was born.

Father had taken all of his limited energy from him and Mother and moved it to Hua Guifei's palace.

The young Lu Jinan once accompanied the Empress staring out the window of the palace door day after day, looking forward to the Emperor down the dynasty, to the Palace of the Reserve Show to change into a bright yellow and silver and white regular clothes, with their mother and son playful talk and laugh.

The fault is not with the Hua Guifei who took away Father, the fault is with Father himself.

Lu Jinan didn't want to repeat the mistakes made by his father.

The image of the prince and his wife being sent off like glue, looking at Xue Yao could not help but show the jealous look of a single dog.

In his last life, because he was abandoned by his parents and had to serve dishes to earn rent in addition to working and studying, Xue Yao felt that he was in too poor a condition to fall in love and wreak havoc on little girls.

He was thinking of graduating from a prestigious school and flying high, and then starting a sweet relationship, but he didn't wait until he graduated, and then he was in a plane crash.

How tragic!

However, only seventeen years old in this life, he was rewarded by the Empress with one thousand taels of silver, with a purchasing power equivalent to more than six hundred thousand dollars in bonuses.

The future is bright and he must find a ......

Have two lifetimes of singles been so unproductive that they are afraid to envision little girls!

Xue Yao felt that his mental state was dangerous, and he had to go back and reflect on it!

Originally, bidding farewell to the Crown Prince's Palace, Xue Yao was going to return to the Green Vault Palace to coax the little fat cub, but the Empress rewarded her with a bunch of things, and the Crown Prince arranged for a carriage to send Xue Yao out of the palace and back to her home.

Xue Yao could only follow the carriages and horses out of the palace first, waiting for tomorrow to go to the academy before coaxing the fat cub.

Xue Yao rummaged through some of the specialty crafts that he had bought back from Jinling City to give to his mother, and the one thousand taels of bounty silver that he had asked Zhou Rui to save for himself.

But Zhou Rui felt that her son had grown up, and after leaving the Xue Mansion, she had been without a husband to be the head of the household, and she wanted Xue Yao to set up an account in a money bank on his own, to be a small head of the household, and to manage his own belongings.

The Fifth Prince and the Seventh Prince took a step back and walked into the schoolhouse.

The Fifth Prince had spent the entire journey making excuses for his swordsmanship blunder the day before, hoping that the Seventh Brother would understand that his brother's strengths did not lie here.

"Touch it here." The Sixth Prince pointed to a spot of soft fur on the rabbit's head.

Xue Yao carefully gently touched it with his index finger, and the rabbit immediately squinted comfortably.

"It's very comfortable to touch the fur here, and when it's nervous, it behaves when you touch it like this." The Sixth Prince laughed.

"So divine?" Xue Yao was incredulous.

The two hadn't felt the sharp-edged gaze of a certain Dragon Ao Tian behind them.

Not a sword nor a bow, but a pair of Star Shattering Hammers, a hammer to swing over and directly smash the opponent's head!"

"Is this appropriate? He is after all Master's sixth brother." The Seventh Prince narrowed his eyes and stared at the back of the Sixth Prince's head, leaping to reply.

"I meant to smash my opponent's head, I didn't mean to smash Sixth Brother's head!" The Fifth Prince was not pleased, "Did you listen to your brother seriously!"

"Your Highness!" Xue Yao's eyes lit up as he picked up the rabbit and ran to the little chubby cub, gently stroking the rabbit's head and showing off, "Look, how well-behaved is this little guy? He especially listens to me."

It's well bred, isn't it? That day I saw that its fur was half bald, and it was cornered and bullied by a black rabbit, and thought for sure that it couldn't be raised, but I didn't expect that its fur would grow back now."

Done.

"There are only a few handfuls of fur left, and when it blows, it's all bald." The confusion of persona caused by self-perception flaws would seriously affect the majesty of the Dragon Pride.

Otherwise the Third Prince will be in power, and you little fat kid will still be bullied every day!

Although he never expected the Seventh Prince to give him the same level of care and response, but when he thought that he was running around for the little fat cub and even pressed his life, but he couldn't even get the most basic understanding, his heart was still more or less a bit aggrieved.
