Two soldiers immediately jumped onto Xue Yao's boat and drew their swords against his neck.

"How dare you!" Xue Yao boldly threatened the soldiers, "If you dare to touch me, the Seventh Prince will never forgive you lightly!"

The soldier on the other boat looked up in horror - the white-haired figure in mid-air came flying in!

They had no time to draw their swords to fend them off before they were knocked into the water by a swimming dragon like cane.

The man in white landed without a sound, and the seventh prince shook the ship when he landed.

Xue Yao couldn't tell if what was in front of him was real or not, and he couldn't even speak as he looked motionlessly at the little fat kid a few steps away.

Lu Qian couldn't wait for the little companion to rush over for a hug, so he took the initiative to walk over, taking one step with the boat shaking, until he reached the little companion, "Yao Yao, how come you haven't returned to the capital?"
