These wrestling, arm-wrestling and other competitions, most of them are picked by the Fifth Prince, this child seems to have endless energy that needs to be radiated outward.

Xue Yao usually doesn't want Little Fatty to participate in this kind of "fighting and brawling" activities, for fear that Little Fatty's "war-loving gene" will be stimulated.

Although he was compelled to participate today, he still didn't want the little fat cub to fight someone else.

Xue Yao decided to go on by himself, for one thing, it would ensure that the Seventh Prince would not suffer any other traumatic injuries, and for another, kicking the acupoints would not need to rely on luck.

However--

"Step forward, Sixth Brother." The Seventh Prince had already chosen his own opponent.

"Your Highness fight me first!" Xue Yao blocked in front of the Seventh Prince, not dying to fight.

The Seventh Prince raised his hand and pointed to the group of onlookers to the west and said to Xue Yao, "Go stand still, don't blink, and watch as Master wins back-to-back battles."

Xue Yao: "......"

Who the hell gave this little fat kid confidence?

The old man is going to show his skills today and teach his cub a lesson in humility!

"Your Highness refuses to fight me, it can't be because you're afraid, right?" Xue Yao narrowed his eyes provocatively.

The Seventh Prince laughed softly as if he heard a joke, walked around Xue Yao, towards the center of the crowd, and gave a perfunctory response, "Yes, yes, yes, I'm afraid of you."

Xue Yao: "......"

Must be usually too gentle with the fat cubs, time to show real strength today!

"Yo, seventh brother also want to show his hand today?" The fifth prince just finished three games, rolled up his sleeves and came over with a sweaty head, pointing to a group of guards on the opposite side: "Pick a rival first, and first of all, don't cry if you fall, and don't lead your big brother over."

"To Sixth Brother." The Seventh Prince had a clear goal.

"Your sixth brother didn't attend! There with big brother to discuss things, see?" The fifth prince explained seriously, "This competition has rules, the first round of the competition, you must first pass the guards that pass, can fight and win, in order to pick their own opponents over the moves."

Luk didn't attend?

The Seventh Prince turned his head to look at Xue Yao.

Xue Yao smiled and made excuses, "Ah, I was wrong just now, it was the Fifth Prince who won several rounds and was particularly powerful."

"Ai--" the Fifth Prince pretended to be modest, "It's a small thing!"

The Seventh Prince's eyes immediately became indifferent, it was good that it wasn't Sixth Brother who was in the limelight.

However, adhering to the traditional cultural thinking of "all have come", the little fat cub still pointed to a random guard, and with a wave of his hand, he was ready to play a little bit in front of the silly Yao Yao.

Xue Yao heard the Fifth Prince say that there is a rule of "first pass the guards", it is not good to disobey, can only hope that the selected guards.

Before the start of the match, Xue Yao purposely walked to the side of the guard and whispered, "In a moment, you kick an inch below the umbilicus of His Highness, and His Highness will admit defeat, His Highness has more meat on his belly than he would like, so don't kick the wrong place."

The guards returned in fear, "I wouldn't dare!"

"What's there to not dare? If you deliberately let the water down, the Seventh Prince will get angry." Xue Yao bluffed, "In front of the Fifth Prince, you can't beat a kid, whether you're really incapable or you're deliberately letting off steam, will you have a chance to be reused in the future? The fifth prince's nature you and I are clear, if you are afraid, then it is not a person inside and outside, the seventh prince will not appreciate your love, on the contrary, look down on you, you have to kick in the weakness of his highness, not only can not hurt people, but also let the seventh prince admire you, you think clearly, try your best to perform, his highness will not be short of you in a while."

The guard is a rough man, heart and mind not Xue Yao more, was a fool, the heart really think it makes sense, imposing on the field, eyes firmly Xue Yao said "the seventh prince's weakness" position.

The Seventh Prince watched Xue Yao mutter to this guard for a while, thinking that he was reminding the guard to release the water.

It didn't matter, anyway, in a while, Fifth Brother would definitely not let go of the water, and His Highness still had the wind out of his sails.

After the match started, the Seventh Prince didn't take it very seriously, anyway, Yao Yao let the water go, and with a casual push, the guards would fall to the ground obediently.

As a result, His Highness guessed wrongly at Silly Yao Yao's mind, and struck out several times, but actually didn't touch the body of the guard.

Instead, the guards looked like they were going into battle to kill the enemy, their legs were powerful, constantly sweeping and flying kicks, forcing the Seventh Prince to retreat back and forth, almost retreating into the crowd.

A group of onlookers' attendants hurriedly backed away to make way for the field.

This made the Seventh Prince lose face.

His Highness's baby-fat little face sank, his eyelids narrowed, and only the arrogant figure of the guards remained in the light-colored pupils of his eyes.

The guard saw the little prince standing motionless, worried that his kick was too hard, wanted to withdraw his momentum, but it was already too late, and watched as he kicked his own foot towards the prince, filled with fear.

However, just a moment before the impact occurred, the young prince suddenly shortened his body and leaped, curling his leg and lifting his knee, and in the process, stepped on the leg that the guard had kicked out, and utilized the force to stomp-

In an instant, the guard only felt a flash in front of his eyes, the young prince had already leaned in front of him, raised his left arm, and slammed an elbow down towards the side of his neck, probably for fear of hurting someone's life, and then halfway reversed his direction, hitting the guard on his left shoulder.

With a muffled "thud", the guard was slammed to the ground as if he had been hit by a hammer, and his buttocks went numb from being slammed down from mid-air.

The squires, who had gathered around to raise a ruckus, were quiet for a moment.

It was probably because he was shocked by the Seventh Prince who had first shown his hand.

Or maybe it was the first time he'd seen a fat cub with such a lithe stance.

This shocked crowd included Xue Yao.

What just happened?

The one that jumped up over a meter off the ground and took off into the air with one foot on someone else's leg, was that the chubby little cub that he had milked himself?

He's dreaming, right? ......

The task of opening the second chakra in the spirit space was clearly not yet completed, how could the little fat cub have such a body?

It doesn't make sense!

However, this unreasonable event has actually happened.

The Fifth Prince was the first to return to his senses and went forward to help the guard on the ground.

Xue Yao followed back to God, rushed forward to ask the guard if he was hurt anywhere, pacified for a while, and took out a small silver ingot, rewarding the guard.

Although he hadn't kicked the little chubby cub, the guard had clearly done his best and deserved a reward.

The guards thanked the reward and retired.

The Fifth Prince was still standing in place with a confused face, looking at his seventh brother and then incredulously at Xue Yao, as if asking Xue Yao, "Was my brother dropped by you?"

Xue Yao looked at the Fifth Prince with a "I didn't expect that either" look, but he was dragged over by the Seventh Prince and held back.

The Seventh Prince didn't like it when people stared at Silly Yao Yao like that, it always felt like they were here to compete for milk, so he reminded Fifth Brother with a look of indifference, "Yao Yao won't reward you."

Fifth Prince: "......"

He wasn't standing around stupidly waiting for a reward okay!

The onlooking squires gradually recovered from their shock and snapped out a cheer.

The roar was so loud that it drew the Third Prince out of the tent.

After letting the boy go to inquire about the situation and learning that the Seventh Prince had just made a big show of himself, the Third Prince burst into laughter and swaggered into the crowd.

"How can you not call me for a match?" The third prince raised his chin and walked into the martial arts field with his hands behind his back, looking mockingly at the seventh prince who had just made a splash, raising his eyebrows and saying, "Third brother came late and didn't see his younger brother's prowess, so why don't you and I have a match, and ask third brother to see how much you've grown over the years."

The Seventh Prince's face was expressionless and didn't answer, turning his head to look at Fifth Brother with a pleading gaze.

Disgusted with third brother, but did not dare to confront him head on, the shadow of his youth made the seventh prince only want to avoid it.

"Which is no growth." The fifth prince blocked in front of the seventh prince and said to the third prince with a smile, "I'm letting everyone get up and encourage the seventh brother, just fooling around! Third brother's strength is too great, seventh brother is still young, it would be bad if I miss and hurt him, why don't I accompany third brother to go through two moves."

"What's wrong with being strong? Can't I just put it away?" The Third Prince glared, "Get out of the way."

The Seventh Prince lowered his head and lowered his eyes, looking for a chance to sneak a slanting glance at Xue Yao.

Of course, Xue Yao understood that the Seventh Prince wanted him to sneak off and find his big brother to come over and relieve him.

If it were the usual, where else would the little fat kid need to make a wink? Xue Yao would have dashed off to find the Grand Prince to complain.

But here and now.

The Third Prince, the villain who had opened the Seventh Prince's veins in the original story, descended before Xue Yao like timely rain.

The opportunity is not to be missed!

Xue Yao purposely ignored the little fat cub's pleading eyes and stared at the Third Prince with a grave expression, unable to take his eyes off of him.

The third prince also knew that someone might go to his elder brother to report, beating his younger brother had to scramble and see what was going on, so he stopped nagging and pushed the fifth prince away, just to do it!

The gathered squires were silent at the moment.

The Seventh Prince was unable to avoid it, and rushed his duck to start a duel with the Third Prince.

In fact, according to the stance that the little fat cub had just displayed, it seemed that if he really fought, he might not lose to the Third Prince.

Xue Yao was worried that after missing the best time at the age of seven or eight, the Third Prince at this moment might not be a match for the little chubby cub anymore.

However, after the match began, the Seventh Prince did not take the initiative to attack, but dodged around, and there was little expression on his face, as if he was waiting for the Third Prince to get out of breath enough to leave on his own.

Xue Yao observed for a long time and realized that although the Third Prince had a lot of force and the air was whistled by the wind of his palm and leg, his movement and reaction power was a great deal worse than that of the Seventh Prince, and it was obviously impossible to hit someone.

The atmosphere around them grew awkwardly silent.

The Third Prince's expression was also getting angrier and angrier.

It's simply fruitless to go on like this.

Xue Yao was worried that the Third Prince would become infuriated because he couldn't hit anyone and start targeting the Seventh Prince again down the road, so he simply gave up on this opportunity to complete the mission and silently turned around and squeezed out of the crowd, wanting to go find the Great Prince for help.

Unexpectedly, the Third Prince turned his head at this exact moment, wanting to order the boy to fetch a bowl of water to quench his thirst, and just happened to see Xue Yao squeezing his way out.

It wasn't the first time he saw Xue Yao going to get help, the Third Prince instantly became furious and rushed over in three steps, lifting his hand and yanking Xue Yao by the back of his neck, shouting, "Where are you going? Bastard! Come and fight with me too!"

Xue Yao was lifted up by the Third Prince and thrown into the middle of the field, and as soon as he looked up, he saw the Third Prince swing over with a slap!

Xue Yao didn't want to provoke the Third Prince, so he had to frown and close his eyes, intending to carry on with his head, but the slap never landed on his body.

When he opened his eyes, he actually found the Seventh Prince with his back straight, blocking in front of him, raising his left hand and blocking that palm of the Third Prince.

The Third Prince was first stunned, not expecting this little bastard Old Seven to take the initiative!

Instantly overjoyed, the Third Prince jerked out his arm, stomped his legs and leapt up, putting all his strength into a kick towards the little bastard.

The distance was too close, the reaction time was too short, and there was still no fighting spirit in the eyes of the Seventh Prince to counterattack.

In a flash of lightning, only two thoughts popped into His Highness's head - dodge, silly Yao Yao was still behind.

In the end, the Third Prince kicked the Seventh Prince solidly in the abdomen, and together with Xue Yao behind him, they flew out and fell to the ground in a smothered heap.

Across the chubby cub's solid body, Xue Yao seemed to feel that kick slamming hard into his heart, and the moment he fell to the ground, his ears were filled with sharp tinnitus.

The kick was far, far heavier than expected, and it kicked the little chubby cub, kicking Xue Yao out of his mind.

Not caring about his broken bones, Xue Yao struggled to get up and went to look at the fat cub's injuries.

The tinnitus gradually subsided, the surrounding panicked cries were all inaudible, Xue Yao's eyes were filled with the little fat cub's pale and helpless face, and in his ears there was only the chaotic sound of breathing.

The Fifth Prince let out a growl.

The Third Prince, still smiling smugly, narrowed his eyes and stared at the Fifth Prince, who was tugging at his front lapel, and taunted, "What do you want?"

The fifth prince's corner of the eye twitched, holding back his anger mute voice: "Third brother this next is too little regard for brotherly love, if the seventh brother is kicked out of a good ......"

"I am also impatient in nature, Old Seven jumping around and hiding all the time, calling me annoyed." The Third Prince smiled with a winning face, "If he wants to fight me in a regular manner, I won't get real, don't you think so?"

Looking at the Third Prince's guiltless face, the trembling in the corners of the Fifth Prince's eyes gradually stopped, and his gaze became desperate and sad-

"Bang!"

Caught off guard, the Third Prince was smashed on the bridge of his nose by his fifth brother's fist and planted on the ground.
