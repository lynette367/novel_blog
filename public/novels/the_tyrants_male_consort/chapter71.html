Overall, this is a weapon born for the battlefield, few people go out to carry such a long lance shopping, not as good as the sword looks dashing and handsome.

The Fifth Prince naturally did not want to choose the horse lance.

"Horse lance is the most suitable weapon for you." Zhang Si pulled up the horse lance and took a step back, juggling it in front of the Fifth Prince, dodging and moving around, explaining for the Fifth Prince: "The horse lance is not just a weapon for charging into battle, its length allows you to be in a safe range at all times, and it is easier to see your opponent's movements.

His Highness's greatest weakness was that he was so eager to deal a fatal blow to his opponent in melee combat that he ignored his opponent's sneak attack.

I know you're trying to make quick work of the double hammer, but in reality your failure is not in the lack of power of the weapon, but in the habitual blind spot of melee combat.

The Fifth Prince carefully recalled every time he had fought someone and suddenly realized that Zhang Si was right.

Keeping his distance during sparring, he was often on top, and once he shortened the distance to the moment of engagement, if he couldn't win with a single strike, he could easily be counter-pressured.

Previously, he had always thought that it was due to the lack of weapon power, making it difficult to suppress the enemy at critical moments, and after listening to Zhang Si's analysis, he realized that his true weakness was his ultra melee combat blind spot.

"You have a point." The fifth prince said, "But I ...... still don't like the horse lance very much, can I switch to other weapons by correcting my mistakes during melee combat ......"

"Your Highness." Zhang Si interrupted him and spoke directly, "I have already said that the most important thing is to find the right direction for your talent first, your weaknesses can of course be corrected by practicing diligently, but when you encounter an opponent who is extremely good at getting close to you, weaknesses will always remain weaknesses, and you have to use your best instincts to put yourself as much as possible at an advantage, in order to be able to survive a hundred battles. "

The Fifth Prince had no way to refute this, and could only forcefully nod his head, reaching out with the intention of taking the horse lance in Zhang Si's hand.

Zhang Si, however, suddenly withdrew his hand and did not hand him the horse lance.

The Fifth Prince looked up at him suspiciously.

"You have to love your weapon from the bottom of your heart if you have set your goal." Zhang Si said sternly, "You don't like the horse lance now, then practice the double hammer and horse lance together, and after a month, use the double hammer and the horse lance to fight against your Royal Instructor separately, then come back and tell me your choice, sincerely."

The Fifth Prince slowly lowered his head, and after a long time, spoke in a slightly hoarse voice: "It's not that I don't like the horse lance, but I don't like a person who once used it to build a career."

Zhang Si froze, staring at this child who looked like the emperor, for a long time, and spoke in a low voice: "The general who built a career with a horse lance, there is no one else in Daqi except for old General Qin, his old man is your maternal grandfather, don't you like him?"

The Fifth Prince nodded and raised his head to look at Zhang Si, "You know my maternal grandfather?"

Zhang Si smiled bitterly and looked at the Fifth Prince with a vicissitude in his gaze, "Before your origin, for a whole decade, I was in the general's residence to make a living, so how could I not recognize Old General Qin."

The small garden suddenly became unusually quiet.

The white clouds in the sky gradually dispersed, and the obscured sun enveloped the garden in a blazing light.

It was like the blazing anger in the eyes of the Fifth Prince.

"I hate him," the Fifth Prince spoke through clenched teeth, "He's the one who made my mother enter the palace to be selected in place of Second Aunt."

Zhang Si's eyes widened in surprise and spoke in a low voice, "Consort Hui's entry into the palace is an honor for the general's residence, and as you are the prince, shouldn't you be proud of this?"

The fifth prince lowered his head, his voice low and inaudible as he replied, "Never. I want to ride the frontiers and build a career like my maternal grandfather, this palace ......"

His green fists pinched and cackled as he steeply looked up at Zhang Si, red-eyed and sad, "This palace is my mother's cage, and it's also my cage!"

Xue Yao and the Sixth Prince were both stunned.
