For Lu Qian, the people who need to be protected in this world are divided into those who are in his world, such as his little companion, his father and mother, his elder brother, and the law-abiding common people of Daqi outside his world.

Anything beyond that is destabilizing.

Only those in Lu's submerged world could touch his less acute emotional perceptions, and the unstable elements that threatened his world were all wanted criminals of the first degree, and had to be rooted out as much as possible.

Because of his flawed emotional perception, Lu Qian does not get sentimental over the execution of criminals, just as a tiger does not feel sad over a wild deer in its sharp claws.

"Your Highness goes to Jiangnan." Lu Qian was calm.

"Yao Yao is not here, they can't catch up with Master."

There was a silence.

The panic and nagging in Xue Yao s heart all subsided, and the rebel pup was really able to convert all of his emotions into ...... anger!

Lu Qian sensed something was wrong, the young companion seemed to be angry, but wasn't sure what it was about.

The brain circuits are separated by a rift.

His Highness flinched his head at the snarling little chaperone and murmured with an aggrieved bow of his head, "Fine, no more children then."

Xue Yao gazed at Lu Qian in silence with anger that had nowhere to be vented.

"Go back to the palace."
