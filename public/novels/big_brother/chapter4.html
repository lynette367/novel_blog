This little guy was indeed a child who had been abducted and sold - Wei Qian had a hard heart and a poisonous eye, and he had seen it correctly.

He was stolen when he was too young to remember the ins and outs very well, and the traffickers kept him for a few months before reselling him to a very remote farmer's home.

That's okay, a son to anyone is a son, and he enjoyed being an only child for two years.

Who knows that the third year, his adopted mother, who was determined by the village doctor to be infertile, miraculously became pregnant, and after another year, she gave birth to a healthy, fat boy.

From that point on, the boy became redundant in his adoptive parents' home, and his days followed each other.

While washing dishes in the cold well water that day, the boy accidentally broke a bowl because his fingers were numb from the cold, offending his adoptive father, who had returned home very drunk.

His foster father stripped him naked and made him stand in the middle of the dripping yard in the middle of winter.

The boy felt like he was going to give in to the freezing cold, and finally, he made a decision that was a bit shockingly bold for his age - he ran.

The boy stole a few adult clothes, casually put them on his body, and then went out over the wall with the help of a ladder in the night, and he quietly hid in a car that was hauling winter cabbages to the city.

He's been a little stray ever since.

It's a miracle he's survived this long and managed to escape every time, so lucky that he's become half an escape expert.

In the boy's eyes, there seemed to be only two kinds of people in the world, one kind of people who disliked him for being dirty and walked around him from a long distance, and also threw stones at him and hit him, and one kind of people who were kind to him, but in their hearts they actually still wanted to sell him.

Until he met such a unique person.

He had heard people call him "Qian'er" in the characteristic localized voice, a man who had helped him fight off big wild dogs and given him food, but who had always left without saying a word to him.

Of course, more often than not, the man would be oblivious to him.

Both Wei Qian's familiarity and lack of communication made the boy feel safe, while at the same time, his occasional acts of charity made the boy feel a rare hint of warmth.

The little boy had actually been changing places to live, but in order to sneak a peek at this man every day, he had unknowingly been living in this little hutong for months.

Before he could think of the result, it was raining, and the boy had no choice but to duck back into the building.

This heavy rain did not stop until the evening, three fat mom came downstairs at noon to help Xiao Bao heat up the food, saw the little boy huddled in the hallway, she was taken aback, bending down to take a closer look at him, "Yo, whose kid is this?"
