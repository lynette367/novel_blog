Inside the traveling Bentley, Lin Zirui leaned back in his seat, his eyes fell on the simple name on the business card in his hand, and he whispered as if chewing under his breath, "Qiao Wen! In person is rather more interesting than in the newspaper."

"Jeffrey, I really don't understand."

Lin Zi Rui laughed lightly and withdrew his hand, "A frail teenager in the slums became a big shot in Hong Kong City in just a few years, and it seems like he was involved in the deaths of several big shots, so that's really interesting."

Qin Yunfei tried, "He should have a smarter brain because he loves to read."

Lin Zi Rui smiled thoughtfully, "Well, I like smart people, especially good-looking smart people."
