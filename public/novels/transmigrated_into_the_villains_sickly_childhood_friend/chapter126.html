Hong Kong reporters have always been well-informed, Liu Peizhi was just called by the police to investigate, several journalists on the wind, and has been added to the hundreds of episodes of brainfeeding him and Qiao Wen Chen Canaan's grudges and feuds.

Although Qiaowen had only spoken a few words with him, in the eyes of the reporters who had been waiting for a long time, it was undoubtedly a verbal altercation between the two of them outside the police station.

The next morning, Qiao Wen just woke up, the villa doorbell was rung, to open the door of the maid, panicked and led in three uniformed police.

"Mr. Qiao, a couple of Asirs say they have something for you."

Qiao Wen stood up unaccountably, he recognized one of them, the very same inspector who had investigated their case, he frowned and asked, "Li Sir, may I ask is there any progress in the case?"

Li Sir said, "Mr. Qiao, Mr. Liu Peizhi of East China Film, was shot dead last night. According to the clues provided by eyewitnesses, the two of you had a verbal altercation in front of the police station yesterday, but also please go back with us to be investigated."

Jovian looked over at him in shock, thinking he'd heard wrong.

Chen Canaan, whose head was still tied with gauze, even jumped up from the sofa and blocked Qiao Wen behind him, "Ah Sir, what nonsense are you talking about? It was your police who investigated that the mastermind behind killing us might be Liu Pei Zhi, we are still waiting for the results of your investigation, how did it turn into us shooting him?" He pointed to his head, "The wound on my head has not yet healed, can you police still be trusted?"

Lee Sir said with a difficult face, "Mr. Chen Mr. Qiao, because Liu Peizhi died very suddenly, we had to routinely investigate."

Chen Canaan still had to say something, but was stopped by Qiao Wen: "Okay, I'll go with you guys." , Li sir breathed a sigh of relief: "Thank you Mr. Qiao for cooperating."

Chen Canaan was like an enemy, pointing at them, "All of you Sirs, don't give in to the fight." He also said to Qiao Wen, "Xiao Qiao, don't be afraid, I'll go call a lawyer right away and pick you up later."

Seeing him so tense, Li Sir could not help but laugh, "Mr. Chen is serious, we are just a routine investigation."

Jovan goes from victim to suspect, and for the first time in his life, he sits in a police station and is taken there.

Except that when he sits in the interrogation room and is confronted with questions from the police, the answer is almost always no. It's not that he's being deliberately uncooperative, it's just that he's so unprepared for and ignorant of what's been going on lately.

The only thing his mind could recall was what Liu Peizhi said to him yesterday afternoon. Saying that if they moved someone else's cheese, sooner or later someone else would take it back, and also saying that for someone to stomp on them was as simple as stomping on an ant - naturally, he didn't really agree with this statement, as weak as ants were, it wasn't really that simple to stomp on them, as the soles of the shoes tended to have gaps in them.

Of course, he didn't feel that today's himself and Chen Canaan were still as weak as ants.

He had previously thought that the two incidents that had occurred one after another were the work of Liu Peizhi, but now it seemed that even if it was really him, there was definitely a bigger hand behind him, manipulating all of this. And the purpose is to get rid of them.

The question is, whose cheese did they actually move?

After the police questioned him for just over half an hour, Chen Canaan brought his lawyer to pick him up. Because there was no concrete evidence, just like Liu Peizhi yesterday, after asking what needed to be asked, Qiao Wen was let go.

Chen Canaan for Qiao Wen was taken away is very nagging, out of the door, do not forget to obscure towards the door spit: "Fuck, take the taxpayers' money, the case can not be solved, but also wrongly accused a good person, I see sooner or later shut down the matter."

Qiaowen patted his shoulder in amusement, "Forget it Nan, let's go back."

Chen Canaan cursed and followed him.

Back at home, Jovian dismissed the maid and closed his eyes as he leaned back against the couch.

"Little Joe, are you tired? Did the police give you a hard time?"

Qiao Wen shook his head and opened his eyes to look at him. In fact, sometimes he was quite envious of him, as if he didn't worry about anything he encountered, always with the silly optimistic mindset that the sky was falling and there was a taller man on top.

It's like there's no fear or trepidation about anything, and no, there is, like the fear of something happening to you if you get hurt.

When he thought of that day, the scene where he had shielded himself under his body, Qiao Wen still couldn't help but feel his heart palpitating.

When he first came to this world, he felt that this era was just right for him to take a risk, to find his motivation to live again, and that's exactly what he did, slugging it out, speculating, and being sharp, only to have to face danger after danger.

Though he was only in his early twenties, he had, after all, lived two lifetimes that added up to more than thirty years, and for the first time he was finally feeling a little tired. For nowadays the driving force in his life had long since stopped being adventure.

Thinking about this, he held Chen Canaan's hand and shook his head, "The police didn't give me a hard time, I'm just wondering, what have we offended?"

Chen Canaan although big, but in fact, not the brain is not loaded with things, the incident of falling lights has not let him put on the heart, this time the car accident, but can not not be taken seriously.

Now that I think about it, I can't help but be afraid, and I'm glad that Jovan is okay.

Originally, he thought that it was that Liu Peizhi, who was jealous of their company's good development and success in founding the Golden Dragon Awards. But now it seems that no matter if those two incidents were done by Liu Peizhi or not, there is definitely another person behind them who wants to screw them, and he is much more powerful than Liu Peizhi.

He then remembered what Qin Yunfei had said in the martial arts hall that day, and tried, "Xiao Qiao, could it be true that the Fifth Master isn't dead?"

Jovian looked at him and nodded, "That's what I thought."

Chen Canaan frowned, "But besides Zhou Chaozheng, who else is that capable of being the fifth master?"

Qiao Wen pondered for a moment, "Brother Nan, have you ever thought that Master Five might not be alone, Zhou Chaozheng is just one of them." He paused slightly, "Zhou Chaozheng exposing his identity because of us is equivalent to us cutting off the Fifth Master's wealth. When the wind has passed, when everyone has forgotten the name of the Fifth Master, it would make sense for the remaining Fifth Masters to get rid of us."

Chen Canaan was alarmed, "Then aren't we in danger now?"

Jovian pointed to his gauze-bound head, "What do you think?"

Chen Canaan held his head with palpitations, he was not willing to try the feeling of having glass stuck all over his head again.

Qiao Wen said, "What we are most afraid of now is not that they will find someone to kill us, assassination can be defended against, but if we dig a hole for us to jump into in some other way, we may not be able to defend against it. So we have to find them first." After thinking about it, he looked at the man across from him and asked tentatively, "I'm thinking, is it better to find Qin Yunfei to cooperate? He might have a little clue in his hands."

Chen Canaan instantly squirmed and deflated, "I think it's only a pitfall to work with him." After a pause, he added, "Otherwise, we should still let Brother Feng go to him."

Qiao Wen said with amusement, "Don't rely on Brother Feng to be a good talker and look for him for everything, this time it's not a small matter, it's hard for us to protect ourselves, it's not necessary to involve Brother Feng again and make him get involved in the danger as well."

"That's true."

Qiao Wen said, "No matter what, I'll go talk to Qin Yunfei first and see what his attitude is."

Chen Canaan: "I'll join you."

"Don't, you'll probably fight again when you meet."

Chen Canaan thought about it, Qin Yunfei, that bastard, would definitely not speak properly when he saw him.

Although he was a thousand times reluctant to let Qiaowen meet with him alone, he still had to nod, "Then bring a few more of your brothers as bodyguards."

Qiao Wen laughed, "That's for sure, now when I go out for a meal, I have to bring a bodyguard with me, not to mention meeting Qin Yunfei."

Qin Yunfei wasn't hard to find, because Lin Zirui was a princeling who went to work regularly every day to socialize, and his bodyguard's working hours were naturally very regular as well.

Qiao Wen let his little brother guard for two days, then found out his regularity, basically Lin Zi Rui in the company, he is either in the cafe across the Lin's building to drink coffee, or in the car to sleep, but also very regular, it seems to be a bodyguard who has nothing to do at any time on standby.

"Fei!"

Jovian walked over to the booth in the corner of the caf17and sat down across from the man.

Qin Yunfei looked up from the magazine in his hands and pulled the corner of his mouth, revealing a sneer and a cynical look as he said, "Boss Qiao, it's been a long time, don't come back!"

Unimpressed by his flippant attitude, Qiaowen beckoned the waiter, ordered a cup of coffee, and said in a light voice, "It's not bad."

"Is that so?" Qin Yunfei looked him up and down, "But I read in the newspaper that you guys don't seem to be having much luck lately, in that case, why don't you go out less."

Qiao Wen turned his head to look around, the caf17was sparsely populated with guests at this moment, and their table was even in the corner, a very secluded location. He took the coffee brought by the waiter and waited for the people to leave before speaking in a low voice again, "Brother Fei, I won't beat around the bush with you, I'm looking for you because I want to cooperate with you."

Qin Yunfei seemed to have heard some heavenly joke as he let out a low laugh with a giggle, "Ah Wen, you are now a famous big boss in Hong Kong City, a billionaire. I'm just a bodyguard and driver, you're talking about cooperation with someone like me? Don't discount me."

Qiao Wen said, "Don't you want to find Master Five to avenge Master Leopard? Tell me what you know, and we'll help you with this revenge."

Qin Yunfei was stunned at first, then sneered, "Help me take revenge? Ah Wen, I know you're smart, but there's no need to treat me as a fool. You guys got Zhou Chaozheng killed back then, interrupting the fifth master's financial path, they are now looking for you to settle the score, if it wasn't difficult for you to protect yourself, would you come to me for cooperation?"

Jovian said, "It doesn't matter, what matters is that he is our common enemy. You tell me, and I'll figure it out."

Qin Yunfei snorted, "That's right, I want to take revenge, those people manipulate everything behind the scenes based on their power, treating the lives of those of us born poor not as lives. If I want to know who the remaining fifth master is, I would have settled it with one shot earlier, and still be able to sit here reading magazines and drinking coffee, waiting for you to come to me?"

Jovian frowned, "You don't know?"

Qin Yunfei said disdainfully, "Nonsense."

Qiao Wen: "Then how do you know that Master Five is not Zhou Chaozheng alone?"

Qin Yunfei was silent for a moment and said, "After Zhou Chaozheng's death, when I was abroad, I happened to learn that the line of the fifth master in Hong Kong City was still in existence, just that it wasn't active in Hong Kong City for the time being."

Jovian nodded thoughtfully and held out his hand to him, "For what it's worth, I hope you'll work with us."

Qin Yunfei lifted those narrow phoenix eyes and raised his eyebrows, "Ah Wen, it's fine to cooperate with me? But I have conditions."

Jovian: "What are the terms?"

Qin Yunfei reached out and frivolously hooked his hand in his palm: "What do you think? My heart for you has not changed at all over the years, as long as you let me sleep once, any cooperation is fine."

Qiao Wen withdrew his hand and said with a cold expression, "Qin Yunfei, do you think you can still talk to me about such conditions now?"

Qin Yunfei shrugged his shoulders in disbelief, "So don't negotiate with me." He stood up, "I'll avenge myself, no need for you guys to interfere. You'd better burn a few more plants of high incense and don't die at the hands of those people before I find out who they are and finish them off."

After saying that, he pulled out a bill from his pants pocket and placed it on the table, "Boss Qiao, this coffee is on me."
