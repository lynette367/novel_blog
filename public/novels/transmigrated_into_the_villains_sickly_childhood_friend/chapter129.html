Chen Canaan and Qin Yunfei fight this fight is not standard, in addition to a face red and swollen into a pig head, body did not hurt. But the beautiful son of the South is now a public figure, with this face naturally not good to go out and flaunt the city, can only temporarily at home to recuperate.

Recently, the public opinion outside the public opinion in the "five masters resurgence", Qiao Wen also do not want to always be surrounded by reporters, simply let Tang Xingwei work materials sent home, while closing the door to work, while accompanying the Chen Canaan.

After two days, Lin Zi Rui really called to make amends at home.

Qiao Wen originally thought that it wasn't a big deal, and Chen Canaan didn't take it to heart, but the other party's attitude was earnest, so he couldn't refuse.

When the man arrived, he came out and opened the door himself.

"Jeffrey, you're too kind." He laughed when he saw the gift box in Lin Zirui's hand, his eyes glancing at the black Mercedes-Benz parked on the side of the road, the driver in the cab was an unfamiliar face.

"I should, my people made a move on the VIPs at my banquet, no matter what, I as the boss have to be responsible." Lin Zi Rui smiled, catching his gaze, and added, "I suspended Fei from work for a few days to let him reflect."

Jovian nodded approvingly and sidled up to invite him in, casually asking, "Is Fei all right?"

Lin Zi Rui said, "It's fine, it's thanks to Anan's mercy."

Qiao Wen laughed, "He didn't hit him dead, or else Nan would probably be lying in the hospital right now."

Lin Zi Rui smiled and nodded, following him through the front garden, surveying the courtyard and the villa's small building as he walked, and after walking into the front door's entranceway, he sighed, "I heard that the Sea and Sky is Mr. Ouyang's masterpiece that came out of retirement after more than a decade of retirement, and now that I've seen it with my own eyes, the master's handiwork is truly worthy of its name. You guys are indeed capable of hiring him."

Qiao Wen said, "It's just luck, in fact, Mr. Ouyang is very nice to talk to, and is not as arrogant as the rumors."

Lin Zi Rui was noncommittal, and when he saw Chen Canaan, who greeted him with a flowery face, he stepped forward and said with an apologetic face, "Ah Nan, are you alright? I'm really sorry for inviting you to a dinner party, only to have you get injured."

Chen Canaan waved his hand, "It's fine, I actually started it."

Lin Zirui said, "Then it must have been something he said that offended you as well."

"That's true," Chen Canaan nodded, "I can't wait to rip his mouth off."

Jovian laughed softly and shook his head, greeting, "Sit down and take your time."

Chen Canaan to Lin Zi Rui this big brother originally did not have much feeling, but see he even took the initiative to make amends at home to apologize, can not help but give birth to a few good feelings, worthy of the elite education of the broad young man, for the people of the world that is not to be said, do not wait for Qiaowen to open his mouth, has already taken the initiative to order the maid to go to pour the tea.

Lin Zi Rui looked around the house and casually asked, "Are you two the only ones living here, Ah Wen and Ah Nan?"

Jovian said, "The family comes over too, it's kind of a vacation home, but Nan and I stay more."

Lin Zi Rui smiled and nodded, saying, "You're not blood brothers, but your relationship is far better than blood brothers."

Qiao Wen said in a faint voice, "We grew up together, and now that we are working together, our feelings are indeed better than the average brother."

The maid served tea, Lin Zi Rui picked it up, gently sipped it and nodded, "Taihu Lake Dongting Biluochun, good tea."

Qiaowen pretended to be surprised, "Jeffrey lives in Europe all the time, it's amazing that he knows so much about tea."

Lin Zirui laughed, "No matter how long you stay abroad, you can't change your Chinese tastes."

Jovian said, "That's just right, it's almost time for dinner, try how China tastes in our house."

He instructed the maid to cook dinner, and the three of them continued to chat politely. When the maid informed them that dinner was ready, they moved from the sofa to the dining table. The maid who cooked at home was from Shunde, and was a good cook of Cantonese food.

At first, Chen Canaan was eating the next door Su Zhizhi's family, causing him to take a bite of a Zhizhi sister, called not to mention how affectionate, as if the person is treated as a real sister. Qiao Wen was afraid of his disappointment and sadness, and could only hide the truth forever. Afterwards, the family invited people, is to consider his foodie nature, lest one day again eat whoever's delicious, treat people as relatives.

Today's dishes are simple, they are all home made.

Jovian said, "Rough tea, I wonder if it's to Jeffrey's taste?"

Lin Zi Rui chucked a piece of salt-baked chicken and brought it into his mouth, his eyes widening in approval, "Good flavor."

Jovian smiled and nodded, "Jeffrey feel free to eat as much as you want, you're welcome."

Chen Canaan loudly echoed: "Right, three big men, don't make your high society that set of etiquette, openly eat casually."

He peeled two shrimp as he spoke and placed them in Jovian's bowl.

Lin Zi Rui didn't move to watch his movements and laughed, "You guys don't think of me as the same as a curmudgeonly Englishman, I'm actually pretty much the same as you, Anan, just a brown old man."

Chen Canaan lifted his eyelids to look at him, "Is that so? How come I don't feel like it."

"That's because you're not familiar with me yet."

Chen Canaan nodded, "That's true."

Qiao Wen ate the shrimp peeled by Chen Canaan, and motionlessly surveyed the man across the table, as if he suddenly remembered something seemingly, casually asked, "By the way, Jeffrey how did you know Fei?"

Lin Zi Rui said, "About last year, he worked in a Chinese restaurant, when I went to eat I met a thief, he helped me recover my wallet. The law and order over in Europe is very chaotic, rich Chinese young masters like me are often taken as fat sheep, I saw that he was good at fighting, so I hired him to be my bodyguard."

Qiao Wen was a bit puzzled to think that the red stick Qin Yunfei, who had been the rage of the Kowloon Walled City in the beginning, could still go to a Chinese restaurant to fight.

Chen Canaan, on the other hand, laughed outright, "He also worked in a Chinese restaurant? I thought he fled to a foreign country to make a living by setting fires for fewer people as well."

Lin Zi Rui said, "When I met Fei, he was quite down and out, and then I realized that he used to be a Wo Xing Society Red Stick."

Jovian asked, "Has he been following you since?"

Lin Zi Rui nodded, "Well, he's still very competent as a bodyguard, several times when he encountered robberies, he was relied upon to turn the situation into a safe one." Said with a light smile and sighed, "Speaking of him, I have to apologize to you again, Anan, it's me who didn't manage my men well."

Chen Canaan waved his hand without much concern: "He and I already had a beef, and when we met back, we probably would have fought. Did he tell you that I broke his leg?"

Lin Zirui rubbed his nose and nodded, "He did mention it."

Chen Canaan said, "So it's none of your business."

Lin Zi Rui laughed, "I don't care if you guys fight elsewhere, but at my dinner party, I definitely have to be responsible."

Chen Canaan couldn't stand the politeness of this kind of posh gentry and hurriedly tapped the plate with his chopsticks, "Eat your vegetables and eat your vegetables."

Lin Zi Rui seemed to be amused by him, and obediently pinched a few chopsticks of food.

The meal took a good half hour while eating and chatting. Lin Zi Rui didn't stay any longer and said goodbye to the two of them after dinner. Qiao Wen sent him out and returned to the house, seeing Chen Canaan leaning on the sofa with a full stomach, he walked over and asked, "Brother Nan, what do you think of Lin Zi Rui as a person?"

He still hadn't been able to make an accurate judgment of the man after today's meal, but something didn't feel quite right.

Chen Canaan said carelessly, "Looked very good a person, the young master of a big family, and has been studying abroad, gentleman educated, but ......"

"But how?"

Chen Canaan frowned slightly, "I always feel that this person is a bit too nice, I don't see any flaws, it just doesn't feel very real. Of course, it's also mainly because I'm not familiar with him yet."

Jovian nodded thoughtfully, and after a moment, coldly said, "He looks like a friend of mine."

"Friend?" Chen Canaan looked at him curiously, "From your previous world?"

Jovian hmmm'd.

"What friends?"

"It's that good buddy I told you about once." It was just a matter of time, and Jovian added with a pretense of relief, "But I can confirm that it's just a resemblance, and the two aren't related in any way."

When Chen Canaan heard this, he rubbed his head and sat up straight, saying incredulously, "That's the good brother who's no longer there?"

Jovian nodded.

Chen Canaan hurriedly held his arm as if he were an enemy: "Since it's just a look-alike, you can't treat him as your brother."

Jovian lost his smile, "Of course not."
