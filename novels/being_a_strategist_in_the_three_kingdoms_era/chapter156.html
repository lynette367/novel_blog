The day of the festival was different in each dynasty. In the Zhou Dynasty, the winter solstice was still chosen, but in the Han Dynasty, it was changed to the upper Xin day of the first month of spring.

Right now, although there is still a month before the winter solstice, it is already considered very late to start making preparations for the festival.

Yan Qing nodded, and had to admit that the young emperor, whether he really suddenly remembered, or someone was helping him behind the scenes, launching the sacrifice of the heavens as an excuse was very effective, whether it was to ruthlessly brush up his presence or to establish a bit of heavenly authority.
