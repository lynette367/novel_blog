The door to the dormitory was locked, and Gu Yuanbai couldn't get out, so she could only stand inside the glass door and stare out.

There was a cold breeze in the night, but all he felt was hot, so hot that sweat broke out on his freshly showered back and body, and his young body got restless and expectant at the thought of a big hotel bed.

A figure darted in the darkness, fast and agile, and in an instant, he ran outside the dormitory door, breathing heavily. Xue Yuan propped up his knees and stared at Gu Yuanbai with difficulty amidst the sweaty rain, "Fifteen minutes."

The fastest half an hour's journey, he ran to Gu Yuanbai in fifteen minutes. A hard-earned red ticket was all given to the cab driver who sent him here, and was thought by the driver to be a life and death event at home.

Running wildly, running wildly, just to sleep with Gu Yuanbai.

Xue Yuan looked anxious, and Gu Yuanbai's eyes swept over him, finally settling on his feet, not knowing what to say, "You're even wearing the wrong shoes."

The shoes on the left and right feet aren't even the same pair, that's how much of a rush it is.

"The shoes don't matter," Xue Yuan was still panting, his eyes glowing brightly as he stared straight at Gu Yuanbai, desperate for an answer, "I'm here, do you mean what you say?"

"......" Gu Yuanbai opened his mouth and saw his own reflection on the glass door, his face contained a teasing hidden meaning with an unspeakable restlessness, Gu Yuanbai had never seen himself with this look before, he unconsciously inclined his head, and "Count."

Who is this person in the reflection and why does he look like he wants to get out of the way.

I blame Xue Yuan.

In his last life, he could barely make love when he was unwell, and after that, he was even more afraid to toss and turn. After so many years, Gu Yuanbai had already forgotten what it was like to have sex.

But the soul had tasted that etiolated flavor, and the young body had opened its stamens even more surreptitiously because of it.

Xue Yuan wiped the sweat from his face, and in the next moment, he was mesmerized by this look on Gu Yuanbai's face, dumbfounded and dumbfounded as he stared at him.

Behind them in the dormitory room an older man yelled at the top of his voice, "Who's out there?"

The sound of slippers coming over grew louder and louder, and Gu Yuanbai took a step back, took one last look at Xue Yuan, and turned to run into the darkness.

Xue Yuan was fixed in place, listening to the sound of Gu Yuanbai's footsteps gradually moving away, and in the quiet darkness of the night, the sound of these footsteps seemed to be able to step on Xue Yuan's heart. The hostel manager came out from his room, and when he saw Xue Yuan standing outside the door, he sternly said, "Which dormitory building? What are you doing here?"

Xue Yuan returned to his senses and turned to run.

The dormitory master put on his coat and went up to check the lock of the dormitory door, wondering, "I'm not a girl's dormitory building either."

Gu Yuanbai ran to the dormitory in one breath, the door slammed shut, and he leaned against the dormitory door panting slightly.

His heart was beating wildly and his head was sweating.

He opened his cell phone to see that it was 1:20 in the middle of the night, and it already showed that it was Saturday.

Gu Yuanbai leaned against the door for a full half hour of brain blankness before climbing into bed to go to sleep, just closed his eyes, and after two or three minutes of low cursing, he resignedly opened his eyes and took his cell phone to set a bell for seven in the morning.

-See you in front of the movie theater at 10:00 a.m.

Xue Yuan was simply too excited to sleep, but forced himself to sleep for a few hours for the sake of tomorrow's date. He jumped up as soon as the time came in the morning and boiled water to meticulously give himself a bath.

Putting on the new clothes that he had spent half of his fortune on yesterday, he took the money and his ID card and went out of his room with a bright look in his eyes.

It was still early for the date, and when Xue Yuan passed by the adult store next to the mall, he had a thought in his mind, and after thinking for less than three seconds, he walked in with a stern face.

You have to buy a set to open a house.

The boss smiled and said, "There is an event going on right now, buy three boxes and you get a small toy, the kind that vibrates, do you want to put one together, handsome?"

A box of ten, three boxes of thirty, can not be used a few times will be gone, Xue Yuan did not even hesitate to pay. Although he is poor, but poor what can not be poor here, would rather the next ten days are steamed bread with pickles also have to buy the best sets.

The boss met such a sharp customer this morning in the first wave of business, and was so happy that he brought Xue Yuan a small pink toy, and when he saw that Xue Yuan didn't know how to use it, he was considerate enough to teach him how to get it.

As soon as the vibrating sound came to mind, Xue Yuan suddenly remembered the scene yesterday morning when he was on the phone with his daughter-in-law, who said that she was washing her face, but there was this "buzzing" sound before she washed her face.

Daughter-in-law playing with herself?

Xue Yuan's brain rumbled, blown to the outside by this idea, his brain went blank, and only after reacting did he realize that his nose had already left a nosebleed.

"...... Fuck." Xue Yuan plugged his nose, glanced down at the small toys on the table, and couldn't help but gulp as he picked them up and left.

He waited until the theater opened and Xue Yuan didn't look at the screen, still looking at him like that.

"Yuanbai ......," Xue Yuan, his mouth dry, whispered, "Let s compare the size of our hands."

If you want to hold hands, you hold hands, and you lie with excuses.

Xue Yuan touched the strength of his fingers, the speed with which they slid from knuckle to knuckle, and the occasional itch that was deliberately left in his palm, the drumming that generally amplified and vibrated to his heart's eye.

Unusual holding hands does not have this feeling, have to use the excuse to play rogue like hand in hand, as if there is such an upper head stimulation of the blushing heartbeat.

Am I spoiling you too much?

After a movie, Gu Yuanbai seemed to be fully absorbed, but actually remembered nothing. As he followed the crowd outside, his hand was no longer his own, and he forced himself to draw it out, "Let go."

Gu Yuanbai was convinced.

After leaving the movie theater it happened to be dinner time, Gu Yuanbai took Xue Yuan to a private restaurant, wanting to make up for this guy's stomach.

Xue Yuan followed him and was respectfully brought to the private room, the decoration of this restaurant was full of undertones, and there were musicians playing music in the hall. Going by Xue Yuan's shallow modern knowledge, a meal here must not be cheap.

But the sad thing is that Xue Yuan has only five hundred dollars left on his body in one piece.

A big chunk of that is still money for the room I'm going to get later.

Xue Yuan wrinkled his brow, and when the waiter went out he whispered, "Yuanbai, I don't have enough money."

After a lifetime of high ranking officials and robbing without money, Bo Yuan Hou felt frustrated for the first time in his life when he came to the modern era.

It's good to have my daughter-in-law here.

Gu Yuanbai was a bit flustered and got up to come over, "Xue Yuan?"

This name was shouted out, but Xue Yuan didn't react, holding his hand to hide his expression, "It's fine."

Gu Yuanbai looked at him worriedly for several moments, poured him a cup of warm water, and had the waiter outside bring a hot towel to wipe over his face. Xue Yuan's heart was so warm that it swelled with pain, and he said vaguely under the towel, "Yuanbai, it's good to have you."

Gu Yuanbai's hands didn't stop, and the corners of his lips curled up slightly.

"No," Gu Yuanbai coughed lightly and didn't want to take the initiative to mention going to the hotel, casually pointing to a nearby drink store, "I'd like to have an iced drink."

But when Xue Yuan paid, he didn't have the heart to care about those points. Poor thing. What kind of life did you have before you found him?

Xue Yuan pursed his lips straight and didn't say anything.

Urgently, what should I tell my reincarnated daughter-in-law that I intend to dig your grave in your past life.

"Never mind," Gu Yuanbai stopped asking, he exhaled a mouthful of cloudy air and unbuttoned his coat to stand up, "I'll go take a shower first."

Xue Yuan's eyes widened, dumbfounded as he stepped into the bathroom, and the moment the shower sounded, he instantly detected a fishy odor under his nose.
