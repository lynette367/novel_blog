When Xue Yuan returned to the mansion, he took off these clothes on his body.

The blood from the wound had already clung to the clothing, Xue Yuan's face was expressionless as he exerted force violently, and the wound that had managed to stop bleeding violently popped out again.

These medicinal herbs Xue Yuan also didn't use them, he just laid them out and kept them to look at.

After being seriously injured and jumping into the lake, the lake water had soaked for so long that I guessed the wounds were going to rot.

Xue Yuan lazily said, "Jade pieces."

Mrs. Xue said angrily, "Is that a jade piece?"

Xue Yuan: "Can't throw it."

But these three words Mrs. Xue didn't hear, she couldn't stop herself from talking for a while longer, and at the end of her words, with tears in her eyes again, she took a handkerchief and wiped the corners of her eyes, "Good son, you be good. Mother will show you a good girl, and when you take the marriage, you will be more stable, and mother will feel relieved."

Xue Yuan laughed, "If you dare to marry me, I will dare to kill my wife."

Mrs. Xue's tears stopped immediately, and she was so angry that she turned around and was about to leave.

Xue Yuan raised his voice, "Remember to return it to me."

The doctor of the mansion was curious in his heart and did not hold back from asking, "The Grand Duke is now twenty-four, why is he still unwilling to take a wife?"

If the eldest prince doesn't take a wife, the second prince naturally can't take a wife either. Now with Xue Yuan's momentum, his reputation is so good, and with it, the second son of Xue's reputation stinks to the point where he probably won't be able to get a wife.

Xue Yuan closed his eyes and didn't answer.

When he smiles, his wit is exposed, and when he doesn't smile, he is much deeper. Resounding manhood, iron bones of a good son, I am afraid that there is no lack of good daughters want to marry.

Seeing that the Grand Duke was unwilling to speak, the doctor stopped talking.

After a long while, Xue Yuan closed his eyes and said as if he was talking in his sleep, "Just be able to marry."

The doctor spoke kind words, "If Duke Xue wants to marry, in my opinion, there's no one he can't marry."

Xue Yuan smothered in his pillow and laughed twice, his shoulders and back moving, then raised his voice and said, "Someone, bring the reward silver. That's a good point, you can't not reward it!"
