This living labor force has been hidden until now.

Gu Yuanbai had long known that there would be a shocking result after counting the household registers and taxes, this project lasted a full two to three years, ensuring that people as small as villages would also be counted by the government, over 600,000 farming households, how many acres of grain could this grow?

Jiangnan's newly counted population is more than 200,000 more than Jinghunan.

Although Gu Yuanbai had expected it, he still felt annoyed in his heart. In the memories of the young emperor, the late emperor was a tired and bitter man, and a large part of the reason was because of the hidden fields and leakage of households.

So in the morning court, he was three parts true and seven parts false, and he lost his temper once.

And all these things take time.

Gu Yuanbai smiled as she watched him scurry around in the pool, wiggling his arms and legs, feeling the just-right warmth of the pool, and then looking up at the miles of clear sky, where every cloud was nice to look at.

What a day.
