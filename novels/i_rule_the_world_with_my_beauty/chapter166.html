As soon as the two returned to the palace, a eunuch from the Hundred Beasts Garden came to report that the two adult wolves that Xue Yuan had given to Gu Yuanbai were about to die.

Gu Yuanbai froze, and followed the eunuch to the Hundred Beasts Garden without even having time to change his clothes. Two wolves whose fur had been covered with a layer of white gray lay helplessly on the ground, and as soon as Gu Yuanbai and Xue Yuan approached, they whimpered from the bottom of their throats, their ghostly eyes turning with difficulty, rubbing against their master's hand with great effort, and slowly lost their voices.

They lived for twelve years and died of old age on this day.

Xue Yuan picked up Gu Yuanbai and comforted him in a low voice, "Your Holiness, let's find a place to bury these two wolves."

Gu Yuanbai was still a bit stunned, "Good."

There were two other wolves in the Hundred Beasts Garden, wolves that had been sent to the palace since they were pups. Gu Yuanbai watched in silence as Xue Yuan led the two wolves out, and together they watched the eunuchs digging a pit to bury the wolf carcasses.

These wolves were wild and untamed, but they were tamed extremely obediently by Xue Yuan, and they accompanied Gu Yuanbai at all times. These wolves added a lot of trouble to Gu Yuanbai, but they also had a lot of fun.

He and Xue Yuan took them for evening walks when they had time, and from time to time, they also pressed their lips together for intimacy amidst the four pairs of green, luridly glowing wolf eyes. But in the blink of an eye, two of them were old enough to die.
