Gu Yuanbai turned his head to look, and it turned out to be Tian Fusheng, who had been sent by him to find the sprinkled palace maid to inquire about the news.

This courtesan face is very beautiful, I do not know whether it is hot or shy, there is a slight blush on the face. Slender hands, clothing is simple, but it highlights the body.

The hot water that had just been poured into the teacups flowed down the table in a hurry, and Gu Yuanbai quickly got up and took two steps back, her brows furrowed.

Tian Fusheng was ordering someone to clean up the water stains on the table when Gu Yuanbai walked over to the sides of the pavilion, his hands spaced out on the handrail, and glanced under the water.

Soon, surprisingly, there were small birds falling against the handrail, their sharp beaks crowing incessantly, and Gu Yuanbai couldn't help but reach out his hand, wanting to touch the feathers of these birds.

But it should be that the jade trigger finger on the thumb is too conspicuous, so that the animals also love, Gu Yuanbai's hand has not yet touched the bird, the bird has been taken by surprise, probing to take away the jade trigger finger on Gu Yuanbai's finger, a wave of the wings, to be articulated with the jade trigger finger to escape.

Gu Yuanbai's smile stiffened as he followed the jade trigger finger and saw the lake.

A dark shadow flashed by his side, and amidst Gu Yuanbai's last glance, he saw Xue Yuan step onto the railing and jump towards the lake without hesitation.

Gu Yuanbai's eyelids snapped, lifted up his robe and walked out of the pavilion quickly, calling out, "Xue Yuan!"

There was a crash of water, large sheets of it gushing down the wooden planes of the road, and Xue Yuan emerged with his upper body, soaked to the skin, the running water in his black hair snaking its way down the lines of his body.

He smiled helplessly toward Gu Yuanbai, "Your Holiness."

"Is Xue Qing this reckless even after he was injured?" Gu Yuanbai's tone was hard, "I'm asking you, if you're lifeless, how am I going to explain to General Xue!"

The fire in his heart steamed, his eyes staring straight at Xue Yuan, fire hidden in his eyes.

So pretty that the whole world lights up.

Gu Yuanbai's voice ended abruptly.

Xue Yuan quietly looked at him, half of his body still soaked in the water, his hand placed underwater lifted up, his five fingers loosened up, and inside was none other than a turquoise jade trigger so green that it was dripping with juice in general.

Gu Yuanbai took a few deep breaths and handed out his hand.

The wet hand gripped it, and the other hand took the jade trigger and slowly slipped it onto his thumb. Gu Yuanbai's throat knots moved up and down as if he wanted to speak, but in the end, he didn't say a single word, he looked deeply at Xue Yuan, and as he wished, he turned around and left the place.

Only after the silhouettes were almost invisible did Xue Yuan put away his smile, he braced himself on the ground with both hands and dragged himself onto the shore almost with the strength of his arms.

After hauling himself to shore, he closed his eyes and took a moment to slow down.

The lower half of his legs were still soaking in the water, and his back was soaked through his clothes to reveal wisps of blood. Fortunately, Gu Yuanbai had already left, or else in this state, after being seen, how could Xue Yuan still have the face to say that he was the most useful.

That is the folding fan that the saint held in his hand before, Chu Wei gave to the saint. Should be overly urgent, this fan was thrown here.

Prince He's face suddenly changed, mixed with disbelief, as he stared blankly at Gu Yuanbai.

"I'll say it for the last time," Gu Yuanbai snapped, "Get lost!"

The Prince of Peace's expression turned ironic as he opened and closed his lips a few times and turned to leave.
