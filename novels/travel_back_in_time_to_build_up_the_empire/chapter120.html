In the ancient times, the filial son treated the deceased as if he were alive. The offerings made on the 100th day clearly indicated that after the 100th day, the family would no longer send food to the deceased every day, but the deceased would make his own "food". This is because they believe that even though a person leaves the earth and goes to another world (the netherworld), he still needs to eat in the netherworld, just like on earth, and after the 100th day, the deceased should be able to get used to the netherworld without having to have his family bring him food anymore. Therefore, it is said to be a good time to get married within a hundred days. Outside of the 100th day, it is the end of the mourning period, which is unlucky, and no marriage is permitted.

Wu Wangsheng was already twenty-two years old this year, so if he didn't get married within a hundred days, he would have to wait three years before starting a family. The males of Great Qin basically discuss their marriage around the age of eighteen, and start a family around the age of twenty, so if Wu Wangsheng waited for another three years to get married at the age of twenty-five, then it would be too late. Therefore, once Chu Chao Hui's proposal came out, all the ministers of the king's court seconded it, and for the first time since Ripple Ji's rebellion, the king's court of Wu passed a proposal unanimously and without any objections. And the people of the king's capital were also happy that their monarch had finished his marriage, because a monarch getting married meant that there would be an heir born to the royal family, and the royal family's inheritance would be in order, so that the country could be stabilized.
