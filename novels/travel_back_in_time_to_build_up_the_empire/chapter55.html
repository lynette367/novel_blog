When they arrived at the border of the fiefdom, the Huben army stopped advancing, and they camped here, waiting for the delegation to return from the pilgrimage.

The lord of the vassal state summoned the feudal lord for an audience, and there was a limit to the number of people in the feudal lord's delegation to the royal capital, which could not be more than a hundred. The lord of the vassal state is wary of the feudal lord, and the feudal lord is also secretly wary of the lord of the state, and under the pretext of safety on the road, he will always let the Huben of his own fiefdom to escort to the edge of the fiefdom, and stationed at the edge of the fiefdom, so as to form a deterrent to the lord of the vassal state, and let the lord of the state think twice when he wants to dispose of them.

The nobles from Dacheng who went to the audience, together with Gong Ziguang there were only eight of them, besides Wei Shen, Chen Meng, Zheng Yuming and Chu Chao Hui, there were three other nobles of Dacheng who were more established, the patriarch Guo was old and didn't come, and Guo was only escorted by Guo Huben. The second most numerous noble in the mission, besides the followers that Gong Ziguang brought, was Nanbu, as Nanbu brought a band that performed and played. Although Nanbu seemed to bring more people than the other nobles, Zheng Yuming and Chu Chaohui both did not bring slaves and servants to serve like the other nobles, but only brought Wei Qing and Huo Sizhong, two teenagers to follow.

Gong Ziguang is the best mood in the mission, he rode a four-wheeled carriage is very spacious, there is a coffee table, the evening coffee table put away, put down the movable board can make a bed to lie down. If the wild sleep in the wild, four-wheeled carriage is a ready-made shelter.

"Zheng Qing, do you people from Bermuda often go out and travel in this kind of vehicle?" Zheng Yuming was always seen on Gong Ziguang's four-wheeled carriage, and as long as Zheng Yuming was not in Gong Ziguang's line of sight, Gong Ziguang would send mute slaves to look for him.

"Yes, it's all in these cars." Self-drive is a four-wheeler, but not a horse-drawn four-wheeler, but an engine-pulled one.

Zheng Yuming is very distressed, after understanding Chu Chao Hui's love for him, Zheng Yuming just wants to keep a distance from Gong Ziguang, one Chu Chao Hui is enough, Zheng Yuming doesn't want to mess with someone else. But Gong Zi Guang's clinginess to him cannot be clearly rejected.

"Zheng Qing, Kang City is very large, its walls are very majestic, and the city is very prosperous. When we get there, Guang will show Qing how it is." When Gongzi Guang talked about Kang City, he had a sense of pride.

"Thank you, Your Excellency!" Zheng Yuming was also very eager to see the ancient capital of two thousand years ago.

"Why doesn't Qing wear jade?" Gong Ziguang's eyes swept to Zheng Yuming's waist without ornaments, and could not help but wonder. Which nobleman does not wear jade, jade is a gentleman.

"Forgot, tomorrow will wear." Zheng Yuming feel the waist jingling is very troublesome, he himself did not buy jade, the existing jade jewelry are Gongziguang given, this time out, are packed in a box to bring around, just ready to participate in major places to wear.

"Qing don't forget to wear jade in the future." Dacheng is a small place, no one says much if you don't wear jade, in a place like the royal capital where big nobles gather, if you don't wear jade, it will make the nobles despise you.

"Aye! Yuming takes note." With Gongzi Guang emphasizing jade ornaments so much, Zheng Yuming felt that in the future, he would have to buy some good jade to fill up his face.

Chu Chao Hui, who rode his horse and followed closely behind Gong Zi Guang's four-wheeled carriage walking, looked straight ahead with both eyes, lips pursed, face taut, a look of full alert, but his ears listened to the conversation of the people in the carriage, refusing to let go of any details.

Wei Shen, who was sitting on the carriage behind Gong Ziguang, continuously stroked his beautiful beard, and in his heart, he was very pleased that Chu Chao Hui was doing his duty.
