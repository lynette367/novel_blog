As a son of heaven, his fief was shrinking year by year, and his income became less and less. With less income, in order to maintain his status, he could not reduce his splendor, and the tribute from the vassals became the main source of daily expenses for the Son of Heaven.

As the vassal monarchs refused to make the pilgrimage in person, the tribute sent by messengers became more and more perfunctory, and in recent years, the vassal monarchs even canceled the tribute on the pretext that disasters had struck their fiefs.

Without the tribute, Ying Zhai still has to spend money. Winning Zhai could be said to have empty pockets and could not afford to repair the damaged cities. The Xiguankou Pass west of Haojing was a necessary passage to ward off the nomads in the north, and without consolidating that place, the Great River Valley would be left open for the nomads to loot at will.

Just when Win Zhai was trying to figure out what to do, Zheng Yuming's petition to right the wrongs of Tang was delivered.

The palace where the bitter Qin Emperor lived had not been maintained for fifteen or sixteen years, and the clad copper on the palace doors was rusted, so for the sake of face, Win Zhai repaired the palace as well.

The day of return promised to the merchants was in May, and at the beginning of May, the merchants came to him for money.
