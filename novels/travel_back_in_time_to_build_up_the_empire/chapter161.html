Zheng Yuming was transported to Qin by the blue light, more than seven years, than in the twenty-first century are busy. All year round, busy never have a free day. But let Zheng Yuming did not think of is that the leisurely days of hope in the body of a serious injury to come.

"Your Excellency! His Highness Prince Zhao has come to visit!" The orderly outside the bedroom came in to inform Zhao Xuan.

"Come in!" Zheng Yuming hurriedly got up from the bed to put on his shoes.

"Your Excellency be careful!" The orderly stepped forward to assist Zheng Yuming.

"I can walk on my own, go ask Prince Zhao to come in." Zheng Yuming waved his hand, his injuries were much better now, and he had no problem walking.

Zheng Yuming and Chu Chaohui both disliked being surrounded by a large group of people in their lives, and were used to doing things on their own. Whether in Ulsan, or in the royal capital of Kang, they have never used waiters and waitresses. In addition to having the Su woman to take care of them, they only had the guards on duty to do the chores. The two of them tried to do as much as they could on their own.

"Your Highness the Crown Prince! My son has an invitation!" Da Niu made a gesture of invitation, allowing Zhao Xuan to enter the bedroom, while Bo Yao and Wu Mou were blocked from the bedroom.

Zhao Xuan walked to Zheng Yuming's bedroom door just in time to see Zheng Yuming slowly get up and move towards the bedroom door, while the young guard soldier beside Zheng Yuming even just left his master to walk away.

"Yudie! Be careful!" Zhao Xuan glanced across at the guard soldier and rushed to Zheng Yuming's side in a single stride to hold him up.

Both Zheng Yuming and Chu Chaohui did not have a nobleman's stance and got along casually with the people around them, and over time, the personal guards were not restrained in front of the two and acted more casually. The casual behavior of the soldiers made Zhao Xuan think that the guards intended to slow down Zheng Yuming.

"Your Highness, I'll walk by myself." Jung Yuming resisted.

When Zhao Xuan held on violently, one hand touched his left shoulder, causing Jung Yuming to bare his teeth in pain. Zheng Yuming's left shoulder had been stabbed by Kui Ji, and the wound had just scarred and was still wrapped in gauze.

"Yu Di, you are a noble prince and a prime minister of a country, how can you not have attendants to wait on your left and right?" Zhao Xuan saw that it hurt Zheng Yuming, panicked and let go of both hands, Zhao Xuan has never taken care of people, nor can he take care of people, now he wants to show consideration, but in turn it makes Zheng Yuming suffer.

"Yuming's injuries aren't that serious, the healer has instructions that walking around more is good for the body." Zheng Yuming stroked his left shoulder to distance himself from Zhao Xuan while inviting Zhao Xuan to take a seat. Zheng Yuming didn't hear that Zhao Xuan was wrathful for him.

Zhao Xuan sat down on a mahogany recliner, after sitting him down he surveyed Zheng Yuming on the soft recliner opposite him.

"Your Highness, please have some tea!" The guard soldier poured hot tea for Zhao Xuan and a cup of plain water for Zheng Yuming. Zheng Yuming was taking Chinese medicine and couldn't drink green tea.

Zhao Xuan carefully looked at Zheng Yuming through the white smoke of the hot tea. Zheng Yuming face because less blood color, skin white shocking, but make the features more distinct. The black hair did not wear a crown, only the top of the head bundled and draped over the shoulders. The whole body was wearing a set of light green twenty-first century Tai Chi Kung Fu shirt style silk clothes. Clothes are very stretched on the body, elegant and elegant, all without half a bit of loose, the whole person exudes a kind of ineffable style.

"When will your highness depart for home?" Zheng Yuming sip of plain water began the guest words, asked and waited for a while, Zhao Xuan did not respond, under the strange, raised his eyes to look, but found Zhao Xuan eyes flooded with infatuation.

"Your Highness!" With Zhao Xuan staring so blatantly and deadpan, Zheng Yuming was a bit embarrassed and couldn't help but raise his voice to remind Zhao Xuan.

"Ahem... My brother will depart for home today." Zhao Xuan woke up, his face burning slightly as he cleared his throat and straightened his back, sitting upright.

"Setting off in such a hurry, a worsening injury is Wu's sin, Your Highness should still get well and return to your country." Zheng Yuming put down the cup in his hand and scanned Zhao Xuan up and down, Zhao Xuan's arms and legs were still wrapped in gauze. Zheng Yuming felt that he could use his injuries to delay Zhao Xuan for a few more days.

When Zhao Xuan heard that Zheng Yuming was concerned about his injuries, his heart warmed and his body slightly inclined towards Zheng Yuming. "Thank you for Yu Di's concern! My brother also wanted to stay a few more days, waiting for Yu Di to recover before returning. But that Tang King sent assassins to assassinate, causing Yu Di to be seriously injured and nearly lost his life after still at large. My brother is now anxious, want to return to the country as soon as possible, send troops to attack the Tang, for Yu brother to seek justice."

Not only did Zhao Xuan speak passionately with his mouth, but he also had deep feelings in his eyes.

Zheng Yuming a bit unable to eat Zhao Xuan's gentle attack, he lowered his eyes to avoid Zhao Xuan line of sight, the heart secretly slander: you Zhao Xuan want to go back early despite the wounds, but want to lead the troops to grab the benefits of it, but to say so flowery, as if the Tang sent troops is for the sake of others.

"Your Highness is so generous, Yuming can't thank you enough! Yuming's injury is a small matter, Your Highness' health is a big matter, hope Your Highness will think twice." Zheng Yuming knew that he couldn't keep Zhao Xuan, but he still had to say the polite words.

"Yudi don't worry, my brother since childhood in the military camp training, this small injury in my brother no harm, wait until the king of the Tang captured, my brother personally escorted the king of the Yong came to the Wu Kingdom with the virtuous brother to celebrate the gathering." Zhao Xuan more and more affectionate.

Zheng Yuming couldn't help but listen, it was a question whether Zhao Xuan could set foot on Tang land, but he was still thinking of capturing the King of Tang with his own hands.

"Then Yuming will wait for Your Highness's triumph in Ulsan!"

Zhao Xuan looked at Zheng Yuming's smiling face and remembered what he had seen after entering the county residence, an idea suddenly rose in his heart, and after this idea rose, it began to grow wildly.

"Yu Di, after you were injured this time, it seems like Great General Chu won't let you handle political affairs?"

The reason why Zhao Xuan thought that Chu Chao Hui was seizing power had to do with Zhao Xuan's own experience. When King Zhao initially asked Zhao Xuan to oversee the country, the ministers of Zhao's court did not take Zhao Xuan as their lord; they remained loyal to Zhao Xuan's father and only reported to King Zhao in all matters.

Zhao Xuan initially supervised the state for one year, only a small part of the power. Zhao Xuan patience for two years, secretly cultivate the heart, pulling together powerful ministers, the third year of supervision, Zhao Xuan control of the real power, the royal court situation is the opposite of the original, Zhao Wang palace door cold, the Prince residence in front of the bustling. Zhao Xuan today in the county residence scene, Zheng Yuming is now in the situation with his father lost real power is too similar. Only Zhao Xuan more power in the hands, the more in the surface of the father respect.

Jung Yuming sighed, "Yeah, Yuming isn't allowed to ask about anything."

Jiang Gui wants Zheng Yuming to recuperate, Chu Chaohui will be all things are taken over, and not allowed to Ulsan civil and military officials to disturb Zheng Yuming, who violated, Chu Chaohui do not ask the reason, all rewarded twenty sticks. Some people forget Chu Chao Hui's police orders, habitually go to Zheng Yuming to ask for political affairs, the result was the guards on duty of the elite guards pressed down on the bench splitting the twenty small stick, since then, people do not dare to arbitrarily go to the backyard to disturb Zheng Yuming.

Zhao Xuan was certain in his heart and felt that Chu Chao Hui must have taken advantage of Zheng Yuming's injury to seize power. Chu Zhaohui gave Zhao Xuan the feeling of being a domineering lord, Zhao Xuan thought that that kind of character would not be willing to submit to others. Zheng Yuming's gentle character was too easy to be controlled by Chu Chao Hui.

"Great General Chu has gone too far."

Zheng Yuming shook his head and defended Chu Chaohui, "Chaohui is also thinking of me."

Zhao Xuan saw that Zheng Yuming's power was taken away and still spoke for Chu Chao Hui, his heart was cynical and sour. "If the Great General treats you wrongly, Yudie can go to Zhao, and my brother will be entrusted with the country."

Zheng Yuming looked at Zhao Xuan in shock, couldn't figure out why Zhao Xuan had to say such a thing to him, how did Chu Chao Hui give Zhao Xuan the feeling that he would treat him wrongly?

Zhao Xuan saw Zheng Yuming surprised, fearing that Zheng Yuming did not believe his sincerity, clutched over Zheng Yuming's hand on his thigh and clenched it. "Zhao Xuan sincerity from the heart, Yu Di remember!"

In Zhao Xuan's opinion, Zheng Yuming was not yet clear about Chu Chao Hui's ambitions and had not yet awakened. Zhao Xuan in Zheng Yuming did not recognize the true face of Chao Hui before, can not bluntly let Zheng Yuming abandon Chu Chao Hui, defect to Zhao, or with his alliance to suppress Chu Chao Hui. He could only leave an invitation half explicitly and half implicitly.

Zheng Yuming slowly drew his hand back from Zhao Xuan's palm, and only then did he realize that Zhao Xuan was misunderstanding that Chu Chao Hui had emptied him of his power.

"Thank you, Your Highness, for your kindness. Yuming only stayed in Wu because of his elder brother Gongziguang's posthumous order, Yuming cannot disregard the wishes of the king's brother." Zheng Yuming was a bit teary-eyed. Seeing that Zhao Xuan refused to die and wanted to convince again, he hurriedly raised his hand to stop him.

"Your Highness don't say much, Yuming will not leave Wu." Zheng Yuming had no intention of letting Zhao Xuan clear up his misunderstanding, Zhao Xuan misunderstood that he had a conflict with Chu Chao Hui in favor of Wu. "Your Highness! Yuming has injuries on his body, he can't give Your Highness a ride, please forgive me, Your Highness!"

Zhao Xuan was disappointed, but he felt that even though he hadn't achieved his goal of abducting Jung Yuming to Zhao, he was able to make Jung Yuming understand Chu Chaohui's ambitions, which should have separated the two.

"Then I'll take my leave now, I hope we meet up soon!" Zhao Xuan got up and bid farewell to Zheng Yuming.

Zheng Yuming, with a smile on his face, followed and got up to see him off, "Yuming will be waiting in Ulsan for the sound of Your Highness's triumph!"

Zhao Xuan took Bo Yao and Wu Mian out of the county residence and got into a four-wheeled carriage, and the monarchs and ministers traveled out of the north gate of Dacheng in the direction of Kangcheng. Returning to Zhao by the same route.

When Zhao Xuan departed for his home country, Huang Zhong had already arrived in Chu with Wei Shuqing, the head of the Wei Clan in Kangcheng. When they arrived in Chu, the two men acted separately, Wei Shuqing went to contact the Wei Clan Chief, while Huang Zhong sought informants to lobby the left and right daimyo of Chu.

The deceased prince fit in the state of Chu is not very good, when he hurriedly fled from the state of Wei, originally to the state of Chen to avoid the wind, and wait for Zhao Xuan's anger to pass and then go back to the state of Wei. He did not want to Zhao Xuan not only anger, but also sent troops to attack, in a short time, the state of Wei fell. The king of Wei opened the city gates and begged for peace. After the King of Wei surrendered, his wife and children were put under house arrest somewhere in Zhao, but two years later, the King of Wei's family got sick and died strangely, and only Gongzi Xie, who hated Zhao Xuan deeply, was left in the royal family of Wei.

After the destruction of the state of Wei, the state of Chen had an ambiguous attitude towards Gong Zi Xie, and Gong Zi Xie was afraid of being betrayed by the state of Chen to the state of Zhao, so he had to continue to flee to the state of Chu. When Duke Shi came out, he did not carry much gold and silver, and if it was not for the head of the Wei family who followed him to Chu with his clansmen, Duke Shi's life would have been in trouble, not to mention the restoration of the state.

Although Duke Shi was supported by the Wei clan, his desire to restore his country with the help of Chu was getting more and more remote. The king of Chu took in Gongzi Xie to disgust Zhao, but did not really help him. After three years of staying in Chu, the king saw that there was no hope for revenge and became more and more disillusioned. Just when the king was drinking every day, the head of the Wei clan brought a man to give him hope.
