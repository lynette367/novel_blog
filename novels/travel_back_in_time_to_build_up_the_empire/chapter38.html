Nambu village although to outsiders have a great change in the impression, but the whole village in the eyes of Zheng Yuming, is still poor and backward. The so-called streets in the village are formed naturally because of the dirt road that connects the pier to the city, and the villagers' houses on both sides of the village street are twisted and uneven.

Zheng Yuming while surveying the village residence, while silently pondering the future development prospects of the village, the line of sight to Yu Kui's wine shop, he could not help but stare. Because the wine consumption is relatively high, Nambu Village, luxury consumers are not too many, Zheng Yuming every time he passes by two-thirds of the time to see Yu Kui at the gate to solicit customers. Out of the village to the dirt road, Zheng Yuming and Huo Sizhong will speed up the horse speed run up, ten minutes to the gap passage under the hillside. Many of the surrounding villagers already know that there is a passage into the valley, because the valley has now become a private territory, the villagers do not dare to enter the gap passage, coupled with the gap passage has been added a wooden door, there are also slaves to guard, there are villagers who entered the village was warned, the curious villagers of the valley immediately rested their curiosity, trespassing into the private territory of the nobles in the Great Qin punishment is also very heavy.

Although he knew that there would be no curious village people trying to enter the slit passage, Zheng Yuming still habitually looked at Zhou before throwing his horse into the slit opening. The White Flower Horse had only run a few steps in the passageway when Zheng Yuming strangled the horse's reins.

"Who are you?"

Not far from the slit passage, a man wearing a dark green coat, wearing a high crown on his head, wearing a sword on his waist, with his head bowed, stood quietly in the center of the passage way. Zheng Yuming's hand moved to his waist, his fingers pressed the handle of the pistol, this mini pistol is not as powerful as the one of Chu Chao Hui, but it is still very certain to take down a person at close range.

The man in green raised his head, revealing half of his face with a beard, this man was Yu Kui, the owner of the Nambu Village Winery.

"Chu Di Yu Kui would like to serve before the two governors' saddles." Yu Kui stood upright and bowed long to Zheng Yuming.

"Winemaker Yu?" Zheng Yuming was stunned, Yu Kui was waiting here in such a gown on a hot day just to follow him and Chu Chao Hui? Could this be the almighty king of the travelers, making the people of this time and space cry out to be their little brother?

Zheng Yuming's brain was fighting whether to accept a junior or just reject this wine shop owner who dared to enter the gaping passage. But this wine shop owner was dressed as an aristocrat, Yu Kui was an aristocrat?

Zheng Yuming had a natural defense against the nobles of this time and space, this traveler who was deeply educated in modern thought, subconsciously placed the noble ruling class on the opposite side of his own, reforming slaves was simple, they were uneducated and had simple thoughts, but the nobles were different, cultured nobles had their own established thoughts, Yu Kui with this look was planning to go into the valley and serve for them, so that thinking nobles could be allowed to go into the valley Can such a thoughtful nobleman be allowed to enter the valley?

"Winemaker Yu, why are you keeping watch here, Zheng Yuming is in Nambu every day." Zheng Yuming did not relax his attitude because of Yu Kui's move to defect.

"Yu Kui has great talent and wants to become the disciple of the two governors, so of course he has to follow the Lord's family around." Yu Kui means he wants to enter the core place of the Lord's family, to become a beloved one.

"What great talent do you have to convince me?" Zheng Yuming's expression was light, he didn't have much of the smugness of having recruited a little brother.

"Yu Kui has a pair of powerful eyes, a pair of sensitive ears, and a clever head, with these three things, Yu Kui judges things ten to nine."

"Judging things can be no more than eight or nine?" Zheng Yuming felt that this Yu Kui appeared to be bragging.

"Lord Zheng, allow Yu Kui to guess the valley behind the passage." Yu Kui had a good idea.

"Good! You say it!" Zheng Yuming flinched and pressed his fingers on the handle of the pistol harder.

"The two lords must have secrets in the valley." Yu Kui dropped a bomb.

"Winemaker Yu is joking, the valley is the fiefdom of Yuming and his elder brother, the two of us brothers don't want outsiders to enter just, many of the feudal lords of Dacheng have done the same." Zheng Yuming pulled his mouth to deny it, this Yu Kui is blinded right, see they don't open the valley to the public.

"Not bad, the valley is like many other fiefdoms, not wanting outsiders to enter seems normal." Yu Kui spoke with a tone. "But both of you, Your Lordships, Wu Da is your sworn elder brother, by all rights he should be as close as a blood relative, but he has not been invited into the valley by you even once, Yu Mou has observed for a long time, and realized that Your Lordships are not not treating Wu Da as a blood relative, so Yu Mou has boldly guessed that the secrets in the valley are very special, and that they can't be made known to Wu Da. Another thing that aroused Yu's speculation was that His Excellency only wanted slaves and not domestic servants, apparently fearing that the domestic servants would break away from the master's family and spread the word about the master's family." A domestic servant is a civilian status and can leave from the main house, however it is not very easy for a civilian to become a nobleman's domestic servant, and a domestic servant basically refuses to leave the main house on his own.

After listening to Yu Kui's analysis, Zheng Yuming was silent, a gust of mountain wind pierced through, the deep clothes on Yu Kui's body was snapped by the mountain wind, this ringing sound woke up Zheng Yuming from his contemplation, this Yu Kui no matter if he was collected or not, he couldn't be allowed to leave the slit passageway just like that.

"Noble Yu, please follow me into the valley first." Zheng Yuming made up his mind to throw Yu Kui, a noble, to Chu Chao Hui to verify, Chu Chao Hui was more ruthless than him, if he felt that Yu Kui was in danger, Zheng Yuming felt that Chu Chao Hui would not hesitate to make Yu Kui disappear.

"No!" Yu Kui's reply was now in the posture of a gatekeeper.

Zheng Yuming asked Yu Kui and Huo Sizhong to ride a horse together in front, and he followed behind to press the front. Huo Sizhong had been taken care of by Yu Kui in Nambu, so when he heard that Yu Kui was going to join the two lords, his heart rejoiced and he could not help but be much more cordial to Yu Kui.

Zheng Yuming on the way back to the villa, just met some slaves from the fields between the collection of work back, the slaves see the master's horses, all stand to the side of the road to make way for the master's horses, these slaves attitude respectful and reverent, no fear, Yu Kui look in the heart, more firmly his heart to join the cause.

The horses traveled to the living area around the villa, and just happened to run into Chu Chao Hui doing the last formation before disbanding, a few days of formation training had some results, today's formation was fast and relatively neat. Yu Kui actually overestimated Chu Chao Hui's ability, now the slave soldiers will only line up the type, fighting skills do not know anything do not understand, Yu Kui see just flowery. Zheng Yuming saw Yu Kui staring at the slave soldiers, heart sigh, this Yu Kui has seen the valley's biggest secret, can not be trusted in the two of them, Chu Chao Hui is sure to not let Yu Kui alive to leave the valley.

Chu Chao Hui turned around and glanced at Yu Kui standing beside Zheng Yuming, and his eyes immediately narrowed.

"He waited for me in the center of the slit passageway, saying that he wanted to plunge under our banner, I don't know whether I should take it or not, this person has some skills." Zheng Yuming detailed Yu Kui's speculations within the passageway to Chu Chao Hui.

Although Yu Kui has seen a lot of knowledge, but saw the villa and the yard is still shocked for a while, paved with a whole big stone yard and green brick and red tile house, in the luxury of the palace can be seen, but the house with a transparent glaze for the windows is too extravagant, no wonder Zheng Chu two people refused to let the outsiders to enter the valley, this is to prevent people from the greed of the heart.

"Yu Mou has the certainty that he can win the trust of the two lords before he boldly enters the valley." Yu Kui did not panic in the face of Chu Chao Hui's stern voice, he pulled out a piece of parchment from his bosom and honored Chu Chao Hui.

Yu Kui's eyes and eyebrows were somewhat similar to the person in the portrait.

"Yu's family is a hereditary nobleman in the Xing Di of Chu, the family ancestor is a straightforward person, six years ago, he did not tolerate the left doctor of Chu, Tian Chen, was framed by this treacherous villain, the whole family was wiped out by the king of Chu, Yu deceived due to hunting in the mountains of the fiefdom was spared, and escaped to the southern port with great difficulty to survive, only thinking that one day he could take revenge and wipe out the grudge."

Yu Kui, also known as Yu Deceiver, talked about his life, his face was no longer calm, his emotions began to flare up, and his eyes reddened.

"You defected to us because you want to help avenge your own family? How is that possible! We're not as powerful as Ulsan-kun, you should have defected to Ulsan-kun."

Zheng Yuming put down the parchment in his hands, Yu Kui this parchment out, is tantamount to handing over their lives to their hands, the Chu left doctor or Tian Chen, this wanted notice is still valid. If Tian Chen knows that Yu Kui is hiding in the south port of Dacheng, as long as the king of Wu, Yu Kui will immediately be deported back to Chu, Chu is more powerful than Wu, the king of Wu will not be for an irrelevant dead aristocrat to offend the neighboring country than their own powerful.

"Chu will be in great trouble if this goes on for a long time!" Yu Kui didn't think that Zheng Chu and the two men had the strength to destroy Chu, but he thought that the two men were fully capable of emptying Chu, Yu Kui hated Chu's royal court to the bone, he didn't care which vassal's mouth Chu's fat meat would go into, he hoped that he could play a part in overthrowing Chu's royal court, Yu Kui didn't simply think that Yu's family had been exterminated merely because of being set up by Tian Chen, and that the dim-witted King of Chu was the culprit.

"Good! Yu Deceiver! It's fine if you want to defect to our heels, you have to swear not to rebel against your master, and you must not urge us to help you take revenge." Chu Chaohui was in love with Yu Kui, this person, whether in ancient times or modern times, is the elite of the elite ah, with this person to help, really got a big help.

"Two lords! Please still call me Yu Kui; if the family vengeance does not go unrewarded, I will never revert to my original name."

Yu Kui performed the disciple salute to the two Zheng Chu and made another solemn oath. The two Zheng Chu had their first disciple.
