Author's comment: Zhuozhuzhu is a kind of deep well with a small mouth. When drilling a well, a "one-type" drill is used to pound the rock by impact, and water is injected into the well or utilized from the groundwater, and the rock debris and water are drawn out by a bamboo tube. The diameter of a Zhuozhu well is only the size of a bowl, and the wall of the well is not easy to collapse. Ancient people also removed the nodes of large bamboo, jointed the head and tail, wrapped hemp rope around the outside, coated with putty, and lowered it into the well as a casing to prevent the well wall from collapsing and fresh water from soaking in. Take brine, bamboo as a fine brine drawer, inserted into the casing, the bottom of the tube to cook the skin for the opening and closing valves, a tube can draw brine several buckets, wells erected on a large wooden frame, with a windlass, car pan to extract brine.

The appearance of Zhuozhu wells marked the maturity of the drilling process of deep wells in ancient China. Since then, the depth of salt wells has been increasing. In 1835, the world's first deep well of more than 1,000 meters was drilled in Zigong Salt District, Sichuan Province.
