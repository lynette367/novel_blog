Zheng Yuming's real age added up to thirty-five years old, twenty-eight years living in the twenty-first century, more than seven years in Great Qin. In these two worlds add up to thirty-five years time, Zheng Yuming hands to do the most cruel things but is to kill fish slaughter chickens and ducks.

In modern times, Zheng Yuming is a regular and law-abiding person, except for a bit of cunning when talking business. When he arrived in Qin, there has been Chu Chao Hui in front of him, and the bloody winds and rain have all been fended off by Chu Chao Hui. Initially, in the valley, Chu Chao Hui killed a large slave to establish prestige, Zheng Yuming chose to avoid looking. Ulsan rise to the crusade, is Chu Chao Hui led the army in front, Zheng Yuming is just walking in the smoke has been extinguished battlefield, see the most bloody scene is lying on the ground, no life feature corpses.

To a certain extent, it can be said that Zheng Yuming in the twenty-first century is too comfortable, to come to Great Qin by Chu Chao Hui is too well protected, lack of experience in the face of crisis. So as to encounter dangerous situations, under the tension, there will be a succession of situations, first forget to give the pistol on the safety, and then forget the pavilion pavilion built on the protruding rocks, rolling to avoid Kui Ji sword amplitude is too large, fell out of the pavilion building, from the side of the stone steps rolled into the mountain stream.

Feeling that the bottom of his body fell out of the air, Zheng Yuming's mind surprisingly only emerged a call: Chu Chao Hui, where is it?

The movement on the pavilion building has long attracted the attention of the surrounding guards and others, Da Niu was the first to rush there, he was the closest to the place of the incident. Da Niu's arrival let Zhao Xuan out of danger, Zhao Xuan was covered in blood collapsed not far from the pavilion building, the injury looked scary. Da Niu's heart is anxious about the Lord, did not go to check Zhao Xuan's death, only want to go to Zheng Yuming's side as soon as possible to protect, when he was almost to the pavilion pavilion, but it was when Zheng Yuming fell into the mountain stream.

"My lord!" Da Niu roared furiously and flew over, trying to tug on Zheng Yuming's clothes, but his fingers only reached the corner of Zheng Yuming's coat.

Da Niu this pounced too hard, the whole person fell on the stone steps, half of the body hanging outside the stone steps, into the eye, Da Niu can only see the bottom of the stone steps gully more than ten meters away, and then below to the waterfall water vapor obscured.

The bull's voice was mournful, the sound echoed through the valley, a few pieces of debris fell down, brown and black shadows scurried out of the corner of the bull's eye, and the wood scurried down the mountain steps, quickly out of sight.

"My lord!"

"Duke!"

"Your Highness!"

Various voices came from behind Da Niu, Ma Ren, Huo Si Hua, Fu Chou, Yang He, Wei Kou, Zhao Xuan Wu Zai Bo Yao, Sheriff Wu Mian and some guards arrived successively.

Ma Ren and Huo Si Hua were the first to jump to Da Niu's side, dragging Da Niu's falling body. Bo Yao and Wu Mou ran to Zhao Xuan's side, anxiously checking Zhao Xuan's injuries. A few guards, on the other hand, held their weapons against Kui Ji, who fell motionless in a pool of blood.

"Quick! Yudie... Eunuch Ming has fallen, go save him." Zhao Xuan was lying on the ground, his blood-stained fingers trembling as he pointed below the pavilion building, not knowing whether he was anxious about his own fate or his heart burning for Zheng Yuming's life. If Zheng Yuming's life was not guaranteed, Zhao Xuan could not hope to return to Zhao safely, Kui Ji was the one he brought to Ulsan.

Although Bo Yao and Wu Mou knew that Zheng Yuming's life must not be lost, but Zhao Xuan is their lord, their immediate concern is Zhao Xuan's injuries, the two quickly checked Zhao Xuan, and found that although Zhao Xuan's whole body clothes are stained with blood, and his appearance looks scary, but in fact, his injuries are not serious. The wounds on Zhao Xuan's body that were scratched by Kui Ji's sword were not deep, as long as the blood was stopped and the wounds were not inflamed, this injury would not threaten Zhao Xuan's life.

Ma Ren and Huo Sihua dragged Da Niu to a halt, and after learning from Da Niu that Zheng Yuming had already fallen down, and that the wood had jumped down with him, the two men's faces immediately turned white. Ma Ren did not think twice about climbing down to search and rescue.

"Listen guys, the wood is screaming!" Huo Si Hua stopped Ma Ren.

Woody 'woofed' from below the pavilion, not too far away by the sound of it.

"It must be that Woody has found the adults." Da Niu was excited, he was strong, but his body was too lanky for going down the cliff to rescue people.

"I'll go save your lordship." Ma Ren was lean and agile, and immediately went down to look for Zheng Yuming.

"Wait!" Huo Sihua stops, standing up and taking off the long summer clothes on his body, turning back to urge Yang He and the others. "All of you take off your clothes."

Da Niu is still frozen, and several others have already understood Huo Si Hua's meaning. The terrain under the pavilion is too steep, Ma Ren, even if he finds Zheng Yuming, will not be able to save Zheng Yuming on his back, Huo Sihua undresses to ask everyone to knot their clothes into long strips, so that it is convenient for Ma Ren to pull on the strength to climb up.

After understanding Huo Sihua's meaning, all of them took off their outerwear, this time is summer, all of them wore single clothes, take off their outerwear, inside is the underwear, it's a good thing that people at this time are conservative, the underwear is like the undershirts and lab coats in the twenty-first century, and wearing the underwear can also cover the body. Only Wei Kou's tunic is muslin, not firm enough, take off and not knotted on the long strip.

The people's clothes are quickly knotted into long strips, the wood is screaming more and more fiercely below, Ma Ren can't wait for the long strips to be knotted enough, tied one end of the long strips to his body and climbed down. Da Niu, Yang He and others continue to knotted cloth ropes for clothes to lengthen above, the guards continue to run up, the material of cloth ropes increased, the guards' cloth soldier clothes are very firm, making long cloth ropes will not tear.

Zheng Yuming fell down to feel that he must die, under the pavilion is a waterfall formed water pool, the distance from the pavilion building is more than one hundred meters, equivalent to the height of more than forty-storey building, falling from that height, falling on the water even if it does not die will have to smash coma, coma in the water will only be drowned.

Zheng Yuming's desire to survive is very strong, when he fell two hands desperately scratching, want to drag something to stop their own down, but Zheng Yuming's luck is not good, dragged to the things with his fall was torn off, until the back of a pain, the whole person threw up a little bit and then stopped, Zheng Yuming hand touched the cypress branches underneath the person fainted from the pain.

Zheng Yuming thought he had fainted for a long time, but in fact it was only a few minutes, when he heard the voice of his beloved dog and opened his eyes, he found Woody wandering around an alcove above his head, Woody wanted to be by his master's side, but there was no place to land.

"Wooooo!" The wood was anxious and tried to go down several times, nearly falling outside, but also dropping dust and debris, which fell on Zheng Yuming.

"Wood, don't move, just stay there." Zheng Yuming felt a sharp pain all over his body, to stop him from falling is a small cypress tree diagonally long, small cypress tree is not big, to stop Zheng Yuming's fall to support Zheng Yuming's body, the roots have been shaken, wood if you come down again, cypress may not be able to withstand the weight of the one man and one dog, Zheng Yuming himself can not move to climb up the cliff, can only wait for the above to come to the rescue.

The wood seemed to understand its master, and it threw back its head and barked furiously, trying to tell the rescuers above of its master's location.

Ma Ren tied the rope knotted by his clothes and descended to the pavilion pavilion thirty meters or so to see the figure of wood and Zheng Yuming, the mood is not excited.

"My lord! My lord! How is your lordship!" Maren asked in a loud voice as he increased his speed.

"Ma Ren..." Zheng Yuming's voice was weak, but it was like spring thunder in Ma Ren's ears.

The people by the pavilion building knew from Ma Ren's voice that Zheng Yuming was still alive and couldn't help but let out a slight sigh of relief. Huo Sihua's legs went limp as she fell to her knees at the edge of the cliff, praying in her heart while stifling low sobs. Zhao Xuan, who was lying on the ground being bandaged by his men, had his heart lifted up and then fell slightly to the ground. Da Niu, Yang He, Fu Chou and a few guards were busy pulling the long strips of cloth so that Ma Ren, who was climbing with Zheng Yuming on his back, could borrow strength.

In people's expectations, Ma Ren carrying Zheng Yuming up, Zheng Yuming's sanity is still awake, only pale, half of the shoulder is stained red with blood. Ma Ren will Zheng Yuming carefully put down, aside the guards hurriedly take out the ambulance bag for Zheng Yuming's shoulder bandage. Ulsan's soldiers and guards each have an ambulance bag, when they are injured on the battlefield, the ambulance bag can first alleviate the injury and allow them to survive until the military doctor comes to treat them.

Ma Ren and the others save Zheng Yuming, and a guard ties on a cloth to go down the cliff to save Woody, who can go down but can't climb up, and remains at the recessed rock.

When Bo Yao and Wu Mian saw the Wu guards take out their special first aid kits to save their lord, they were dissatisfied because they used torn strips of cloth from their clothes to bandage Zhao Xuan, and the guards all had first aid kits, but none of them came to save Zhao Xuan. Although Bo Yao and Wu Mian were dissatisfied, they didn't dare to show it on the surface, they were in a delicate situation now. How to make the people of Wu believe that Kui Ji's assassination has nothing to do with Zhao is a problem.

Fu Chou assists Da Niu and the others in rescuing Zheng Yuming, and turns to look at Kui Ji, who has fallen in a pool of blood, and associates Kui Ji's delivery of the letter to him, and then links Kui Ji's behavior with the hints of his mentor in the letter, Fu Chou seems to see the truth of the matter.

"Fu-chou... Sonny boy! Coward! Ungrateful!"

While people were focusing on Zheng Yuming's injuries, Kui Ji, who had fallen in a pool of blood on the pavilion floor, suddenly struggled to get up and half-sat, his fingers pointing to Fu Chou, his eyes containing poisonous intent. The guards at the pavilion raised their weapons and surrounded Kui Ji, but they did not stab Kui Ji because they wanted to find out who was behind the attack.

"You were sent by the Tang King!" Fu Chou blurted out.

Kui Ji is back to the light, he woke up to see Zheng Yuming is still alive, he knows that his ideal is not successful one day, see staring at his Fu Chou, Kui Ji failed to vent all the anger to Fu Chou, if Fu Chou to assist him in the assassination, Kui Ji thinks that his assassination will be able to succeed, will not be like today such a hasty action.

"Fu Chou Junior! Have you forgotten what your master taught you? Your teacher is the King of Tang's trustworthy minister, and you are also carrying your father's hatred, but the little boy even forgets his father's hatred and serves his enemy, you are an unrighteous person..." Kui Ji used his last strength to shout at Fu Chou, and before he finished shouting, his mouth spat out a large mouthful of blood, and it looked like he was going to die.

Fu Chou's place of internship is the refugee camp in Nambu. What he saw and heard in the refugee camp in the past few days made Fu Chou understand more about the situation of the Tang and other lords' people, and what he saw and heard with his own eyes allowed him to see through the fact that his original revered monarch, the king of the Tang, who was described by his mentor as noble and majestic, was so bad.

Fu Chou tilted his head slightly and straightened his back, his face pale but his eyes firm: "I love my teacher, but I love morality even more! I love my teacher, but I love morality even more! The Duke's benevolence is for the people, and the people have benefited a lot from it! Chou is not loyal to the Prince, but to the people of the world! Chou is not a person like you who can't distinguish between right and wrong!"

Kui Ji couldn't think of a reason to refute Fu Chou, the blood coming out of his mouth prevented him from pronouncing his words, Kui Ji's throat 'ho ho ho' a few times, his body went into convulsions, his legs stomped, his eyes rounded, and he collapsed softly on the ground, with no more air coming out of the tip of his nose. The guards surrounding Kui Ji did not dare to relax their vigilance, still holding weapons aimed at Kui Ji's corpse.

The crowd by the pavilion building had different thoughts when they heard what Fu Chou and Kui Ji said. Zhao Xuan's officials were breathing a huge sigh of relief; Kui Ji's last few words finally let Zhao off the hook for the assassination.

Yang He looked at his schoolmate's face full of shock, he did not know that Fu Chou actually had a fatherly grudge against the two Zheng Chu.

Huo Sihua looked at the eyes of Fu Chou full of complaints, blame Fu Chou did not expose Kui Ji early, causing her favorite adults in danger, Huo Sihua originally Fu Chou has a very good feeling, and now there is Kui Ji this matter, Huo Sihua that a little bit of goodwill to fly to the sky.

Zheng Yuming's shoulder injury was not serious, but he suffered internal injuries from falling onto a branch, and when he was rescued, his chest ached, and when he heard Kui Ji's conversation with Fu Chou, and then looked at Zhao Xuan's juniors not too far away, he instantly gave orders to Ma Ren.

"Ma Ren, you are in charge of the Ulsan military, Ulsan administration is in charge of Yu Kui, blockade the assassination, send heavy troops to protect the Great King, no one is allowed to see the Great King until the Great General comes back, Ulsan is under military martial law, if there is any movement, no matter who it is, kill without pardon!" Zheng Yuming gasped when he said this, he had blackness in front of his eyes, while he was not unconscious, Zheng Yuming made arrangements in advance.

The reason why Zheng Yuming had his men take control of the situation and put Wu Wangsheng under house arrest was to beware of any unrest in the Wu Kingdom in case he died, and he wanted to leave everything in the Wu Kingdom to Chu Chao Hui.

Zheng Yuming handed Ma Ren the jade pendant at his waist, which was a fish-shaped jade pendant that King Wu had personally carved for Gong Zi Ming, and could represent Gong Zi Ming as well as Zheng Yuming.

"Put the Zhao Xuan monarchs under house arrest, all matters will be handled by the Great General when he returns." Zheng Yuming issued his final order to Ma Ren in a low voice before passing out.
