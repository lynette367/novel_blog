Chen Canaan said, "I dreamed that you told me that you were leaving for a faraway place and would never come back."

Qiao Wen was suddenly stunned, and after a moment, he curved his lips and smiled softly and laughed, "Brother Nan, I'm here."

He covered him with a thin blanket and lay down next to him, reaching out and carefully hugging his shoulders, "Nan, I'll hold you, I won't be cold if I hold you."

Chen Canaan leaned her head toward his neck and closed her eyes reassuringly.

Qiaowen silently gazed at his face, a sudden wave of fear welling up in his heart.

Almost, just a little, the young man before him had perished because of the plot he had disrupted by his arrival. He was but nineteen years old, and had done nothing evil, much less had time to enjoy the pleasures of money, and did not deserve such a fate.

Luckily.

Originally this was a sad scene, but because Xiao Qiao in the dream, always with a gentle smile on her face, even the usual sadness between her eyebrows disappeared, apparently with gladness to say goodbye to him.

So this dream doesn't seem to be a nightmare.

So Chen Canaan couldn't be saddened by this, just couldn't say whether it was loss or disappointment, and finally unified into bewilderment.

He opened his eyes in this great daze, jerked his head up and heaved a sigh of relief at the sight of the familiar back in the doorway a few steps away.

Little Joe is really still around.

What happened after that, he barely remembered.

This bandaging skill is far better than Uncle Wah's Mongolian doctor.
