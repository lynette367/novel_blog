Of course, at this time, Zhou Renjun should still be just a small police officer who has just joined the profession.

Although Hong Kong City in the 1970s was in the midst of an economic boom, under colonial rule, the entire social order was in chaos, with capitalists making huge fortunes and ordinary people living in hardship. The police force is corrupt and acts as an umbrella for the gangs, and the black forces run amok.

It was not until the ICAC was set up in 74 and the black police officers were all brought down that the tumors were gradually purged.

In this general context, Zhou Renjun can be said to be an anomaly.

His family is well off and he is a real rich young man, when the police is entirely because of the sense of justice, the ideal is to fight against the black forces to return the people of Hong Kong peace, and has been committed to pulling out the tumors in the Kowloon Walled City. In the original book in 74 years, three thousand police force into the walled city to sweep the black, is he played the vanguard, Zhao Shanhai is also dead under his gun.

For such an idealistic and ambitious police officer with a strong sense of justice to show up at a gambling stall in Kowloon Walled City, it is obvious that he cannot really be here to gamble.

I'm afraid it's just to come and feel out the current state of the place.

However, it is still early for the ICAC to be set up, and this mapping exercise of his should only be a personal act.

Qiaowen suddenly thought of Zhao Shanhai.

He was hoping that Chen Canaan could quit the He Xing Society earlier, but according to the rules of the society, if you quit the society, you have to suffer three knives and six holes, and you have to be crippled even if you don't die. In this way, even if he regained his freedom, he would not have a good life in the future.

The one remaining method is to bring down Master Panther and dismantle the Wo Xing Society, which will ensure Chen Canaan's safe disembarkation, and also allow the common people of the city fortress to stop suffering from exploitation.

He looked at the two young men who were chatting as if they had known each other at first sight, and suddenly found it interesting.

In the book, the two are completely enemies and deadly rivals, I did not expect this butterfly effect of his own, but let Chen Canaan become Zhou Renjun's benefactor, and there is a connection in advance.

And looking at Zhou Renjun's attitude towards Chen Canaan at the moment, it was likely that he was trying to rope him in to help himself.

If that's the case, maybe we don't have to wait until two years from now.

Just as he was thinking about it, those two had talked and even made a date to come back and play again next time.

As Qiao Wen had expected, Zhou Renjun was indeed not specifically here to gamble, but was privately here to probe the Triad's crimes in the Walled City. For the Wo Hing Society and Zhao Shan Hai, he has long heard about it, but helplessly Kowloon Walled City is a unregulated area, the police can not control here. So although he knows that this place is overrun with gambling and drugs, and that Zhao Shanhai and the Wo Xing Society are doing all sorts of evil, he can't do anything about them.

Although he was still only a junior policeman who had joined the force for one year, his ideal was to knock down all criminal organizations that endangered the stability of Hong Kong, which naturally included the Wo Hing Society in the Kowloon Walled City. And he always believed that it wouldn't be long before the Hong Kong government ordered the removal of the cancerous tumors in the Walled City, so that the sunshine could shine into this dark city of sin.

As expected, the gangs here were indeed arrogant, and if he hadn't encountered this brother of the casino who had stepped in to help him, he could have really suffered a big loss.

Of course, his decision to make Chen Canaan a friend was not just because of his outpouring of help, but in order to investigate the crime situation here and find enough evidence to knock out Zhao Shanhai and the Wo Xing Society, there had to be an insider informant to come inside and out.

He was really lucky today, meeting a perfect candidate on his first visit.

Zhou Renjun today's hand is explosive, and know the members of the Wo Xing Society, self-conscious that the identity is well hidden, pulling out the tumor of the Kowloon Walled City is just around the corner, and when he said goodbye to Chen Canaan, the curves of the corners of his mouth are just short of grinning to the temples, and he feels that his dream of being a hero is just around the corner.

It was also not until then that he noticed Qiao Wen beside Chen Canaan, probably because he felt that such a clean and beautiful young man should not appear in the mixed gambling stalls, and with a flash of consternation on his face, he smiled at the person again.

Chen Canaan was very pleased to see his face enjoying himself.

"No, although I can't resist, if he curses me, I'll curse him back a hundred times in my heart."

"He asked about what happened here that evening?"

Jovian continued, "I'm guessing this person is someone close to your Abba and has an unusual relationship."
