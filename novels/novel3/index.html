Whispers of the Manor

✍️ Author: Anonymous 🌐 Translator: Cross The Line 📅 Updated: Oct 25, 2025
Romance

Set in Victorian England, a mysterious tale unfolds in an isolated manor. Lady Catherine discovers secrets hidden within the estate's walls, leading her down a path of intrigue, romance, and shocking revelations about her own family's past. This compelling Asian pop novel explores themes of mystery, forbidden love, and the weight of family secrets.
