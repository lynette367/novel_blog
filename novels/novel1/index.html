Rosemary and the Silent Heir

✍️ Author: Anonymous 🌐 Translator: Cross The Line 📅 Updated: Oct 25, 2025
Epic Power

A historical romance set in the Edwardian era—love, betrayal, and redemption intertwine in a tale of forbidden passion. When Rosemary, a spirited governess, meets the enigmatic heir of a crumbling estate, their worlds collide in ways neither could have imagined. This compelling Asian pop novel explores themes of forbidden romance, social class boundaries, and the courage to follow one's heart.
