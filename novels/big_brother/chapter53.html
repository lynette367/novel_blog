Unfortunately, it didn't take long for all of these people to cave in - because Old Bear did something particularly out of character.

He divided his family's wealth, his own parents were richer than him and didn't have to worry about it, so the old bear split his property in two, leaving half to Chen Lu's parents and half to a temple on the outskirts of the city, and then shaved his own head and went in to become a monk.

It is said that because of its outstanding economic contribution to our Buddha, the old bear went in and worshiped directly under the abbot, becoming a late entry, seniority of the closed disciple.

So many years have passed, and he's actually back to being the same big douchebag who hiked up and down the mountainous regions in the high altitude and lack of oxygen to buy pots and pans.

And then later ......

Wei Qian parked the car and dragged the newly bought big suitcase out from the trunk. The suitcase had already been filled with some things, all of which he thought he needed, the suitcase felt good to carry, it was very loadable, it wasn't heavy, it looked sturdy, and it looked good - of course it was good, Weiqian picked half a dozen of them before picking out the most expensive one like this one.

If he personally needs something, he walks into a store and ends up buying away from one of the moderately or even moderately inexpensive ones. To be honest, if it wasn't for his own essence and appearance, when others saw this young man, 80% of them would think that he was either an insurance seller or a salesman.

He also still drove his battered little McLaren, so whenever he needed to go out to meet people, he had to replace his transportation with the company car so as not to look too shabby when he was seen.

The suitcase certainly wasn't something he'd spare for himself, Wei Qian carried it all the way upstairs and left it at Wei Zhiyuan's door, reached out and knocked on the door to get the attention of the people in the house with their backs turned to him before turning around and walking away without saying a word.

Wei Zhiyuan looked back, his brother had already left, and not far away came a slamming of the door.

He stood up and silently dragged the suitcase into the house, reaching out and rubbing the handle of the suitcase. And then, after a moment's hesitation, he walked to Wei Qian's door and stood still for a long time as if he were being punished, raising and lowering his hand three times as he tried to knock.

After that glorious annual meeting, the two of them had been in this state - Wei Qian still did everything he could for Wei Zhiyuan, but kept treating him like air, and if he had to talk to him, he would be as brief as a telegram to conserve the environment and never look him in the eye.

Originally, according to Wei Qian's usual temper, he would have been furious.

Wei Zhiyuan was punched by him at that time to wake up the wine, and thought that he would be beaten up next, and on the way home, he even thought that Wei Qian might cut off relations with him and never talk to each other again.

But none of it.

The series of events that happened afterward left both of them drained, and Wei Qian didn't have the time or energy to beat him up.

As for the worst ending Wei Zhiyuan conceived ...... he realized with hindsight that he had underestimated his brother s feelings, even though they were not the kind of feelings he wanted.

Late at night, Wei Zhiyuan will be no reason to introspection and reflection, he found that "cut off, jade and stone" and so on, is the only thing that can be done out of the big brother's heart, but where there is still a little bit of emotional sustenance, he will never come to that point.

Wei Qian's love and favor for his younger siblings were all in the immobility of day after day, becoming something almost as imperceptible as the background color, and now, instead, it was brought out in such a resistant attitude.

Wei Zhiyuan felt some kind of lingering attachment to himself - it was time for him to go.

Leaving was not his idea, is a day, Wei Qian printed out the enrollment information of several famous foreign schools, along with a card with money deposited in front of Wei Zhiyuan, and did not mention anything, everything is in the words: you look after yourself.

A year later, Wei Zhiyuan had completed his application and a series of formalities, and he was about to leave on the next day's plane with his acceptance letter, flying to an unfamiliar country more than ten hours away.

The person he loves is on the other side of the globe, and the long time difference makes the ancient saying of "sharing the same love for a thousand miles" an impossible illusion.

Wei Zhiyuan ended up not alerting Wei Qian, and he quietly went out alone.

He sat aimlessly on the bus, walking through the streets and lanes of the entire city, this place is so different from more than ten years ago, at first glance, the change is almost completely different, at that time, Wei Zhiyuan did not think that this place will end his wandering.

...... Later on, it didn't occur to him that this turned out not to be his last stop.

Wei Zhiyuan did not know how far he had traveled by car, the bus drove all the way to the terminal, and he was the only passenger left in the downtown compartment where people can be squeezed into photos.

The steward gave the young passenger a strange look and came over to remind him, "Young man, it's the end of the line, get off."

Only then did Wei Zhiyuan wake up as if from a dream, and muddledly got out of the car in an unfamiliar place.

Sometimes the outskirts of the city are as far away as the next county, Wei Zhiyuan first did not react to this place, he stood on the side of the road for a while, saw an illegal "one-day tour" group. The tour guide was holding a small red flag and leading the way in front of him, followed by a row of tourists who were as tired as dogs.

There were just a few words of the narration that drifted into Wei Zhiyuan's ears, and he heard the name of a certain temple, and only after a while did he remember that this seemed to be the place where the old bear had gone to his home.

Wei Zhiyuan does not know out of what state of mind, followed the group of tourists all the way to the entrance of the temple, he originally just wanted to take a look, did not expect to meet the old bear, did not expect to see the goods at the ticket booth.

I saw the old bear with a big naked bald lady, clad in a robe, one hand to collect money and the other hand to pass the ticket, but also did not forget to spit on the tourists to sell some: "Do you want to buy incense? This temple wishing very good - want to ask for peach blossoms of the female masters please!
