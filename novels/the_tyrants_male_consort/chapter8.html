📖 Est. 10 min read

Xue Yao's mother didn't have the same balm, but he promised Princess Shio that he would bring it to meet her tomorrow.

Because when the party was in shock, his mind was spinning fast, and he inadvertently noticed that he seemed to possess a special ability-accessing plot details from the original story.

Previously he had wondered about this, it was just a cool novel, so why did he remember the plot so well.

He had recalled some of the episodes before, the general passage was easy to think of, but when it came to the detailed episodes it would be very laborious, and the more detailed the episodes were, the more laborious it would be to recall them.

Just now, because of the shock, he subconsciously followed the plot of the abortive death of Princess Shi Fei's daughter to deepen his memories, and the details of this episode immediately appeared in his mind: when Princess Tong's party conspired, they copied down the formula of the spices for this scented capsule.

Xue Yao was even able to search his memory to find the specific proportions and portions of the herbs on the recipe.

Accessing the memory of the details of this episode made Xue Yao feel dizzy.

It might be a special ability that required the consumption of mental energy, and he could even access details of events not written in the novel.

After Xue Yao excused himself, he didn't return to his residence directly, intending to create a packet of the same spice before nightfall according to the recipe he had acquired in his head, and bring it to Consort Shi for delivery tomorrow.

The secret recipe material of the scented capsule is not a rare and precious medicinal material, but only the matching ratio and processing technique is special, which can be bought in the pharmacy.

He could get the guys at the pharmacy to help with the preparation and processing.

In addition, the Xue family has a cousin who has opened a spice store in the back street of the Xue residence, and any ingredients other than the secret recipe should be available in the spice store.

This scented pouch does not require chemical synthesis, as long as the ingredients are polished to a fine powder, according to the proportion of the preparation can be, and is not time-consuming, you can ask relatives store fellows to help as soon as possible to make.

After thinking of a plan, Xue Yao went to the medicine store first.

The medicine store's fellows were nimble, grinding the herbs according to Xue Yao's requirements, followed by blending, cooking, roasting, and packing to completion.

It took half an hour, but even the handmade fee was confiscated, and only three hundred Wen herb fee was charged, the price of folk commodities is really fair.

Immediately afterward, Xue Yao took the secret recipe and went to the spice store.

The spice store fellow knew Xue Yao and greeted him with a smile as soon as they met, saying that the spices ordered by the third house hadn't been delivered yet and would have to be picked up again in a couple of days.

Xue Yao then went down the slope, pretending that he had come to run an errand for his wife, and told the fellow, "My wife has obtained a new spice recipe, the materials are all readily available, and you should have the ingredients here as well, but my wife wants it in a hurry, so I'm going to trouble the master of the spice mixer to match it up tonight."

With that, he handed the fellow the recipe sheet.

Xue Yao had asked the pharmacy staff to transcribe the recipe, and the proportion and weight of the ingredients and the preparation process were all written in great detail.

Seeing that the word "secret recipe" appeared several times in the recipe, he asked Xue Yao what the secret recipe referred to.

Xue Yao handed him the prepared medicine packet and said, "This is the secret formula that the wife bought for a lot of money, it has to be concocted while it's fresh, it's urgent."

The fellow replied back, "No problem, the ingredients on the list are all available in our store, the second master will come to take a look tomorrow and we will rush to prepare it as soon as possible."

Xue Yao pulled out a bunch of copper plates and handed them to the fellow, emphasizing once again that "the wife is in a hurry, so it must be matched first thing in the morning."

The fellow received the reward money with a joyful smile and promised to do it.

Early in the morning of the second day, Xue Yao fetched the matched spices, and after smelling the odor, there was almost no difference, so he went to the palace with full confidence.

Princess Shi Shi had smelled the scented pouch and knew that Xue Yao had not lied.

She slowly lowered her hand, not looking angry, but only staring helplessly at the grass and trees outside the temple.

The only trusted sister in the palace is trying to harm her.

Thinking of Consort Tong bringing her that kind of attentive care on weekdays, Consort Shio slowly sucked in a breath, tears falling downwards like broken beads.

Xue Yao knew why she was sad, but the drama had to be done, so he could only pretend to be surprised and inquire, "What's wrong with the Empress? Does it taste different?"

The courtesan on the side shook her head at him to keep quiet.

Xue Yao immediately lowered his head and took two steps back.

The palace maid took the bag of incense that Consort Tong had given her from Consort Shi and gasped, "The slave girl will go and burn this labor!"

Concubine Shih shook her head with tears in her eyes, "We can't burn it, this incense bag has to be kept, or else she will find out, I don't know what method she will think of to harm me, let's just pretend that we don't know, and change the medicinal herbs in it."

The palace maid nodded her head and responded, seeing that Consort Shi was very sad, she advised in a low voice, "Your Highness need not be sad, you have His Majesty's favor, so why should you care about the sincerity of that upstart villain?"

Concubine Shih looked miserable and said, "How long can His Majesty's favor last? I am ultimately a lonely life."

She took a deep breath, looked sadly sideways at Xue Yao, and held out her hand, "Good boy, come here."

Princess Shih lifted her hands and feet, all with a special flavor, the corners of her eyes were still hanging with teardrops, her sad face was nevertheless immortal, and with those perfectly contoured features, it really made Xue Yao see a true classical beauty.

No wonder there were still a bunch of villains hitting on Princess Shio twenty years later, this woman was truly beautiful to the bone.

Before Xue Yao crossed over and lived into his twenties, he had never blushed at anyone, but at this moment, his heart was actually racing at Princess Shio.

However, when he thought that the cannon fodder who coveted Consort Shi's beauty had all been lynched by King Ning, Xue Yao immediately became purified and stepped forward with righteousness.

Consort Shio grabbed his hand and said in a warm voice, "It's rare that you're willing to tell me the truth, this time you've not only done me a favor, but also helped me recognize people's hearts, what kind of reward do you want?"

Xue Yao pretended not to understand her words and said in surprise, "What favor? I'm just being honest, the incense your mother uses is indeed the same as my mother's, and I really like the smell."

Concubine Shih hooked her lips and smiled, as if breaking through the ice and blooming in spring, a pair of teary peach blossom eyes looked into Xue Yao's eyes and asked: ''What's your name? Who are your parents? What do you do for a living?"

Xue Yao answered one by one.

Hearing this, Princess Shio asked him again, "Would you like to come and compensate Old Seven for studying together?"

Xue Yao was startled.

Is this an invitation for him to throw his hat into the ring of the ultimate golden thigh!

Wouldn't he have gone from villain to villain then?

Ears and tail saved?

After a brief moment of excitement, he calmed down again.

It won't work.

Before the Seventh Prince came of age, he encountered so many disasters that almost all the decent friends around him died.

The cannon fodder who took the knife and the pot for the male lead could circle the earth ten times hand in hand!

Xue Yao was well aware of the original plot, before the Seventh Prince came of age, the Third Prince and the Great Prince were the safest golden thighs, and he could not shift his position for the time being.

When the palace maid saw his hesitation, she was just about to scold him when she was stopped by Consort Shio raising her hand.

Concubine Shi still looked at Xue Yao gently, "This palace likes a child like you who has no city, if you are willing to come to the Green Vault Palace, I can give you three times the monthly allowance as it is."

Although Consort Shio is currently not in power, but after all, she is the mother consort of Long Ao Tian's male protagonist, Xue Yao could not afford to be offended, and hurriedly replied back, "Little man would also like to serve Consort Shio and His Highness the Seventh Prince, but only, this matter needs to be discussed with the father of the family first."

A slight hint of disappointment passed across Princess Shio's face.

Xue Yao had just said that his father was an official of the Ministry of Rites, so surely he wouldn't be willing to let his son come and serve as an attendant to her family's oldest seven.

Full of civil and military, who does not know that her family old seven does not have the life of the Crown Prince, to defect, will also only defect to the most promising great prince.

Even if you burn a cold stove, you should also defect to the Third Prince, in any case, there is no part of her family's old seven, only to blame her as a mother is a foreigner.

Although Consort Shi was disappointed, she didn't blame Xue Yao and had the palace maid give him a red envelope as a thank you gift.

After leaving the Green Vault Hall, Xue Yao curiously unwrapped the red packet and found that inside was a silver ticket with a face value of fifty taels.

According to the price of goods in Daqi, this is equivalent to more than thirty thousand Renminbi!

The fairies are really generous.

Carrying a huge sum of money and ready to leave the palace, halfway there, I heard another beep ringing in my head: [Whitewash Points +95]

I guess the courtesan took care of the spices in that scented bag.

Xue Yao did the math, 95 whitewashing points, 20 points more than saving the Xue family's old lady before.

Is it true that you can only get 75 whitewash points for indirectly saving a human life?

Currently, the total number of whitewash points was 167, and opening the food mall required 130 points to be consumed, so he still had 37 points left, which could be used to exchange for food.

Xue Yao was a bit excited, wondering if he could redeem processed foods from his own time, such as chili fries or something.

He couldn't wait to speed up his pace and get home to try it.

When he passed through the east gate of Huaxing Yuan, he saw two familiar-looking eunuchs walking head-on.

Eunuchs on duty in the palace could not just greet each other, and Xue Yao did not carefully recognize them, preparing to brush past them, but the two men arrow-stepped and blocked their way.

The tall eunuch greeted him in a shrill voice, "Second Master Yao, Empress Tong has invited you to the palace to catch up, come with us."

Xue Yao froze, Tong Fei invited him ...... to catch up?

Princess Tong doesn't know him at all?

Is it possible that there's a mole in the inner circle around Princess Shio?

Then I'm afraid that all of his actions in Consort Shi's palace were known by Consort Tong!

Xue Yao's heart suddenly went cold.

The secret formula in that scented capsule was specially formulated by Princess Tong to find a magician, which was not available in the folklore at all, but Xue Yao lied that her mother had the same scented capsule and could not wear it because she was pregnant.

This is obviously knowing the secret of this balm!

What's the explanation?

"Second Master?" The eunuch called out to him, "Don't be nervous, the Empress just asked you to ask a few questions."

"Oh ...... well." Xue Yao barely regained his composure.

No matter what, it was always impossible for Consort Tong to kill and exterminate, Xue Yao was the son of a court official.

But in this case, he betrayed Consort Tong, the third prince side, I'm afraid to stay.

With Xue Qiong by the side of the Grand Prince, it was unlikely that Mrs. Chen would allow her lord to find a doorway to help Xue Yao mix up the quota.

Xue Yao laughed bitterly, now I'm afraid that I really have to go and serve as an attendant to the Seventh Prince.

Previously, by relying on the credit of protecting the old lady, Xue Yao had managed to make a bit of a name for himself in the Xue residence, but now if he ran off to serve as a reader for the seventh prince, who was less than four years old, he would surely become the laughing stock of the entire residence.

Especially Mrs. Chen and her son, Xue Qiong, are afraid that their mouths will be crooked with laughter ......

After all, no one knew that the fleshy seventh prince would seize the throne and look out of the world in the future.
