Although the matter of grain collection was successfully resolved, Xue Yao's heart was still unsettled.

With only a year and a half left before the crown prince was deposed in the original, it always seemed that something else unforeseen would happen during that time.

The dynasty was at peace, the officials involved in the trial change had already left for the local institutions, and everything was functioning normally under the new policy of lending and releasing grain.

As far as Xue Yao remembered, the Crown Prince was abolished by the impeachment of the speech officials.

Impeachment mainly has two contents: one is the change of law in the trial county of the people, starvation; second is the appointment of officials secretly increase the interest rate of borrowing, reported to the emperor's account is the surplus and deficit.

The meaning is simple: first, the Prince is suspected of enriching himself, and second, the loan has led to mass starvation of the local people.

Xue Yao speculated that the Prince's profit and loss was offset because he was set up during the grain collection period and bought high priced grain, and the first year's borrowing would not have earned much, and it was normal that there was no profit.

As for the claim that the change of law led to starvation, it makes it incomprehensible.

Xue Yao's memory wasn't too good, but he had long since realized that as long as he concentrated, he could recall the details of any plot mentioned in the original, which must be a special ability given to him by the system.

In the episode where the crown prince was abolished, the original only recorded the content of the impeachment zhengben of the speech officer, no detailed records of what happened in the affected counties, there is no way to find out why a lot of ordinary people died of starvation.

The story of the mass starvation of the common people should not be a false accusation of rumor.

After all, although the Emperor was trifling with his wives, his most valued son was the Grand Prince from start to finish.

If things hadn't gotten to the point where a lot of people had died, the Crown Prince wouldn't have been deposed anyways.

Even if he lost all the one million taels of silver that the Emperor had shelled out, the Emperor would not abolish the Crown Prince.

Therefore, Xue Yao was worried that the people of the affected counties would experience sudden changes, especially that Ping Rong County.

There is a system mission to control the plague in Ping Rong County, so there is a possibility that a plague will start in Ping Rong County and spread to the two neighboring affected counties, resulting in the death of some of the common people.

This disaster, so that the people of the affected counties a large number of deaths, the Prince's lending naturally half can not be recovered, the loss on the books, may be filled by the Prince's own pocket, just barely break even.

And the old people who died of illness were said by the speech officials to have died of starvation, and all the sins were poured on the Crown Prince, which led to his eventual abolition.

But this guess doesn't quite make sense.

With the Emperor's regard for the Crown Prince, it was impossible for him to slap a conviction without verifying the cause of death.

After thinking about it, Xue Yao thought that it would be best to have the Crown Prince pay attention to the situation of the common people in Ping Rong County over the past two years, and look for some famous doctors to stay in the area, so as to prevent the spread of malignant diseases at any time.

However, this kind of proposal was made out of the blue, and would surely arouse suspicion, and in the event that the disease did spread in the future, the onlookers might even suspect Xue Yao of plotting and scheming.

Moreover, the Crown Prince was busy with his official duties, and Xue Yao, as the young prince's companion, was not in a position to go to the door alone to ask for an audience, so it would be best if he could drag the little chubby brat along with him.

For some time afterward, Xue Yao took some medical books from the Gap Space every night and studied them diligently.

From getting started to giving up.

Medicine is a subject that is not at all suited to improvisation!

The content is too heterogeneous, and the knowledge of different subjects varies by a hundred thousand miles, so it is not easy to get started with a basic ear, nose, and throat in five years, not to mention applying it in a practical way.

The dream of becoming a self-taught miracle healer is shattered!

Xue Yao gave up studying medicine and was so disturbed that when she couldn't sleep, she casually clicked on the Multidimensional Time Reference Line Mall to watch some boring images to help her sleep.

The images in the reference line mall are boring because you can't freely search for the plot of the character you want to see, you can only randomly refresh it.

If you want to search freely, you have to open the Ultimate Mall, and the condition for opening it is to complete the [Cold-Blooded Tyrant's Heartbeat] quest.

Obviously, with such an odd task, Xue Yao had already given up on treating it, so he just made do and watched.

Most of the random images that appear are of the everyday lives of the common people, working in the fields or simply sleeping at night.

That night, the image Xue Yao refreshed was of a simple bedroom, and on the bed, a white-haired old man wrapped in a quilt, coughing from time to time.

Not long after, the door was pushed open and a middle-aged couple walked in with an old gentleman dressed as a Langzhong, checking the pulse of the white-haired old man who was bedridden.

The Bone-setter fished out a small porcelain bottle from his traveling bag, poured a pill into his palm, picked up the old man, and personally fed him the pill.

The old man seemed to have difficulty swallowing, and the bone-setter patiently helped him to smooth out his breath, but the old man steeply spurted out a large mouthful of blood and coughed.

The groom's face was stained with blood, and the couple hurriedly handed over a towel to help the groom wipe it clean.

Looking at the image, Xue Yao's brows unconsciously furrowed.

I don't know what kind of disease the old man was suffering from, but the blood stains on the groom's face were even close to black, it looked very creepy, Xue Yao didn't dare to look at it anymore and hurriedly clicked refresh.

The image that followed appeared to be of what appeared to be a medical clinic, with an old gentleman sitting behind a long table, coughing uncontrollably.

Xue Yao took a closer look and realized that this old gentleman was, in fact, the Langzhong from the previous image.

The Langzhong in the image coughed more and more violently, pulling out a handkerchief from his sleeve to cover his mouth and nose, his body shuddered, and the handkerchief he covered his mouth with was gradually penetrated by blackened blood.

Xue Yao panicked and exited the mental space.

How do you refresh two such images in a row in the middle of the night?

After calming down, a sense of foreboding rose in Xue Yao's heart.

What was the system reminding him of?

From the old man to the groom, coughing, thick black blood.

Could this be the beginning of the plague in Ping Yung County?
