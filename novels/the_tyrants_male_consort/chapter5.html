Xue Yao's face turned white, he could already see the mood of this little guy who was impatient to ride the horse, knowing that it was not advisable to stay for long, he immediately took a step back and forcibly misinterpreted His Highness's meaning, "I understand, within three days, I will definitely fix Your Highness's wooden horse and return it as it is!"

His Highness wants to ride a real horse!

But the Seventh Prince was ultimately young, and although his memory was unbelievable, he was only good at repeating other people's words, and trying to express his own meaning didn't go down too well.

What's more, he was in a hurry to ride at the moment, and when he saw that this boy "misunderstood" his own meaning, he was even more anxious to say nothing.

Xue Yao where dare to wait for this dragon Ao Tian cub to say the order, in a hurry and panic, turned his head, toward the courtesan arched his hand and bowed: "Please aunt to point out the way, I will move the wooden horse to the repair, at most three days, will certainly be returned in the same condition!"

Hui service palace maid is the seventh prince's personal palace maid, bystanders respectfully called her "Yiqiu aunt".

Yiqiu at the moment face hung a cold smile, thinking that this child looks less than ten years old, the mind is sharp.

She was watching from the sidelines, and she knew all about Xue Yao's little thoughts.

So, Yiqiu smiled without slowing down: "The wooden horse has fallen, you take it to repair, you have to glue, glued together, there will be cracks, how can there be the possibility of returning it as it is? I think you are deceiving our little master's young age, don't be so perfunctory!"

Xue Yao's heart thumped, bowed low and said, "Auntie don't worry, I've already discussed with my friends in the Inner Officials Supervision, not only will the wooden horse be glued and repaired, it will also be brushed with new paint, so that half a crack will definitely not be visible."

Yiqiu remained gloomy: "Just because you can't see it doesn't mean it's not there."

At this time, the Seventh Prince, who had been holding back for half a day, finally expressed his wish: "No small wooden horse, master wants to ride a big horse!"

Xue Yao was originally in an apprehensive mood, but was amused by the little fat boy's cry of "Master", a three year old doll calling himself "Master" is a bit of an anticlimactic and silly feeling.

Once Yi Qiu heard that His Highness was making things difficult for Xue Yao with this request, she immediately stood as a wall and stared at Xue Yao with a sneer.

Xue Yao can see that this palace maid intends to make things difficult for herself, lowered her head, her eyes slightly flowed, raised her head calmly and self-containedly arched her hand towards His Highness, "Your Highness would like to ride a real horse, the villain should be on the sidelines to watch over the horses, the horses can be borrowed from the Third Prince, they are all readily available, just waiting for aunt to arrange for Your Highness to spare some free time, the villain is always ready to wait."

Yiqiu was shocked at his words and did not hesitate to rebuke him: ''Daring! Your Highness is young, how can you ride a horse? If something happens, can you afford it?"

Xue Yao was not panicked and looked at the courtesan with a smile on his face, "I previously touched His Highness's wooden horse and could not absolve myself of the blame, but now that His Highness has given me the opportunity to make up for it, I will remove all the difficulties and put my life on the line to protect the driver, and as long as I can win His Highness's heart, the rules are not what you say they should be."

With a twist of his head, the Seventh Prince's expectant gaze shifted to Yi Qiu.

Yiqiu suddenly turned pale, not realizing that she, who was in her twenties, had actually been put on the spot by this eight year old!

If Xue Yao can't coax the Seventh Prince, he will be disqualified from serving as a reader, and anyway, promising the Seventh Prince a horseback ride is not likely to be realized, he is now a dead pig in a poke, he dumped the pot himself and pretended to say yes, and the pot naturally went to Yichu's head, so how else could she watch the good show?

Although she wanted to villainize the Third Prince's attendants, at the moment it was barefoot and not afraid of wearing shoes, if Xue Yao took it a step further by promising the Seventh Prince that she would ride a horse and make the decision her own, then she would be the only one to end up being the villain.

She couldn't afford to eat this loss, her face changed, piled up a smile, bent down and picked up the seventh prince, smiling: ''What time is it? Is His Highness hungry? The slave girl will take the palace down to find a nurse, okay?"

Xue Yao on the slope of the donkey, not waiting for the seventh prince to respond, turned around and followed the little eunuch slipped, stepped out of the flower hall threshold, heard behind the seventh prince milky voice an angry rebuke: "To the master to chase!"

Xue Yao scampered away!

This door-to-door apology trip was less than ideal.

Unexpectedly, what the Seventh Prince held a grudge against was not that the Trojan horse was damaged, but that Xue Yao had gone back on his word.

I think the Dragon Ao Tian cub looked at his wooden horse with broken ears and tail, and was very depressed in his heart, but only realized in retrospect that it must be more fun to ride a real horse, so he waited every day for Xue Yao, who made a promise, to come to his door and take him to ride a real horse.

If the seventh prince accidentally fall out of a good or bad, not to mention the qualification of the readers lost, this Daqi is the king of the law of the nine clans waiting for him!

It shouldn't be difficult to repair it, let's go to the Inner Government Supervisor and ask the best craftsman to repair it!"
