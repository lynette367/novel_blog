The original text doesn't describe much about Princess Shio's character, the general impression is that she's an uncontested fairy.

The episodes about her are mainly depicting how beautiful she is, so much so that after the Emperor's death, King Ning went to his fiefdom and actually kept hearing about people in the palace hitting on his own mother, Concubine Shio.
