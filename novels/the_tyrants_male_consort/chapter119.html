Azhu's fleeing back makes Xue Yao lose hope of completing the heartwarming mission.

Calm was restored to the yard.

Xue Yao looked back at Lu Qian, taking the initiative to soften, "We shouldn't fight in a place like this."

Lu Qian looked down at his little companion and spoke majestically, "You stepped on the organ."

Xue Yao couldn't deny it and muttered in a low voice, "This is indeed an evil sect, actually arranging such insidious organs."

Lu Qian didn't say anything, staring at the little companion as if waiting for something.

Xue Yao avoided his gaze and distracted the other in a low voice: "I'm wondering if they poisoned the chicken legs, I feel a bit dizzy now."

Lu Qian remained staring at him.

Still trying to escape, Xue Yao twisted her body and said, "Let's go back to the house, at least it's safer than here."

Lu Qian didn't move a muscle.

Xue Yao was unable to avoid it and met the pupils of those teal eyes.

A silent contest, which began when the two brothers looked at each other.

Xue Yao knew very well: if he couldn't wait for those words, this careful-eyed Long Ao Tian wouldn't let himself go.

Okay.

Xue Yao's gaze, pretending that nothing had happened, dropped inch by inch, her eyes finally resting on the tip of Lu Qian's chin, whispering, "Thank you, Your Highness."

The identity of the protector was recognized, Lu Qian hooked the corner of his lips, lazily propped up on the ground and stood up, reaching out to the little companion in need of protection.

How much does a boy that age want to prove his power?

Especially if you want to prove to someone that you have all the basics to protect them, that you have more than enough to protect them with your own strength.

Therefore, Lu Qian hid his cowardice that he almost cried in fear the moment he saw the organ being triggered, and reached out to help his little companion with a gesture of more than enough strength.

He exerted all his strength, silently doing his best to show an impatient inquiry--Yao Yao look, is Master more powerful than Big Brother?

Subconsciously, Lu Qian knew that he couldn't compare to his big brother, which made him even more eager to prove himself.

Ten years later, Lu Qian would instead try to hide his sharpness by keeping a low profile as much as possible, but the self-confidence that came from that composure and self-knowledge was a state of mind that the seventeen year old Lu Qian couldn't reach.

On the contrary, at this moment, Xue Yao had the mentality of an elder, and refused to give up his position as protector to fulfill the self-esteem of the boy in front of him who was eager to become a man.

The two of them are still secretly fighting.

That's why Xue Yao didn't put her hand into Lu Qian's palm, but instead propped herself up on the ground, to be strong enough to get up on her own, and turned around to walk towards the veranda.

Lu Qian's careful eyes began to play tricks again, he badgered at the back of the little companion read to be strong, his mouth suddenly let out a "bang!" , pretending to be the sound of a trap being triggered again.

Xue Yao jumped in place like a little rabbit in fear, turned around and actively wrapped her arms around Lu Qian's neck, looking around in horror!

The heartbeats of the two men dovetailed in a moment of embrace.

The heart was beating so joyfully that Xue Yao's ears began to ring.

The ringing in his ears drowned out that [Tyrant's Heartbeat Quest Progress 21%] alert.

It was only a few moments later that he realized he hadn't stepped on the mechanism again, and that the noise was Lu Qian's way of scaring him.

"Why are you scaring me!" Xue Yao, who had regained her senses, let go of Lu Qian, dropped a complaining look, turned around and ran into the house.

Xue Yao didn't understand Lu Qian's rebellious mischief, although this body was only twenty-one years old, he had lived until he went to college in his last life, and added thirteen years of Royal Kindergarten life in this life, thinking that he could have the experience of a thirty year old, not realizing that he similarly lacked the variety of a relationship he hadn't experienced before, in order to make his understanding of the world more complete.

"Miss Azhu won't help us slip away now." Xue Yao sat alone at the table in despondency.

"Does Your Highness want to be trapped here for the rest of your life?" Xue Yao said sharply, "Your fifth brother is about to get married!"

"The mob doesn't dare offend the Sword Saint."

"I'm not hungry, if they see the Sword Saint's letter of abandonment, they'll dare to kill us."

So, Lu Qian took out the letter from his sleeve pocket.

Xue Yao reached for it.

Xue Yao took the small edge of the triangle, thinking to himself that the rebel pup still had him in his heart, and was actually so considerate as to only give him a small slice at a time.

Take the small edge and don't turn your head, easing it into your mouth and chewing.

"Hmph!" Xue Yao turned his back to show his determination.

What do they want at this hour?

Late night snack delivery?

However, the person who entered was not carrying a food box in his hand.

"The gang leader invites you to come." The visitor looked unkind.

"This late?" Xue Yao didn't think it was a good time to meet.

"Please, both of you." The visitor had no intention of being polite with them.

The two followed the visitor out.

The man, who probably knew where there were traps in the courtyard, walked ahead and barked, "Follow me."

Walking out of the courtyard, I realized that it didn't look like some gang's territory, just a single family home in the mountains.

The Xuan Night Sect had an unconscionable secret cultivation technique that was able to suck the vital energy of virgins, so many elders of this sect secretly kept many young men, raising them for themselves to suck their vital energy after they were raised.

The wall with the painting on it "clucked" and slowly rose, revealing a secret passage to the basement.

Xue Yao: "......"

Sure enough, it's a cult configuration!
