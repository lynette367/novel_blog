The old man was stunned, and immediately thanked him profusely for untying the sack and showing the color of the rice to the Sixth Prince: "There is nothing wrong with our family's rice! It's possible for the boss to sell it as top quality rice, look at the color!"

Sixth prince cupped a handful, take a closer look, indeed crystal clear, think this price is the grain store's price of entry, is bound to be the lowest grain price, then joyfully ordered the eunuch: "Settle with him, this car of rice and grain, I'll take it all."
