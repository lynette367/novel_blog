Guifei's face was still facing in the direction of the southern passageway, but her eyes lazily slanted toward Lao San, still not responding.

Lao-san froze, staring blankly at Guifei.

The oldest grinned, as if relieved, and murmured, "That's good enough."

Your Highness looked at him coldly, "I'm sorry, Chongshan."

The oldest shook his head and laughed softly, "There's nothing to be sorry for."

It was a farewell of sorts, and your consort turned without looking back, striding out of the cell, and was about to turn the corner of the passage when she heard, as if in a trance, behind her, a low--

"Mother Consort, take care."

The soldier guarding the corner of the cell saw the teardrops hanging from the tip of your consort's chin gather into a good-sized one as she went out the door, wanting to fall.
