Prince said, "Zhejiang grain merchants aren't afraid that things are out of the ordinary in which there are frauds? The Prince was lost in thought.

Eunuch Liu reminded in a low voice, "Why don't we stay put and wear him out first. No matter what Beijing merchant he is, even if he has more power, he can't casually afford a million taels of silver."

"The problem is that we can't afford to spend it now." The Crown Prince exhaled a deep breath, "Half a month later, the peasants in most of the affected counties will be borrowing from landlords and rich merchants, and the effect of the new law trial will be minimal."

Eunuch Liu said nervously, "How about, in the name of the imperial court's disaster relief, we ask the Zhejiang merchants to put off their other business and sell the rice and grain to us at a thin profit."

"Do you want the new law to be reduced to a laughing stock?" The Prince was deep in thought.
