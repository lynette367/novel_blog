Gu Huaiqing in the capital mysterious, according to Hao Da Dao said, these days he has been out early and return late, do not know where to go. Jing Shao urged him to hurry up, but he always put off the day after tomorrow to shirk.

"Are you sure that person is the King of Huainan?" The Fourth Prince asked with furrowed brows as he heard the man's words.

"Since it is a thousand times true," the person who spoke is still wearing court dress, is standing in the palace corridor quietly said to the fourth prince, "yesterday he personally came to the door, contact for so many years, the micro-chen can be sure that he is Gu Huaiqing."

The Fourth Prince rolled his eyes, "Where does he live now?"

"I don't know," the man was a little difficult, Gu Huaiqing has always been cautious, even if he is a beloved subordinate, he may not know where he is, "but he told me that these days are in the capital, if there is something to look for him, go to the Drunken Immortal House in the city to leave a note."

The fourth prince heard, immediately plan on the mind, the king of Huainan without summoning private into the capital, catch him is a great merit. Although it is not a big crime, but the father is worried about not find his handle, now bribe the officials in the capital, privately into the capital to plot mischief, ironclad evidence, but it is a good opportunity. So, turned toward the imperial study room.

"What exactly are you doing in the capital?" Jing Shao looked at Gu Huaiqing, who was leisurely sipping wine across from him, and only felt his forehead veins popping.

Gu Huaiqing glanced at Jing Shao's fist that was about to be swung out and slowly and leisurely said, "To relieve the pain of lovesickness."

"Cut the bullshit," Jing Shao glared, "Come on, what the hell are you doing?"

"I'm at least your big brother, can't you show me some respect?" Gu Huaiqing knocked his wine glass onto the table and said angrily.

"You also know that you're my big brother, and you're still always hitting on Jun Qing!" Jing Shao also knocked his wine glass onto the table and said even more angrily.

Mu Hanzhang took a few packets of marinara and drilled through the peach forest, just to see the two in the pavilion start to stare at each other again, looking about to fight: "I just left for a while, why did you start arguing again?"

When the two saw him, they simultaneously changed into a smiling face and said in unison, "We're both talking about playing!" Then glared at each other.

Mu Hanyang looked at the two, could not help pursing his lips and laughing lightly, opened the oil paper bag in his hand. Because of Gu Huaiqing's special identity, it is not easy for others to know, so even Yun Song was also left outside the garden, bought something need Mu Hanyang personally go out to pick up.

"This is the marinara from the south of the city, Jing Shao likes it very much and always says that if big brother comes he must ask big brother to try it." Mu Hanzhang said as he loaded the things in the oil paper into the plate, arranging them neatly one by one.

"Hanyang is still so good at talking." Gu Huaiqing smiled and picked up a chicken wing.

"Big brother, stop in moderation!" Jing Shao crunched the chicken wings in his hand.

"Woohoo!" A black and yellow ball of fur suddenly scurried out of the forest, picking at Jing Shao's knee to make its way to the marinated chicken wings in his hand.

"Yoo-hoo, you guys even brought it?" Gu Huaiqing looked at the growing stronger tiger cub and teased it with the chicken wings in his hand.

"Being bored at home for fear of raising a cat, I brought it out for a walk." Mu Hanzhang reached out and rubbed that furry head, and Little Yellow rubbed against his palm, then turned back and bared his teeth at Gu Huaiqing.

"This garden is quite nice, but it's a pity that the peaches aren't ripe yet." Gu Huaiqing brought the chicken wing to the tiger's mouth, only to almost get his hand bitten.

Little Yellow seemed to be very hostile to Gu Huaiqing, and reached out its claws to scratch it when it didn't bite. Mu Hanzhang rubbed the back of its neck, which settled it down a bit, only a low muffled growl was still coming from its throat, seemingly ready to pounce at any moment.

"This garden is brother's, in half a month you'll be able to eat peaches." Jing Shao looked at a peach branch that reached into the pavilion, on it were several plush peaches, just small and green in appearance.

"This is Jing Chen's garden!" Gu Huaiqing froze at his words and looked around, the peach forest was lush and green, the grass on the ground was as neat as a blue wave of brocade, one could imagine what kind of bloom it was when the peach blossoms were in bloom, "Is he coming today?"

"I guess it will arrive in a while." Jing Shao picked up the wine pot on the table and poured a cup of green plum wine for his own princess.

Mu Hanzhang cups were different from the two men's, not the little bamboo cups made by the Qingmei girl herself, but the exquisite little wine cups made of suet jade.

"Second brother, although the wife is delicate, the difference is too obvious for you, isn't it?" Gu Huaiqing took a look at the bamboo cup with a furry rim in his hand, and then looked at the nearly transparent sheep's fat jade, and immediately felt that this big brother was doing a miserable job.

Jing Shao ignored him, Jun Qing will habitually fondle the things in his hand when he thinks about things, since last year was this bamboo stick stuck in his hand, he carries a small goat jade cup with him, when he meets no suitable cups, he will take it out and give it to his own consort to use. From then on, the small cup and balm box have become something that Jing Shao must carry with him.

Mu Hanzhang took that white jade goblet and only felt a warmth spread from his fingertips all the way to his heart.

"Woohoo!" Little Yellow suddenly yelped, leaving the pavilion and running a few steps, crouching low and hiding in the grass, pouting and stomping her hind paws as if she was about to pounce at any moment.

"Someone's coming?" Jing Shao glanced at Xiao Huang and stood up.

Gu Huaiqing sniffed and followed suit, not long after hearing two voices coming from the forest.

"The second imperial brother is quite elegant, with the peach blossoms falling and the peaches not yet ripe, he still has the pleasure to sip wine here?" The young tone of voice carried some undisguised arrogance, it was none other than the fourth prince Jing Yu!

"Just casually coming to take a look." The calm and pleasant voice was Rui Wang Jing Chen, only his voice was slightly higher than usual, obviously reminding the several people in the forest.

Oh no! Mu Hanzhang looked around, this garden could stop others, but it couldn't stop the Fourth Prince who had cheekily followed Jing Chen in. Although Jing Yu does not know Gu Huaiqing, but at first sight, seeing a stranger in the private garden of King Rui, will certainly cause him to suspect. In the past, the small mistake of sesame seeds will be the fourth prince will not let go, not to mention a stranger from an unknown source.

Seeing that he had already met the hem of the dark yellow royal prince's regular clothes, Jing Shao had a bright idea and whistled at Xiao Huang, taking the chicken wing in his hand and flinging it at Jing Yu's body.

"Woohoo!" The little tiger had always loved this game, and when he saw Jing Shao throwing out the delicious food, he immediately jumped three feet high, like a black and yellow arrow, and whooshed out.

"Ah!" Seeing the tiger that suddenly pounced out of the forest, the Fourth Prince lost his voice in fear, and suddenly he was struck in the face by something slippery, so horrified that he stumbled and tripped into a section of the protruding peach tree root, and at the same time, the ferocious tiger pounced over.

The fourth prince fell to the ground, and the already heavy tiger pounced on him, pressing down on both shoulders and opening his bloody mouth, full of fangs that had grown in, one sharp as the other.

"Help!" The fourth prince's face turned white with fear, when he saw the tiger open its mouth, biting down on his neck, struggling to strike.

"Don't move!" Jing Chen, who was on the side, suddenly spoke up, stopping the Fourth Prince's hand from pulling out his sword, but he saw that the tiger sought out a chicken wing in the nest of his neck, took it into his mouth, turned around, and ran away.

Jing Yu climbed up, and only then did she see that it was a two-foot-long, half-sized tiger, pouting its buttocks and running into the pavilion in the center of the forest, where the two Cheng Wang Fu Fu were standing.
