Jing Shao frowned, since the princess read the book of the sages since childhood, the art of war naturally never read, bring him to this also did not expect him to help in the battlefield, simply do not want to be separated from him only. "Warlord, what is this?" Zhao Meng looked at the fork drawn by Mu Hanzhang, unsure.

Jing Shao's eyes widened suddenly when he saw the symbol.

"This formation is called Punching Yoke," Mu Hanzhang said slowly, pointing the wooden stick at the corners of the fork, "the soldiers stand in four corners, still marching in a long snake, with the front, left, and right as fronts, both offensive and defensive."

"Wonderful, wonderful!" The Right Guard froze for a moment, then suddenly clapped his hands and shouted.

"This matter is very important to the battle of southwest, you guys these days more watch over, do not make any mess." Jing Shao snorted, apparently unwilling to talk more, a few people also have the sense to shut up.

Only after leaving the center tent did Mu Hanzhang ask suspiciously, "Where are we going tomorrow?"

"To find a secret treasure." Jing Shao hooked his lips and smiled mysteriously.

Mu Hanzhang glanced at him and turned around to head towards his own tent, while the prince behind him followed very consciously.

"How did you follow in again?" "Yeah? I don't even remember." Jing Shao scratched his head, these things have been more than twenty years for him, how could he still remember, he couldn't help but feel regretful, very curious about what the Jun Qing of his childhood looked like? He thought that he must have been white and tender, with a pair of big eyes as clear and penetrating as pearls. "What did I do then?"

"You ......" Mu Hanzhang thought of the situation when he was young and couldn't help but laugh out softly, "You want Mu Lingbao to give you as a horse to ride, he refused, you beat up and cried. Mu Hanzhang looked helplessly at the king who was acting as if he were a deserter, leaning into his arms and yawning a little, "Where are we going?"
