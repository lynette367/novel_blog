Poked a bamboo stick and handed it to the mouth of his own princess, Mu Hanzhang frowned, eating on the road is not what a gentleman does, it's very rude, but he didn't want to brush off his good intentions. Looked around, quickly opened his mouth to bite down the balls, crispy balls with fresh sauce, but surprisingly delicious. Mu Hanzhang couldn't help but bend the corners of his mouth.

This series of expressions naturally fell into Jing Shao's eyes, and he was about to tease a couple of times, when suddenly a déjà vu figure flashed out of the corner of his eye. Sure enough, when he returned to his original position, he saw three men surrounding Mu Hanzhang, his back to him could not see what he was doing, but look at the expression of his own princess, pursed lips look obviously angry. Mu Hanzhang's eyes widened, his mouth half-open as Jing Shao wrapped his arms around him, "Junqing, are you alright?"

"Little spoon, they ......" Mu Hanzhang looked at the three people who fell to the ground, the corner of his mouth twitched a little.

"How are you guys doing your job? See Wangfei being molested and still not come out?" Jing Shao scolded the two guards behind him, and looked down at the three "dengtu" on the ground, the two covering their stomachs seemed somewhat familiar, and the one who climbed up with one eye green seemed to be his great-uncle--Mu Lingbao?
