The fat lady glanced at him and grumbled in anger, "That deadbeat listens to me on everything, but this one refuses to listen, I said I'd let him take the martial arts exam, but he just doesn't want to go, he just knows how to kill a pig, he's really no good!"

Mu Hanzhang heard gently hooked lips, this lady looks rude, but actually very fine heart, she has long known that they are here to persuade Hao Da Dao, let him come in to wash the tiger, in fact, is to persuade them to stop insisting on: "Sister-in-law really is a sensible person, just now the world has changed, Hao family ancestor even if the former general, but the great-grandfather has always been praised for it, but also did not Hao family designated as a rebel. And Hao big brother he was born in the Great Chen Dynasty, naturally is a citizen of the Chen Dynasty, so he can rely on his own skills to build a career."

The fat lady sniffed and looked down at the tiger cub eating, but did not answer.

The little tiger shook off its soaked fur, grubbing at the meat pieces to eat sweetly, but the teeth did not grow, biting and biting the empty place, only to change the direction and bite again.

Mu Hanzhang saw it eating happily so he didn't disturb it, he just spread out the cloth towel and let the sunlight onto the small body so that the fur could dry: "Sister-in-law is so diligent and thrifty, she is only a rich butcher after a lifetime of mixing. The fat lady sniffed, couldn't help but start to get angry again: "This is something I scold him every day, even if he goes to be a constable, his children won't have an expectation in the future. This is good, give birth to a son also as a butcher, give birth to a daughter no one dares to marry!"

"Woohoo~" laying on the roof, the little tiger excitedly scratched at the sheets underneath him, Jing Shao pinched the restless furry mass up, "If you make any more trouble, I'll throw you down!"
